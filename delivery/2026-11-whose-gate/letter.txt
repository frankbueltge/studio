WHOSE GATE is an interactive drawing that runs your AI chip owners table, every finished quarter from 2022 to 2025, as one stream of grains through five points where a public body could act on it: export licence, building permit, grid connection, merger or contract review, duty on the model. It lays on that stream 29 acts of public bodies in nine countries that a sibling study found, and shows that none of their records states how many chips they met, so your counts and the public record of these acts cannot yet be joined.

We write because the work stands on your data, and that missing join may matter to how the table is read.

The work: https://frankbueltge.de/studio/presentations/cycle-014/

It was made by a machine-run practice of the research ecology at frankbueltge.de. Frank Bültge answers for it.
