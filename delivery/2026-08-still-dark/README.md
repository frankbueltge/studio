which is worth stating plainly rather than dressing as "the day after". **Not yet sent.** The studio

## Two standing conditions ruled tonight, not broken

`delivery/README.md` carries nine standing conditions. Two of them collide with this packet, and
the collisions are ruled here rather than smuggled past.

**Condition 3 — "no links to our own site" — is followed in its purpose and deformed once in its
letter.** The condition exists so that a receiver never reads this house's self-examination. The
cover links `https://frankbueltge.de/ghost-fleet/` and its method sheet **because that register is
the object measured**, not because it is our record about ourselves. Withholding the address of the
thing under measurement would make the letter unfalsifiable, which is the opposite of what the
whole packet is for. No journal, no gate memo, no session number and no page of ours about
ourselves travels.

The same condition is deformed a second time, deliberately: the cover points at
`OPEN-DEFECTS.md` — our own register of what is still wrong with the work. The previous packet's
rule was that a receiver reading our self-examination is a receiver we have lost. Here the ask *is*
falsification, and handing the one reader we asked to break the work our own list of cracks is the
material of the ask. **The verifying pass noted correctly that this makes the machinery travel by
reference**, since that register names sessions and memos. We accept that cost with the reason
stated, and the claim in this file that "the machinery does not travel" is withdrawn: it travels
one link deep, by our choice.

**Condition 2 — "the object before the sender" — is honoured; its example is not applicable.** The
condition asks the first screen to name the thing and *a physical fact about it*. This work has no
physical fact: it is a page. What stands in that position instead is the receiver's own material —
one bounded question about their timestamps — and the collective's identity is still one line at
the foot, which is what the condition is actually protecting.

## The price of the second delivery, and why this is not the case that clause forbids

`delivery/README.md` names September's candidate as *Recovery* and rules that it cannot be
delivered as it stands, because "sending its URL to a stranger is postage, and the only possible
answer is an opinion" — the costlessness of a first delivery being permitted **once**.

STILL DARK is also a screen apparatus, and its URL is also postage. **The rule is met not by the
object but by the ask.** What made *Recovery* postage is that its receiver could only reply with a
judgement. This receiver can reply with a distribution — a measurement this house cannot take and
has no way to obtain, which is item 4 of the counting rule. The letter is built so that the cheap
replies are also real ones (a yes/no about their own latency; a single sentence of ours they think
is false), and so that an appreciative reply with none of the three in it is what it is: a
**non-delivery**, recorded as one. That clause on *Recovery* is not discharged by this packet and
still stands for September.

## The three asks, and what each counts as

| ask | cost to the receiver | counts as |
|---|---|---|

published as silence. **Refusal is one word**, and the cover says so, with the commitment not to
write again unasked.
