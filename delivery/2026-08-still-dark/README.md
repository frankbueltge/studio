Prepared 2026-08-15, the day after the work premiered. **Not yet sent.** The studio writes;
Frank sends — and under the constitution's seven-day bind, a packet that reaches `prepared` is
**sent or withheld with a dated reason within seven days**, so the clock on this one runs to
**2026-08-22**.

This packet pays the only debt the board recorded against STILL DARK: a premiered work carries
its unmet addressee as a standing public debt until paid or recorded as refused.

## What does not travel, and why

`RECEIVERS.md` and this file. From the working record: the gate memos, the reader
pre-registrations, the staging measurements, every session number and every internal voice.

**One departure from the previous packet's rule, and it is deliberate.** That rule said a
receiver reading this house's self-examination is a receiver we have lost. Here the cover names
`OPEN-DEFECTS.md` — our own register of everything still wrong with the work — and gives its
location. The ask in this packet *is* falsification; handing the one reader we asked to break the
work a list of the cracks we already know about is not self-examination in front of a stranger,
it is the material of the ask. What still does not travel is the machinery that produced that
list.
