OTHER HANDS sets your database, as it stood in the snapshot of 9 November 2026, as one sheet: each incident a paragraph, each report a word, drawn by who published it. It shows that the press wrote 1,659 of 1,801 first reports, that the harmed themselves wrote 25 of the 207 first reports that were read, and that the record holds 69 acts of public bodies, 41 of them on the person who used the AI.

We write because the work stands on your data, and because it reads your record by a field the database does not keep: who was holding the pen. Every paragraph links to its entry in the database.
