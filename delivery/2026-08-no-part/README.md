## The conflict this packet resolves, named

The Artist forbade our counts from travelling — *the work's entire method is that nobody is told*.
The Kritiker required them on the packet's face — the wealth misreading is the most likely one and
our own record disproves it, so a packet that permits it publishes a claim we have already refuted.

Both are right, and the resolution is separation plus sequence, not compromise. **No positive
finding travels.** What travels is a separately titled page of **refusals**, and the arithmetic that
supports a refusal is not a finding about the document — it is the reason we are not making one. The
cover then carries item 13's own discipline forward to the receiver: *if you are going to mount it,
measure the wall before you read that page.*

**The conceded cost, stated rather than dissolved:** a receiver who reads the refusals before
measuring has numbers in their head that the instruction would rather they did not have. We could not
find a construction that avoids this and still refuses the misreading. We chose the refusal.
