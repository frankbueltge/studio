# Receivers — *NO PART*, August 2026

**One receiver, named, per send. No list, no cc, no broadcast.** A second name is written to only
after the first has answered or the month has closed. Silence is published as silence.

The receiver is an **organisation and its programme**, not a private individual; Frank addresses it
through the institution's own public channel, and whoever it routes to is who answers. We do not
compile people.

## Why this class of receiver at all

Two reasons, and the second is the operative one.

The Artist's: these are the only readers for whom a 5000-series docket number is a caseload rather
than a convention, and a break chosen in their corridor is chosen by people who know exactly whose
name they are putting round a corner. That is item 15 at full strength.

The Kritiker's, which overrides it and which we adopt as the rule: **send to the receiver who can
falsify you, not the one who can use you.** These are the only receivers who can read our account of
Rule 39.8, the Rule 38(a) filing bar, the recusals and the thirty-one individuated entries and tell
us we have it wrong. The ask is therefore a correction, never an endorsement.

The Artist's own ranking put a **scholar of the certiorari process last**, on the ground that a
scholar receives the document as text — which is the one thing this work is not — and answers with
an accuracy assessment, converting a work into a claim. We follow that: no cert-process scholar is
on this list.

## First send

**The Roderick and Solange MacArthur Justice Center**, at Northwestern Pritzker School of Law's
Bluhm Legal Clinic.
<https://www.law.northwestern.edu/legalclinic/macarthur/> — the school's own page describes a civil
rights litigation clinic of over thirty years, working across the criminal legal system including
the criminalization of poverty, police misconduct, prison discrimination and racial injustice, with
law students in every stage of the litigation. Contact channel on that page: 375 East Chicago
Avenue, Chicago, IL 60611-3069, +1 312.503.1271.

**Chosen despite an obvious fit, not because of it.** Their declared focus includes the
criminalization of poverty, which is precisely the reading our own measurements contradict
(`WHAT-WE-DO-NOT-CLAIM.md` §1: 69.8 % before the sentence, 71.0 % after). Under the Kritiker's rule
that is an argument *for* them, not against: a receiver whose frame our work refuses is the receiver
who will tell us if we have the refusal wrong. The negation page is what makes this send permissible,
and it is not optional.

## Reserves, in order

**2. The Supreme Court Advocacy Clinic, Yale Law School.**
<https://www.law.yale.edu/studying-law-yale/clinical-and-experiential-learning/our-clinics/supreme-court-advocacy-clinic>
— its own page: pro bono representation before the Supreme Court, "an active docket of cases at both
the certiorari and merits stages", "particularly interested in assisting indigent or
under-represented clients". The cert stage is exactly this document's stage, which makes it the
sharpest instrument for ask 1; its published contact is a representation-inquiry address at a law
firm, which makes it a worse channel for a letter that is not about a case. Second for that reason
alone.

**3. The National Center for Access to Justice.** <https://ncaj.org/> — its own page: research and
advocacy on civil justice accessibility, maintaining a Justice Index that tracks self-representation
access among its metrics; contact `ncaj@fordham.edu`, 150 West 62nd Street, Suite 7-165, New York,
NY 10023. Third because their instrument is state-level policy data rather than the federal cert
docket, so they can falsify us on less of it.

*(A fourth slot is deliberately empty. The Fordham Law A2J initiative page was behind a login when we
went to verify it and is therefore not listed; nothing goes on this list that we have not read
first-hand.)*

## What is recorded when a send happens

Receiver · channel · date sent · date answered or closed · which of the four counting outcomes it
produced (mounting record / correction / documented refusal / wall measurement) — or **non-delivery**,
which is what an appreciative reply with no number in it is called here.
