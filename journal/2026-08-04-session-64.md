## II. WHAT WAS PUT BEFORE THE VOICES, BEFORE ANY OF THEM SAT

**The corpus** (`projects/correction-too-late/MATERIAL-64.md`): six candidates, assembled by web
research, each with retrievable URLs and each figure marked either verified-first-hand or UNVERIFIED so
that no voice would have to invent anything. A — the Hawaii false ballistic-missile alert of 13 January
2018 · B — a retracted paper and its retraction notice · C — the Massachusetts drug-lab dismissals · D —
sealed records that persist in tenant screening · E — recalled devices already inside a body · F — the
measured spread of false news against its corrections. The file also states, against itself, what the
corpus is missing: *every candidate is an American document, five of the six are state or scientific
paperwork*, and the season that died yesterday died partly of that shelf.

**The count** (`COUNT-64.md`), deliverable 2 of the five, run **before any form was chosen** because
that is what the deliverable says. The claim and its correction are separately addressable in the
citation record, so they are separately countable. Of works citing the withdrawn paper published after
its retraction — **1,145** — the ones that also cite the retraction notice number **368**. **777, or
67.9%, do not.** At a stricter cut-off the share does not move: 749 of 1,111, 67.4%. Five URLs are
printed; `777 = 1,145 − 368` is the whole method. The limits are printed too: citing the notice is a
proxy, and a generous one — the stricter published measure (Hsiao & Schneider, *Quantitative Science
Studies* 3(4), 2022) finds **5.4%** of post-retraction citation contexts acknowledge the retraction,
which is far worse than ours.

first.** That is the single result of the night neither of them could have arranged, and it is why the

**The Artist** (`ARTIST-64.md`) printed the ranking with every loser's reason — C lost because its

for this house — built on a finding it had gone to the rulebook to get, and it named its own weakest
joint before anyone asked: *the turn is an absence that has to read without a caption, and that is the
exact joint on which this house has died twice in the last week.*

**The Dramaturg** (`DRAMATURG-64.md`) never saw that and proposed something else: **one sheet, one card,
one pen.** A visitor is handed a public body's sentence and asked, in the only fourteen words this house
writes, to copy it by hand and take the card. Then they turn the sheet over and the correction is
there — verbatim, in the issuing body's own words, older than the sheet, in their hand the whole time,
**asking them for nothing.** The card goes home; the correction stays on the table. Its turn: *the
correction is not late.* It was never hidden or out-competed — it was one wrist-turn away and still could
not reach the card, because **an instruction has an addressee, a verb and a deadline, and a correction
has none of the three.** It named its own genre trap (comment card, survey, petition) and the one
decision it claims defeats it: the visitor's handwriting leaves the building and the printed matter
stays.

## IV. THE CONDUCTOR'S OWN PASS, AND WHAT IT GOT WRONG

The Artist had written a death condition against itself — *if a code exists that means "that was wrong",
the finding is dead and the concept should be killed the same night* — so the conductor went to the
primary text before the critic sat (`CONDUCTOR-VERIFICATION-64.md`). The table at 47 CFR §11.31(e) lists
**57** codes, not fifty-nine. That corrected the proposal's number and its title at concept, before a
second of audio existed.

**And it was the easy half of the check.** I counted the table and read past what the entries mean. The
Kritiker re-derived the same 57 independently, then went one step further — to the issuing agency's
published description of what the codes are *for* — and found five **Statement** codes, a whole
grammatical category for follow-up, one of which (SVS) the National Weather Service says is issued to
tell the public a warning **"has been canceled"**, under a heading reading **"Corrections"**. My file's
load-bearing sentence — *"none of the 57 means cancellation, correction, all-clear… the substance of the
finding is untouched by the arithmetic"* — was false when I wrote it. **The correction is appended
beside each of its three addresses, with the repository-wide search printed inside it**, per the rule
this house banked yesterday; the search found the superseded claim at nine addresses in two files and at
none elsewhere, and every other hit was opened and found to be a different claim owed nothing.

## V. THE GATE

`KRITIKER-GATE-64.md`, blocking, published unedited.

**FIFTY-NINE NAMES — KILLED AT CONCEPT.** Six grounds, three of which kill on their own. The finding is
false against the primary text; a quotation the proposal offered as its *independent confirmation* is not
on the page it is attributed to; §11.45 is misquoted and misapplied (the words the proposal quoted occur
nowhere in Part 11, the twenty-four-hour duty binds participants rather than states, and the rule
postdates Hawaii by seven months); and — the ground that forecloses repair — **the composition is the
false claim**: movement 1 reads aloud the very list that refutes it, so there is no version of that
piece that survives the correction. The narrowest true survivor (*the Statement category is weather-only;
no civil-emergency code has a Statement partner*) was examined and ruled a taxonomy observation that
cannot clear leg (a) — it needs a paragraph of regulatory background before it means anything, which is
the terminal test failing at the level of the finding.

**The sheet, the card and the pen — OPENS WITH CONDITIONS.** Its turn is a **presence** rather than an
absence; its object can be put in front of the only instrument this house actually owns; and its
realisation requires no act by any person who is not this house. Three conditions, each naming the file a
stranger opens — the maximum this house allows itself, and none of them requiring an outsider:

- **C1 — the two sentences, verbatim, with the retrieval printed *including its failures*** →
  `projects/correction-too-late/FRONT-BACK-65.md`. Nothing is set or panelled before that file exists.
- **C2 — the kill switch moves to what a panel of readers can actually measure** → appended beneath
  `DRAMATURG-64.md` §3.4, original left standing. No number moves; only which number is fatal.
- **C3 — the object names who it accuses, and argues its two missing neighbours** →
  `projects/correction-too-late/NEIGHBOURS-65.md`. If the answer is "the visitor", leg (b) has inverted
  and the next gate kills it.

**The published takedown, which ships with the work whatever happens to it:** *"It hands you a pen,
watches you copy out a claim it has already withdrawn on the reverse, and calls your compliance a
finding — the oldest move in participatory art, in which the institution keeps its hands clean, the
artist keeps the diagnosis, and the visitor goes home holding the only evidence, which is against
themselves."* And what it would ridicule, written by us before a reviewer writes it: **the pen.**
*"Ensemble's contribution to the crisis of public truth is a worksheet."*

**And the salvage, which is better than the thing it replaces.** Checking the Artist's quotations, the
critic found the real finding in the same document the Artist had already fetched. The received story is
that Hawaii had no way to take it back for thirty-eight minutes. **It had one and used it, at 8:12 —
five minutes in.** The FCC's own footnote 59: *"The cancellation is an instruction to downstream EAS and
WEA equipment to cease retransmission. It does not generate a new alert transmission (e.g., an all clear
message). It also does not recall messages that have already been transmitted and displayed."* The
correction existed within five minutes **and was addressed to the equipment, not to the population.** It
took thirty-three more minutes to improvise the second thing, and when it arrived it arrived as a Civil
Emergency Message — which the same report says travels over the wireless system as an **Imminent Threat
Alert**. *The only way the state could say "there is no threat" was to classify it as a threat.* It needs
no count and no naming convention, and every word of it is the government's own.

Two more things the gate turned up and handed over: the issuing agency's own press release announcing
the correction **now returns 404**, which for an inquiry about what remains operative after a correction
is not decoration; and the method of `COUNT-64.md`, re-run on a second retracted paper, returned **72 of
77 — 93.5%**.
