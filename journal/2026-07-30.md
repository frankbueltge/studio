## The Dramaturg, on the strong tier: the ending

It returned having opened two rendered sheets, and it changed its own mind in public twice.

**The ending, named as it actually is.** Under item 16 the sheet-1 end is *closed* — an inside corner,
a dead end — so the body comes back along the line. **The walk is out and back.** Three movements:
~1.6 m of the only individuated speech the document contains, then the one sentence that disposes of
761 petitions, then 6.3 m of rows walked knowing, then sheet 1 where the document finally names itself
and the wall stops — then the same 6.3 m again and out past the sanctions. *"The work ends on
repetition and on the way it came in. The last thing the body meets is the thing it met first."*

**The second error, which no reader found and the Dramaturg found in its own file.** `STAGING-NOTES.md`
§B had argued that forward entry means *"the mass is walked in ignorance and never re-walked with
knowledge"* — and item 16's own second clause contradicts it. There is no version of this work in which
the mass is walked once. That sentence is now struck by its author.

**Item 16 stands, and its case is cut from five reasons to one.** Kept: the mass sentence sits
**1.56 m from the sheet-39 end and 6.76 m from the sheet-1 end** (`line-profile.json`,
`meta.sentenceLocation`; verified by the conductor before the change was applied) — so a body that
gives up early has met the work's only event if it entered at one end and may never meet it if it
entered at the other. That is an argument about attrition in walking bodies, and this house has never
observed one. Struck, all four by their author: the residue (pre-registered, and gone tonight); the
never-re-walked claim; the first-encounter claim — **the document is individuated at both ends and
empty in the middle**, measured in the 150–190 mm band at 0.040–0.060 on sheets 1–3, 0.002–0.010 on
sheets 30–32, 0.051–0.086 on sheets 33–36, which is exactly why the forward cell scored no worse; and
the terminal-test claim, which neither cell ever tested and which is therefore *unsupported, not
refuted*, and not available as a reason.

**It refused to reverse the entry**, and the refusal is the more disciplined move: the only number
pointing that way is ASYMMETRY 3/3 forward against 2/3 reverse, and the pre-registration's own limits
section forbids effect-size claims from three readers a cell. *"Reversing a binding gate condition on a
one-reader difference would be a worse offence than keeping a rule with one honest reason."*

**The work changed in exactly one place.** Item 20 — the record a realiser sends back — now also asks
**which end of the line the way in reaches first, and whether the room allowed a choice.** That is the
only route by which item 16's remaining reason can ever be checked, and it asks about architecture, not
about visitors: the Dramaturg refused to put an observer of bodies inside a work whose boast is that it
cannot detect one. Everything else in the instruction: unchanged. *"The instruction survived the
refutation of its own ending without a word being falsified. The ending was never in the work. It was
in our description of the work."*

**And it found something in the source that four sessions of this campaign had walked past.** The
sentence six of six readers carry out **is cut in half by a page boundary**: sheet 33 ends *"…As the
petitioner has repeatedly"*, the folio 33 sits beneath it, and sheet 34 opens *"abused this Court's
process…"*. The conductor re-confirmed it in the file's own content streams before it was written into
the notes. A break between those two sheets is legal under item 12, and item 14 forbids adjusting the
joint to avoid it — **so the realiser's one free decision may put the work's measured residue round a
corner, the accusation on one run and the sentence on the next.** Nobody chooses that; nobody may
choose against it.
