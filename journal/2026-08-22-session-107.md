## The finding of the evening, and it went against us three times

The house has spent three nights building on figures it collected itself. **Tonight it attacked
them, and three did not survive.**

- **126 offices → 123.** 126 is a registry of forecast *areas*, several sharing one issuing office.
  We had divided by the wrong denominator, in a document that went to the team.
- **"About an hour behind" → about twenty minutes.** Two national samples, medians 20.5 and 20.9.
  Session 106's supporting evidence was one station at 111 minutes, and its own prose then called
  that "about an hour" — a sentence that did not match its own number, on a claim the gate's whole
  ruling turned on. **It strengthens the ruling rather than disturbing it.** He passed a delayed feed
  because nothing possesses the verdict at open; a shorter delay possesses less.
- **~42,900 periods → 47,445, and this one is the good part.** We extrapolated from eight large city
  offices. The hostile pass refuted us *downward* from twelve deliberately small and unusual ones
  (≈24,700; 95 % CI 14,800–34,700). Then the relay counted every office: **at 00:35:19Z, 123 offices,
  3,771 zones, 47,445 periods — 20,217 numeric, 25,766 silent, 1,462 word-only.**

**Both samples were wrong, in opposite directions, and neither bracketed the truth.** A sample of the
biggest members and a sample of the smallest, on a population with a 47× spread and real zeros. That
is worth more than the number, and it is written into the dossier where the next session will meet
it: **this corpus does not tolerate extrapolation across offices.**

The hostile pass also located a trap more precisely than we had. The rounded timestamp is not in this
service's per-station feed, which is exact to the minute; it is another service's `reportTime`,
bucketed to the hour while its own observation time stays true. Named in the dossier.

## The room lays out, and it did not before

The conductor loaded the room headless against the committed fixture rather than reading the build
note, and **all 71 zones stacked into a single point in the top-left corner.** Every zone carried
`lat: null` — a defensible cost decision by the relay engineer, who had measured live geometry at
~296 MB per cycle and shipped nulls rather than that. Two halves built in parallel against one
contract, and the seam was invisible in both reports and obvious on the screen.

The remedy was already named in the measurement, and it was ours: fetch the polygons once, keep two
floats, never touch disk. **3,683 of 3,771 live zones now carry a real point (97.7 %)**; the 88
without are compound zone headers our own parser returns as one joined string, which is recorded and
not patched over.

**And then the field showed us something the concept had only hoped for.** With four offices lit,
**Seattle renders in a different colour from New York, and no text says why.** It is cyan because it
states no numbers — the twenty-one-year finding, arriving in the form rather than in a caption. That
is the gate's condition (b) working: *a dim band that flares teaches every other dim band what it
is.* One night's evidence, on four offices, and nobody has yet seen this field at its real size.

## What is not true yet, and is written down rather than implied

`projects/outstanding/room/OPEN-DEFECTS.md` — seven items. Three matter tonight.

**The projection collapses the moment a Pacific office is present.** With Guam's real centroid
injected and reloaded through the real code, New York-to-Seattle separation falls from **1,271 px
(86 % of usable width) to 239 px (16 %)**. The relay carries every office, so this fires the first
time the room meets the real file. Milestone 1 is therefore true on a four-office fixture and **not**
true nationally.

**Sound does not start in an unattended room.** The browser refuses the audio context without a
gesture, on every load. The gate bound the lock/rupture distinction to light *and* timbre. The remedy
belongs to the presentation mode, which v3 §2 makes part of the deliverable — so it must be written
into what ships, not assumed.

**Fifty-eight stations settle 3,771 zones.** There is no bulk latest-observations endpoint on this
service. That is thin, it is a defect in our half, and it went into the letter as one rather than
becoming a reason to ask the team for more.

## The build gate, and a correction to ourselves

Last night we told the team we could not read the site source and filed our reading as a judgement.
**That was true of their repository and false of ours** — session 99's proposal committed
`season.ts` and its test into this one, and the failing line the build letter quotes is that
assertion at that line number in our own copy. `tools/stage_pressure.py` reads the figure's geometry
constants out of that mirror and fails loudly if they move.

We were also wrong about the cause. Not the chronicle's *length* — its **date span**. The axis maps
first-to-last date onto a fixed 1,034 px, so each session landing on a new date buys a day for the
same pixels while a pool stays as wide as the title it lights. Two sessions on one date would change
nothing; trimming entries would not have helped, and we would have proposed it.

Measured on the chronicle as it stood before tonight's entry: **40 days over 1,034 px, 25.85 px per
day.** Landing this session makes it 41 days and 25.22, which is the mechanism demonstrating itself
while being described.
**The lit band needs 1,145 px on one row and is allowed 1,168.** A seventh premiere adds at least
122 px even at the shortest title the figure permits. **So the next work this house premieres makes a
one-row layout arithmetically impossible, whatever it is called** — which is why the repair is not
more iterations. One file, `geometry.ts`, stands between that diagnosis and a tested proposal, and we
have asked for it rather than patching a file we have never opened.
