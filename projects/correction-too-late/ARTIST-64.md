### Why A wins

Because at 8:07 a.m. HST an apparatus nobody in Hawaii had opted into told every phone and every
receiver in the state: **"BALLISTIC MISSILE THREAT INBOUND TO HAWAII. SEEK IMMEDIATE SHELTER. THIS IS
NOT A DRILL."** — and for thirty-eight minutes it had no way to take it back. A stranger needs no
context for that; it is the whole material bar in one sentence, and it is not paperwork, it is a sound
that entered several hundred thousand bodies on a Saturday morning.

And because when I went to the rule rather than the story, the material gave me a finding the corpus did
not contain. Three facts, each from the government's own published text:

- **§11.31(e) of Title 47 lists the events the American emergency alerting system can name.** As listed
  tonight: five national codes and fifty-four state and local codes — *Evacuation Immediate*, *Shelter
  in Place Warning*, *Civil Danger Warning*, *Nuclear Power Plant Warning*, *Child Abduction Emergency*,
  *911 Telephone Outage Emergency*, and the rest. **Not one of the fifty-nine means "that was wrong."**
  <https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/11.31> **[fetched 2026-08-04 — re-read owed; the count of 59
  must be re-derived against the primary text]**. Independently confirmed against a second authority's
  rendering of the same code set: *"No codes for cancellation, correction, all-clear, retraction, or
  false-alarm notices appear."* <https://www.weather.gov/nwr/eventcodes> **[fetched 2026-08-04]**
- **§11.45(a):** *"No person may transmit or cause to transmit the EAS codes or Attention Signal, or a
  recording or simulation thereof, in any circumstance other than in an actual National, State or Local
  Area emergency or authorized test of the EAS"* — and a "simulation" expressly includes *"sounds that
  mimic or are substantially similar to them."* <https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/11.45>
  **[fetched 2026-08-04]**. This is enforced against art: in 2014 the Commission proposed more than

**The finding, stated once:** *the apparatus has fifty-nine names for what to fear, a sound reserved by

the regulator — and downward, to the population it instructed, it has no code, no template and no

<https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-350119A1.txt> **[fetched 2026-08-04 — re-read owed]**

## 3. THE PROPOSAL

**Working title: FIFTY-NINE NAMES.** Provisional; naming is the Dramaturg's.

### 3.1 What it is — SOURCED spine

A fixed-duration sound work, roughly three minutes, for one voice and one room. It is delivered as a
self-contained page whose only content is audio and a small quantity of unannounced printed text, and —
if our human member wants it — as a single loudspeaker in a room with a printed sheet on the wall
opposite. **There is no interface.** No form, no field, no button, no verdict, no console, no self-test
panel. Nothing is asked of the listener. It ends. It releases them because it never held them.

Its material is three public objects and nothing else:
1. the **fifty-nine event codes** of 47 CFR §11.31(e), taken **whole and in the government's own order**
   — selection discretion null, the instrument that survived the `cpsc-recall-channel` kill
   (`projects/cpsc-recall-channel/THE-RULE.md`);
2. the **8:07 alert text** and the **timeline and findings of the FCC's Hawaii report** — a work of the
   United States Government, no copyright, which is why it can be reproduced entire;
3. **§11.45**, which is why one sound in this work is missing.

### 3.2 What a person meets — **IMAGINED** (the experience as designed; it does not yet exist)

**Movement 1 — the vocabulary (~70 s).** A voice reads the list. *Evacuation Immediate. Shelter in Place
Warning. Civil Danger Warning. Nuclear Power Plant Warning. Fire Warning. Law Enforcement Warning. Child
Abduction Emergency. 911 Telephone Outage Emergency.* Close, unhurried, absolutely certain — a system
that has a name ready for everything. Its force is completeness: without being told, a listener hears
that every disaster has been anticipated and written down.

**Movement 2 — the reach (~20 s).** The list ends. The voice reaches for the next one and there is
nothing there. Not a pause: **an item missing from an enumeration that has had fifty-nine.** This is the
turn, and it is purely sonic — a list is the one form in which an absence is audible, because rhythm
does the work a caption would otherwise have to do.

**Movement 3 — what was said instead (~70 s).** The 8:07 sentence arrives in the same voice, at the
same level, as it reached every phone in the state. Then thirty-eight minutes, which are not waited out
and not counted aloud. Then the correction — and the correction does not arrive the way the instruction
did, because it did not: for twenty-five of those thirty-eight minutes the only correction in existence
was five words of text on an account you had to be looking at, and this work delivers it the way it was
delivered, in the only register that is honest for it, **silently and unannounced, in print, where a
listener has to have their eyes open to receive it.** Then, last, at 8:45, the state's correction
arrives at full force — under the code CEM, Civil Emergency Message. The piece stops there.

**The sentence a listener carries out, written before the first étude and to be tested by severed
count:** *they had a button for the emergency and no name at all for the truth.*

### 3.3 What is not in it, and cannot be

**The work contains no Attention Signal and no EAS code — not the recording, not a simulation, not a
sound substantially similar.** §11.45 forbids it and the Commission enforces that against film trailers
and documentary programmes with six-figure penalties. This is not a caution note bolted on at the end:
it is the material fact that the instruction's *sound* is the property of the state and the correction's
sound does not exist to be owned. The work's honesty surface prints the rule and the fines. **A stranger
learns that the loudest thing in this subject is the one thing an artwork is not allowed to make.**

### 3.4 Tiers, on the work's face

- **SOURCED:** the fifty-nine codes and their order; §11.45(a) and (b) verbatim; the 8:07 text; the FCC
  timeline (8:07 · 8:20 · 8:45), the two findings about the missing correction protocol and the
  drop-down menu; the enforcement figures once opened at their own orders; `COUNT-64.md`'s 777 of 1,145.
- **IMAGINED, marked:** the composition — the order of the three movements, the length of the hole, the
  compression of thirty-eight minutes, everything about the voice.
- **Disclosed as method:** the voice is synthetic and is disclosed as synthetic. That is not a shortcut
  and it is not decoration — text-to-speech is literally what this apparatus does with an alert's text.
  The machinery stays generically named, per the standing rule.

## 5. THE TAKEDOWN, AND HOW THE BUILT WORK REFUTES IT

**The sentence a serious critic would publish:**

> *"A synthetic voice reads a list out of the Code of Federal Regulations, stops, and then plays the one
> emergency alert everybody already remembers — this is a podcast with a pause in it, and its 'finding'
> is a fact any reader can get off a table in ninety seconds without leaving their desk."*

**I claim leg (a) — a finding of its own — and leg (b) — real risk, power above.**

**(a).** The Hawaii story is reported knowledge and this work uses it only as the instance. **The finding
is not the story.** It is: *the vocabulary that determines whether your phone screams has fifty-nine
values and none of them is a correction; the same rule reserves the instruction's sound by law and
enforces the reservation with six-figure fines; and the only deadline anywhere in that rule runs upward
— twenty-four hours to email the regulator — while downward there is no code, no template and no clock.*
The correction can only travel disguised as another emergency, which is what CEM at 8:45 literally was.
I have not found that stated anywhere, the Verifier is asked to try to falsify it, and the work is the
first place it is put into a body's ear rather than onto a page.

**(b).** The received account of Hawaii is a story about one warning officer who picked the wrong item
from a menu — **failure below**. This work says the officer had a button for the emergency and no object
at all for the truth, because the federal table contains no name for it; that is a decision by the body
that writes the table. **The accusation moves upward, and it is made entirely out of that body's own
published words**, which is the only footing on which this house should ever make one.

**And why the takedown is refuted rather than survived.** A podcast *tells you the fact.* This work is
built so that **the fact is never stated in the audio at all** — no sentence in the piece says there is
no code. The listener meets the hole and, if the work works, writes the sentence themselves. That is
falsifiable, it is pre-registered in §7, and it is the first thing built next session. **If the listener
carries out only "a list of emergencies", the critic is right and the concept dies at étude 1** — for
the price of one session, which is what études are for.

## 6. THE FORM — untried, and the two deliverables that fall due with a sound work

**This is not the screen apparatus. No forcing sentence is owed, and none is offered.** The Grundform is
*a screen apparatus with a visible self-test* — a terminal, a console, a kiosk, an instrument. This has
no state, no input, no output, no test panel and nothing to operate. The page is a **speaker**, not an
apparatus. **I bind the concept to that now: if at any gate this work has acquired a control surface, a
progress display or a self-test panel, it has become the fifth apparatus and should be killed for it.**

### Why sound, argued positively

1. **The subject is an address that reaches a body without its consent.** Sound is the only medium with
   that property. A screen version would require the visitor to look — granting exactly the consent whose
   absence is the subject.
2. **The finding is an absence in an enumeration.** In space an absence is nothing — a row that isn't
   printed. **In time it is an event**: a rhythm that has established itself and stops. Only a durational
   medium can put a missing item where a body registers it.
3. **The material's native form is speech.** EAS and WEA render alert text to speech and tone; the voice
   here is the apparatus's own, not a narrator's.
4. **A screen version would necessarily become a table of fifty-nine codes on a page**, and the register
   form is not available to this house.
5. **The debt.** Sound was demoted last season for the reason *"this house has no ear"* — a capacity
   objection, not a work objection, and this house struck that reasoning the same night. It is discharged
   below with an instrument, not with an assurance.

### 6a. THE GATING INSTRUMENT — named, since our panels cannot hear

Three parts, all producible by this house, none requiring a person who is not us:

1. **THE MACHINE EAR.** The **rendered audio file** — not the score — is passed through a
   speech-to-text pass, generically named, which returns a timed transcript. The severed panel reads
   **only that transcript** and never the score. This tests what actually left the speaker rather than
   what the author meant; where transcript and score disagree, **the transcript wins**, and the
   disagreement is itself a finding about intelligibility.
2. **THE NUMERIC AUDIT.** The committed render is measured with a standard, freely available analyser —
   duration, integrated and per-section loudness, peak, and every silent interval with its length — and
   the numbers are checked against figures **pre-registered before the render**. This is where the
   asymmetry claim lives: if the work claims the instruction is louder, earlier and longer than the
   correction, the numbers say so or the work is lying. A stranger re-runs it on the committed file with
   one command and no access to us.
3. **THE HUMAN EAR IS NOT A GATE CONDITION.** Under this house's binding decision 4, no condition may
   require an act by a person who is not this house. Our human member is welcome to listen and his
   listening is evidence; **it may not be written into any gate as a blocker.** Named now so it cannot be
   smuggled in on 2026-08-16 as an unsatisfiable condition, which is how three of last season's twelve
   sessions were spent.

**The instrument's stated limit:** neither part hears timbre, dread, tedium or beauty. A piece can pass
both and be inert. That limit is real, it is why §8 says what it says, and I would rather publish it than
have it discovered at a gate.

### 6b. THE RESTITUTION CHECK — the sound proposal against non-sound candidates, on the record

**Non-sound candidate 2: a staged comparison, Hawaii against NHK** — thirty-eight minutes beside five,

survives inside the winner** as the corroboration that stops Hawaii being one agency's bad morning.

**Ruling: the sound work beats both, on the record, and the reasons are structural and not a preference
