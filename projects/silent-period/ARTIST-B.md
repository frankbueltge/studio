# The proposal the conductor did not forward

*Kept in full so the gate can overrule the choice. A second Artist, briefed on the same material and
the same stake but required to make the visitor's action part of the claim.*

**The stake, in the Artist's words.** A stranger walks up to a dark field made entirely of words —
the actual phrases a forecast uses: "isolated", "slight chance", "chance", "likely", and, more than
anything else, blank space where nothing is said at all. Touch any word, or any patch of blank, and
it opens into a mark showing how often the sky actually delivered rain after that word — or that
silence — was used. *"Within a few touches the pattern is felt, not read: the field never lets you
touch 'none', rarely lets you touch 'certain', and the blank patches — the majority of the surface —
still bloom with rain marks about one time in sixteen."*

**Claim.** *"The public vocabulary for the sky has no word for zero, almost none for certainty, and
lets its silences carry risk it never states."*

**Machine advantage:** verification — every word or blank a visitor touches checked on the spot
against all 334,912 settled claims, *"the same depth for the ten-thousandth touch as the first."*

**Form.** Words and blanks sized and clustered at their true frequency in the archive. A touch opens
a scored line drawn from the record; the mark stays, permanently redrawing that patch, so the canvas
is cumulative across visitors. Untouched for an hour, the work does not idle: it continues the same
act alone, moving through the archive in order and laying its own marks — *"proving its record to an
empty room, tally by tally, patient in a way no person keeping watch could be."*

**Its own strongest objection.** *"After the first handful of touches, the discovery is made… and
further touching risks becoming confirmation, not revelation, sliding toward the kiosk this brief
forbids."* And: showing a word beside a rate it seems to betray *"invites a stranger to read policy
language as dishonesty, when it is closer to a system straining against genuine uncertainty."*

**Why the conductor did not forward it.** Two reasons, both open to being overruled. A field of words
sized by frequency is a **word cloud** — the most exhausted form in this territory, and neither
search was asked about it because the form arrived after them. And in an unattended room the work's
whole argument sits behind a touch that most visitors never make; its idle state is good, but the
idle state is then the work, and the interaction is furniture.
