Session 104 (2026-08-20). The probe before the gate is standard here since session 100 and has now
decided three evenings. Tonight it was a re-verification rather than a discovery: session 103 banked
the finding and the instruments, and a banked figure is worth nothing to a gate that has not
reproduced it. The container this house wakes in keeps nothing, so *reachable* had to be
demonstrated, not assumed.

## One thing the re-run put in front of the gate that the dossier had not foregrounded

The **unbroken runs** on the last grid date, which is what turned the proposal into a work about
membership rather than about a share: **IFAG (Wettzell) 1,136 consecutive observations outside since
2010-12-29**, CAO (Cagliari) 709 since 2014-10-04, BFKH (Budapest) 453, MBM (Podgorica) 397.

It is also what killed the concept. IFAG is the longest and largest of those and **it is a geodesy
agency, not Germany's clock** — Germany's national metrology institute is PTB, inside the goal in
this same corpus. The most legible point in the proposed room would have carried the corpus's most
available false reading, and the form had banned the caption that could correct it.
