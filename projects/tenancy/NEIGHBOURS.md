## The genre is crowded, and that is the danger

Naming litany against a published standard: Cennetoğlu's *The List*; Haacke's *Shapolsky et al.*
(1971); Belmore's *Vigil* (2002); DinéYazhi''s *A Nation Is a Massacre* (Pioneer Works 2019);
Ọnụọha's *The Library of Missing Datasets*. Formally closest: **Hansen & Rubin, *Listening Post***
(2001–2006) — unattended, durational, text from a live dataset, staged in movements, in a room. Also
On Kawara's *One Million Years*.

**And outside art the move is ordinary.** Climate Action Tracker rates ~40 countries against 1.5 °C
with a historical explorer; the FATF "grey list" publishes named jurisdictions three times a year
with an archive; the EU Excessive Deficit Procedure names member states over 3 % of GDP. *Who is
outside this line, named, over time* is a standard accountability device, not an invention.

**One claim from the form search is not carried.** It reported the keeper's own bulletin as "already
a public machine-readable *who is outside* record". It is not: *Circular T* publishes values, and the
sorting against the goal is the act nothing found performs. The claim is struck here rather than
repeated.

## The stakes, checked — and two analogies refused

**ITU-T G.8272** defines a Primary Reference Time Clock, PRTC-A, as **within 100 ns of UTC** — the
number lands on a real one. **SOURCED, not VERIFIED:** read in a secondary summary, because the ITU
text itself is login-gated. GNSS ranging: 1 ns of clock error ≈ 30 cm (elementary from *c*·Δt; the
pages carrying it returned 403). VLBI correlates at tens of nanoseconds.

**Two analogies are false and are forbidden to this house's texts:** MiFID II financial timestamping
allows **100 microseconds** of divergence (Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/574, Annex Table
1, read first-hand at https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2017/574) and grid synchrophasor timing
~26–32 **microseconds**. Both are three orders of magnitude looser than the goal in this corpus. A
hostile reader will check them, and would be right.

## The counter-argument, at its strongest

Participation is voluntary; the goal is a 1993 aspiration "when feasible"; a laboratory at 300 ns
harms no identifiable person. The present tail is largely institutions whose mission is not
nanosecond timekeeping at all — a geodetic observatory, an astronomical observatory, a naval
observatory, a meteorological observatory, a crescent-observation centre — beside small metrology
offices that have kept publishing their own numbers for thirty years rather than opting out. On that
reading the tail is the system working, and transparency is the thing being punished. The search
could establish the mission of six of the twenty-three and **not** of the other seventeen, so it
could not settle whether naming the tail is accountability or punching down. **It did not have to:
the gate killed the concept on the same bind from the other side.**
