VERDICT: DEAD

**What decided it.** Cover the captions. Remove the calendar ring, the corner readout, the twelve-word source line, and what is left on the black field is a ring of dots flashing and clicking and slowly tightening. That is Brian House's *Synchronizing Uncertainty*, one year old, built physically and therefore better than a screen can build it. The proposal's only claimed daylight is its source — real institutional error rather than simulated oscillators — and the proposal's own author writes the sentence that kills it: *a motion designer could fake it in a week with invented laboratories, and nobody standing there ninety seconds could tell.* That is a confession that the daylight lives entirely in the caption we are required to cover. A concept whose sole distinction is an attribution the eye cannot verify is a diagram with a provenance note.

**The four rulings.**

*1. The probe contradicts the proposal — DEAD as brought, not a corrected sentence.* I will not split it. The arc is not decoration on the claim; the arc **is** the claim. "Eighty-seven laboratories whose one job is to be UTC exactly are shown missing it… the promised unison is visibly and audibly a smear" does not survive a record in which the median offset falls 404 ns → 6.0 ns and the share within ±10 ns rises 5 % → 56 %. Invert it and every load-bearing part changes: the indictment becomes a vindication, the sound runs from polyrhythm *to* unison so the piece ends with the institutions succeeding, the smear becomes a knot, and the visitor's dented belief ("the time is one thing") is instead confirmed. That is a different work with the same data. Rewriting one sentence would leave a work arguing the opposite of what it was conceived to argue, which is not editing, it is substitution.

*2. Rarity — fails, but not the way it looks.* The volume here is genuine: ~144,000 placements, and at ~20 dates/second the whole thirty years runs in under two minutes, so the temporal compression is real and the tightening would in fact be seen. That is the honest strength of the proposal and I credit it. But the probe shows the volume carries almost no differentiation — in 2026, fifty of eighty-seven seats sit in the innermost two rings of a log dial — so the continuous truth on offer is a slow, monotone contraction, and the only event with visual force occurs in 12 of 11,417 transitions (0.11 %). The work therefore has volume without change and change without volume. *Rarity is not a form* applies to the second half; the first half is worse, because a perceptible advantage that never varies is a texture, not a claim.

*3. The neighbour — excuse, not daylight.* Real published error is a real difference in what the work is *about*. It is not a difference in what the work *is*, and the neighbour search is right: the form is taken, twice, including the delay-proportional-to-error click mechanic in *Metric Displacement* (2021). A source becomes daylight only when it is perceivable; here it is legible only as a citation.

*4. The BIPM plot — the same death.* "The data view exists; the ensemble view does not" is a true statement about artefacts and a false one about findings. The ensemble adds simultaneity, which is presentation; it adds no knowledge the issuing institution has not already published about its own ensemble. This is the same failure that killed the last concept, wearing a better costume.

**Law 5 — the material bar, pressed.** It fails. At a median of 6 ns there is no consequence a stranger can attach to anything; nothing in a human life, a grid, a market, or a border is decided at that magnitude. "The time is one thing" is a mild curiosity, not a stake, and the proposal concedes as much with "No lesson." As brought, this is metrology's housekeeping wearing a dial. The stake that genuinely exists in this material — the nine laboratories still outside a microsecond, and which states they are — is not in the work; the dial shows anonymous seats.

**The attribution rule — passes, and that is not a compliment.** The subtraction is not the off-stage discriminating act. UTC−UTC(k) is arithmetic against a published algorithmic reference; no person ranked, selected, or judged before the machine touched it, and the work credits the machine with nothing but drawing. It clears the rule cleanly. But it clears it *because* it contains no discriminating act at all — which is the same reason it has no finding of its own under law 6.

**Law 6 — the takedown.** "*UNISON replays a metrology bureau's own monthly bulletin as a polar scatter with a click track, converts thirty years of documented convergence into the appearance of failure, and where it is beautiful it is Brian House's beauty with a citation attached.*" The built work would merely survive this — and only by conceding the middle clause. It offers no (a): the one real finding in the file was made by the conductor's probe, not by the work. No (b): it implicates no power; the institution audits itself in public and comes out ahead. A weak (c) at best, and a weak (c) is not a substitute.

**What survives — and it is more than nothing.**

- **The material. Bank it.** 364 issues, zero unparsed lines across 32 sampled, both layouts read, CC BY 4.0, ~0.9 MB packed, no runtime network. The neighbour search establishes that **no artist has ever used Circular T** and that GitHub holds zero parsers. Commit `probe.py`, `probe-bands.py` and the parser to the house library as reusable material, not as a dead project's leftovers.
- **The finding, which is the real asset.** The core collapses by a factor of ~67 while the 90th percentile never leaves the 600–7,500 ns band and the 2026 maximum barely improves on 1998. Convergence is not shared. That is a finding, it is unclaimed, and it is not what the institution headlines.
- **The named shape that would not fail this way.** Not a dial: *a work whose subject is the tail and whose content is its membership.* Who is still outside, continuously, named, for thirty years — a persistence of identity, not a distribution of dots. Volume becomes evidence, the naming becomes the act, the threshold stays BIPM's own so the record does the discriminating and the attribution rule stays clear. That work implicates budgets, sanctions and infrastructure — power above it — and its captions are the content rather than the alibi. It is a different concept and must be brought as one.
- **Two method notes.** The probe ran before the gate and decided it; make that ordering standard. And run the neighbour search *before* the form is chosen — committing to flash-plus-click before checking House cost this evening.

This is the third death. Write the report.
