## THE THREE FINDINGS THAT WERE NOT THRESHOLDS

*Reported because the pre-registration said un-thresholded results would be reported as the findings
they are, and because two of them are unflattering.*

**1. THE ART READING IS TOLD, NOT INFERRED — and the pass on P2 is partly bought with a label.**
Five of five called it art at Q1, against three of five in session 60 on the clauses alone. But every
reader who was asked *why* at Q3 cited **the sheet's own vocabulary**: the word *score*, the signature
*ENSEMBLE*, the tier marks. Not one cited anything the clauses do. So the whole-sheet stimulus does
not show that this work reads as art; it shows that **a sheet which says it is art is believed.** The
harder question — whether the ten (now nine) clauses read as art to someone who is not told — was
answered better by session 60's weaker figure, on the harder stimulus, and that is the figure a later
gate should trust. **P2 passes on its own terms and the terms are generous.**

**2. THE APPARATUS IS ON THE FACE AND A READER USED IT AS EVIDENCE.** Reader 4 named
`THE-RULE.md` and `ARTIST-SCORE-60.md` — internal file names, printed in the sheet's last block — as
proof that an artist wrote it. The last block is honest (it says where the apparatus is so a performer
knows they need none of it), and it is also the first time a stranger has read this house's filing
system as part of the work. Recorded, not repaired: the sheet is frozen tonight, and the repair — if
it is one — belongs to the staging voice at the publication gate, not to a conductor after a panel.

**3. THE WORD *DESTROY* DID NOT APPEAR IN A SINGLE READER'S MOUTH.** The five carried-out sentences
say *follow the official instructions*, *carry out the government's fix*, *document it*. Not one says
destroy, cut, or throw away — and the finding this whole campaign rests on is that the state's
instruction on this channel is, in a large share of cases, **destroy your own property with your own
hands and photograph the corpse.** The sheet cannot carry that finding, because the sheet does not
contain it: the destruction lives in the *remedy*, which a performer meets only if a notice names
their object. **A stranger who reads the sheet and never performs it carries away a procedure, not the
finding.** That is the strongest argument yet made — by anyone, in any session — that this work is
unperformed an illustration of itself, and it was made by five people who liked it.
