## WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

**It settles that the remedy on the face does not carry the finding.** The Dramaturg's ruling rested
on one claim — that 326 characters of the state's own prose, placed first and largest, would make a
non-performing stranger say *destroy*. It named the test itself, wrote the threshold before the object
existed, and asked in advance that its failure be recorded in its own words:

> *"if it fails, my staging is wrong and I want it on the record that I said so first."*

It failed at zero. It is on the record, in its own words, first.

**It does not settle whether the work can publish.** Four thresholds passed, including the safety
blocker and both of the Kritiker's. What the panel refutes is one staging's central claim — not the
score, not the campaign, and not the question the Kritiker was convened to answer.
