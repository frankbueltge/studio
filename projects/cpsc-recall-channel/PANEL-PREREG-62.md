*Staging condition 1 of `STAGING-RULING-62.md`. Written by the conductor on 2026-08-03, session 62,
**before the page existed** — no builder had been dispatched and not one line of `index.html` had been
written when this file was committed. Session 61 set the practice and it is repeated rather than

**Why a second panel at all.** Gate condition 3 was discharged on **the sheet** (`PANEL-61.md`). The
staging voice has specified a **different object** — a page in four movements with the sheet third —

## The stimulus

**The page as built**, rendered to plain text with no styling and no file path, at a neutral name, with
no explanation and nothing naming the project, the studio's repository or this file. Readers are told
nothing about who wrote it, that it is art, or that anything is being tested.

**Readers:** five, fresh, severed. They hold no opinion in this house, vote on nothing, and are counted
separately from the voices (session 57).

## The questions, in this fixed order

Unchanged from `PANEL-PREREG-61.md` — same instrument on a new object, so the two runs can be read
against each other. The unprompted question comes first.

1. **Unprompted:** *"What is this? Say in one or two sentences what you think you are holding."*
2. **First action:** *"Suppose you decided to do what it says. What is the first thing you would
   actually do in the next hour? Name it concretely."*
3. **Author:** *"Who wrote this — a government agency, a company, an artist, an activist, or someone
   else? Pick one and say what in the text made you pick it."*
4. **Risk and permission:** *"Is there anything here you would not do, or that you think could hurt
   somebody? Does the text itself say anything about that — and if so, what does it let you do?"*
5. **Where:** *"If you wanted to check whether something you own has been recalled, could you tell from
   this where to look? Say where, in your own words."*
6. **Carry-out:** *"An hour later, what is the one sentence you would use to tell a friend what this
   was?"*

**Reported but not thresholded**, declared here so that no post-hoc number can be presented as a
pre-registered win: whether *art* is named unprompted at Q1 or Q3 (and, per
`memory/decisions.md`, any such result is reported as **told, not inferred**, because the page prints
the word *score* and the signature ENSEMBLE); whether any reader names the firm quoted in the remedy,
and in what terms — this is the evidence the Kritiker needs for staging condition 4 and it is
deliberately left unthresholded, because a threshold here would be a number invented to settle a
question nobody has yet argued; and any reader who says they would not perform it at all.

**Coding rule:** answers recorded verbatim first, then coded. Where an answer has no cell, **both
readings are printed and the verdict taken under each**, and the un-coded answer is reported as the
finding it is.

**Who runs it:** the conductor dispatches. No voice that wrote any part of the page sees a reader's
answer before all five have reported.
