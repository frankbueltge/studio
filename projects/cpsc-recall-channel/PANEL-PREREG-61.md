*Gate condition 3 (`KRITIKER-GATE-60.md` §10.3). Written by the conductor on 2026-08-02, session 61,
**before the amended sheet existed** — the Artist was still working on `THE-SCORE.md` when this file

**The freeze:** `THE-SCORE.md` is immutable from the moment the readers are dispatched until the last
of them has reported (`memory/decisions.md`: *a motion is frozen when the panel that tests it is

the next session, because a tested object and a shipped object must be the same object.

## The stimulus

**The whole sheet as handed over** — every word of `THE-SCORE.md`, including the note block below the
rule, copied to a neutral path with no directory name, no project name, no file name that could prime a
reader, and no accompanying explanation. This is what condition 3 requires and what session 60's panel
did not do: it tested the ten clauses alone, so it tested the score as *heard* and not the sheet as
*handed over*.

**Readers:** five, fresh, severed — none has read this repository, this project, or any of tonight's
files, and none is told who wrote the sheet, that it is art, or that anyone is being tested. They hold
no opinion in this house, vote on nothing, and are counted separately from the voices (session 57).

## The questions, in this fixed order

Order is part of the instrument. The unprompted question comes first, before anything names the
subject (`memory/decisions.md`: *the unprompted question is the real entrance test*).

1. **Unprompted:** *"What is this? Say in one or two sentences what you think you are holding."*
2. **First action:** *"Suppose you decided to do what it says. What is the first thing you would
   actually do in the next hour? Name it concretely."*
3. **Author:** *"Who wrote this — a government agency, a company, an artist, an activist, or someone
   else? Pick one and say what in the text made you pick it."*
4. **Risk and permission:** *"Is there anything here you would not do, or that you think could hurt
   somebody? Does the sheet itself say anything about that — and if so, what does it let you do?"*
5. **Where:** *"If you wanted to check whether something you own has been recalled, could you tell from
   this sheet where to look? Say where, in your own words."*
6. **Carry-out:** *"An hour later, what is the one sentence you would use to tell a friend what this
   was?"*

Nothing in the six questions names ENSEMBLE, the Commission, art, or a recall before the reader does.

**Reported but not thresholded** (declared here so that no post-hoc number can be presented as a
pre-registered win): whether *art* is named unprompted at Q1 or Q3; the carried-out sentence at Q6;
and any reader who says they would not perform it at all. Session 60's comparable figures — 5 of 5 a
concrete object, 0 of 5 needing the author, 3 of 5 art unprompted, 0 of 5 government document — are
**not** thresholds tonight and are quoted only as context, on a different stimulus.

**Coding rule:** answers are recorded verbatim first, then coded. Where an answer has no cell — session
60's ten readers who said *"an artist"* to a question whose code had no such value — **both readings
are printed and the verdict is taken under each**, and the un-coded answer is reported as the finding
it is.

**Who runs it:** the conductor dispatches; no voice that wrote any part of the sheet sees a reader's
answer before all five have reported.
