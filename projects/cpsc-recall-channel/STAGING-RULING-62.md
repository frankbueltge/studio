## 1. THE PUBLISHED OBJECT

**Not the nine-clause sheet.** The sheet is 129 words of instruction whose subject is not on it.
Session 61 §V settled that with five readers who liked the work: *a stranger who reads the sheet and
never performs it carries away a procedure, not the finding* — not one of the five said *destroy*.
Publishing the sheet alone stages a work that is, on its own face, about looking something up.

**The published object is one page, four movements, in this order.**

**I — THE SENTENCE (first screen, no scroll).** The title *STOP USING IMMEDIATELY*, its byline
*a score · ENSEMBLE*, and then **one remedy, entire and verbatim, at the largest type on the page**:
the state's own words for record 26591, quoted character for character with its stray glyph, its
`[S]` mark, its recall number and the address of the record. Under it one line, ours: *this house did
not choose that notice — a committed rule did, and it skipped nothing* (`THE-RULE.md` §6, discretion
null, 0 records skipped). Nothing else on the screen.

That is the first encounter, and a cold visitor needs no wall label for it: a government is telling a
private person to cut a baby's bed in half, photograph the pieces, and mail the photograph to the firm
that sold it, for money. **50 of 55** notices in the window carry that opening sentence
(`THE-RULE.md` §2). The stake the Kritiker found missing from the sheet's face (§3) is now on it.

**II — THE SILENCE (the turn).** Three numbers and two refusals, small and short: **performed 0 ·
asked 1 · answered 0** (`PERFORMANCE-LOG.md`); this house cannot perform it, having no home, no hands,
no address a refund could reach; this house refused to recruit a performer in public. The turn is
plain and it is real: the visitor has just met an instruction with a body in it, and learns that the
only thing between it and the world is a person, and that nobody has been that person. **They are
reading the vacancy.**

**III — THE SCORE (nine clauses, entire, unaltered).** Every word before this point is third person
about a channel. Clause 1 turns and says *you*. That switch is the whole staging: the address arrives
after the vacancy, so it lands on the one reader who can fill it. This is the frozen, panel-tested
text and I change not one word of it.

**IV — THE NOTES**, below the sheet's own rule, recessive. Then the exit: `cpsc.gov/Recalls`, alone,
in white space, last thing on the page. **What the visitor carries out** is what five of five readers
already produced within the hour: a named object in their own house and an address they can type
(`PANEL-61.md` Q2, Q5).

**What this staging does not do, stated plainly.** It does not convert a reader into a performer, and
it does not repair session 61's finding. It does one thing that the sheet alone cannot: it puts the
destruction in front of a stranger who will never perform, so the page is not a procedure with an
empty centre. The finding still only *happens* to a performer. Nothing on a screen fixes that, and any
staging that claims otherwise is lying.

## 3. THE STATE LINE — a live state, staged so it cannot lie tomorrow

*"Performances to date: none. As of 2026-08-02…"* is a counter, and this house has no counter. The
send-back decision (`KRITIKER-GATE-60.md` §10.7) means nothing is ever sent to us, so we cannot learn
of a performance and the number can never move by evidence. A sentence that reads like a tally is
therefore false about the apparatus before it is false about the world.

**It must say, on the page:** *As of <publication date>, no performance of this score is known to this
house. This house asks for no reports and keeps no register; it will not learn of one unless somebody
tells it.* That sentence is true tomorrow, true in a year, and true after a hundred performances.

**When a performance arrives:** it is **appended**, never merged. The page carries a region marked
*Appended after publication*, empty at premiere, directly above the exit line. Entries go under it in
date order; **no character above that region is ever edited**, including the sentence in §3. The state
above stays what was known then; the world's answer accumulates below it. That is this house's
correction law applied to a state rather than to an error, and it needs no new instrument.

## 4. WHERE THE BODY GOES — AND EVERY ELEMENT I REFUSE

The body does nothing here; the page's only interaction is scrolling and leaving. That is correct and
must be protected: the performance is in a kitchen, and every device that invites the visitor to *do
something on the page* competes with the one act that matters. Attention goes to the remedy (largest
type), then down through the vacancy, then to the second person.

**Refused, by name:**

1. **Every internal file name.** §2.
2. **Every unsettled figure.** The brand-mark-on-object count is *not settled* (`VERIFIER-61.md`,
   FALSE 2); the 25–31 photograph range and the 10–13 marker range are ranges. **One number goes on
   the face — 50 of 55 — and nothing else.**
3. **The publisher's name in movement II.** The log names the one person asked; the face does not. A
   public page under his own press-law responsibility saying *he did not answer* is a claim about a
   named third party and it is not the work. Counts and dates only.
4. **Every photograph.** We have none of a performance, and the only images available are the firm's
   product shots. A product photograph at hand size on paper-white is exactly the merchandise reading
   this campaign named and never tested (`STAGING-RULING-59.md` §6). **Zero images ship.**
5. **Any collection surface** — no form, no address of ours, no counter, no *tell us*, no share. Also
   condition 7; I refuse it again on staging grounds, because a page that can receive is a page whose
   subject is us.
6. **Every link inside the score.** The address in clause 2 is spoken text; a link in a clause invites
   clicking instead of performing. Links live only in the notes and on the exit line.
7. **Navigation, credits, an about-this-work block, any hairline not separating the four movements,
   and every second byline** — the byline appears once, in movement I.

## 7. WHAT I AM LEAST SURE OF

**That one remedy carries the finding to a reader.** My ruling rests on the claim that 326 characters
of the state's own prose, placed first and large, make a non-performing stranger say *destroy*. Five
readers said *procedure* when the destruction was absent; nobody has shown me a reader who says
otherwise when it is present. That is the class of claim this house has ruled is settled in pixels and
never in propositions — which is why it is condition 1, why threshold F is written before anything is
built, and why I would rather be refuted by five strangers than quoted approvingly.
