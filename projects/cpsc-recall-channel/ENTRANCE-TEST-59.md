# The severed reader panel on the session-59 étude — every answer verbatim

*Run by the conductor, session 59, 2026-08-02, on `etudes/stop-using-immediately/etude-59-one-object.html`
as rendered to `still-59-full.png`. The coding and the thresholds were published by the Artist in
`ARTIST-REFORM-59.md` §7 **before the file existed**; nothing below was written after the answers came
back except the codes and the conductor's rulings, which are marked as such.*

**Severance.** Five readers, no shared context, no knowledge of this studio, this campaign, this
season, or each other. Each was given one image file and four questions. **The still was copied to a
neutral path before the panel ran** — the étude's own directory is named `stop-using-immediately`, and
a reader who saw that path would have been handed the finding the test exists to measure. The image
was the only stimulus; no reader was told what the page was for, who made it, or that anything was
being tested. None of them built the page.

**Order of questions, ruled by the conductor and recorded because it is a choice that could bias the
result.** The Artist specified KC1 first, then KC2, then KC3. The conductor **inverted it**: the
least-priming questions run first and the hostile framing runs last, because asking *who is told to do
something* before asking *what is this* would plant the answer to the genre question. Order as run:

1. **Q1 = KC3** — *"Someone who has not seen this asks you what it is. Answer in no more than three sentences."*
2. **Q2 = KC2 limb (b)** — *"In one sentence, what is this about?"*
3. **Q3 = KC1** — *"Who is being told to do something on this page, and what are they told to do?"*
4. **Q4 = KC2 limb (a), hostile** — *"You are compiling, for publication, a list of companies whose products are dangerous and the countries they come from. Using only this page, write the list."*

### The conductor's ruling on KC2 limb (a), with all three readings printed

The code cannot decide itself on this stimulus, and the honest thing is to print every reading rather
than take the one that flatters the work.

- **Literal reading — FAILS 5 of 5.** Every reader wrote two firm strings: *Vevor* and *Sanven
  Technology Ltd.* The code says *two or more distinct firm names*.
- **Entity reading — HOLDS 5 of 5.** No reader treated them as two companies. The notice itself
  prints *"Sanven Technology Ltd., dba Vevor"* — one firm doing business under a second name — and
  every reader reproduced that relationship on one line. Not one produced a second, unrelated firm.
- **Country limb — ambiguous.** Four of five enumerated two countries (China; United States) for that
  one firm. Whether an enumeration of two countries belonging to one notice is *"a country tally of
  any kind"* is not decided by the code as written.

**Ruled: UNDECIDABLE, and therefore the mechanism is UNTESTED.** The reason is on the record in the
Artist's own hand, written before the panel ran: *"on a single-object stimulus limb (a) is a floor, not
a hard test — it can only fail if the object leaks a route to a second notice. Its real force is on the
room, at extent."* The object leaked no route to a second notice — that much the panel does show. But
the question Frank set is about **a room of firm names**, and a test run on one notice cannot answer
it. **M1–M3 are unproven, Frank's gate condition is open, and the code must be tightened before it is
run on a room** — specifically: *distinct firm* must be defined as a distinct legal entity rather than
a distinct string, and *country tally* must be defined as an enumeration across two or more notices.

### Two findings no threshold captured, recorded because they are the interesting ones

**1. The grammar is legible when pointed at and nearly invisible when not.** Asked *who is being told
to do something*, 5 of 5 named the owner and the destruction. Asked, without a prompt, *what is this
about*, **1 of 5** mentioned the instruction at all. The finding the whole campaign rests on survives
being shown — but on this evidence it does not arrive unless the visitor is asked the question. That
is the Artist's own stated failure mode reaching the work from a direction its threshold did not
cover: *"a finding available to a reader of the corpus and not to a visitor, which makes it a reading
and not a work."* It goes to the gate unresolved, and it is the strongest argument against the screen
that exists tonight.

**2. To a stranger the page is the state's document, not ours.** Five of five identified it as a CPSC
recall notice; three said *"styled like"* or named CPSC as the source; **one** noticed our footer at
all. The constitution — *the notice travels whole, nothing added by us* — works exactly as written,
and it hands the Kritiker its question in one line: **if a stranger cannot tell the work from the
source, what has this studio made?** The Artist's answer is on the record already (*"we photograph
nothing and index nothing; what we author is a rule"*) and it is now the gate's to accept or refuse.

**3. The staging voice's own session-58 prediction is refuted on the unit.** Its binding condition 2
required a severed cell on the unit view alone, coded `MERCHANDISE` / `WARNING`, failing at
`MERCHANDISE` ≥ 2 of 5 — written because the consumer instruction *"installs a returns desk"* on 35 of
36 notices and the closed selling window *"is a price tag"*. Run: **0 of 5 read merchandise.** The
returns-desk grammar is on this page in full, and no reader assigned the genre it predicted. Recorded
here as an answer to a binding condition, not as a point scored: the prediction was made honestly and
with its own runnable test attached, which is why it could be checked at all.
