# 0. WHAT THIS IS, IN FIVE LINES

A room of things. Every object in it was designed, manufactured, imported, listed, photographed,
bought and taken home — and then the United States government announced that it can hurt or kill
the person who owns it. The room fills one Thursday at a time, on the Commission's clock, never
on ours. You meet the whole stock before you can read one word of it. You pick one thing up. The
only instruction anybody has, printed on the state's own notice, is addressed to you.

# 1. WHY THIS AND NOT ANOTHER PDF CORPUS — the board's charge, answered first

The board's owed line tonight is that the material is due to be chosen **for what a stranger can
be shown**. So: the unit of this work is **a photograph of an object**. Not a sheet, not a row, not
a numeral. A folding wooden stool. A chest of drawers. A pool drain cover. A baby's bib. A
rechargeable hand warmer. A chandelier. Children's sandals. A bottle of paint thinner shaped like
a drink.

My predecessor's sentence against itself is the sentence I have had over my desk all night: *"I
did not search the world for material. I searched for another supremecourt.gov."* The test I set
myself was therefore not *can this house render it* but **can a stranger be shown it in one
second, with nothing read aloud**. A recalled object passes that test in the time it takes to
recognise a chair.

And I say plainly what this house's record obliges me to say: on the **material bar alone**, the
runner-up in §12 beats this channel. It is about drowning people. I did not choose against it for
production reasons — the reasons are in §12 and they are the clock and the geometry the board set
— but I record the loss on the bar in the ledger, in writing, the way my predecessor did, rather
than pretending the winner won everything.

# 3. THE CADENCE, EVIDENCED FROM THE SOURCE'S OWN RECORD

**2026-07-16, 2026-07-23, 2026-07-30 — all three are Thursdays.** (2026-01-01 was a Thursday;
2026-07-30 is day 211 of the year, ≡ day 1 mod 7.) Today, 2026-08-02, is a Sunday. **The next
expected publication day is Thursday 2026-08-06 — four days from now, inside this season, with
six to ten units on it.**

The cadence is therefore *evidenced*, not promised, and it clears the condition that killed the
first candidate outright: nothing here waits for October. I record the limit of my evidence
exactly: **three consecutive Thursdays is the whole of it.** I did not retrieve a CPSC statement
that says "we announce on Thursdays", and I do not assert one. The rule below is written so that
it does not depend on the pattern holding — it indexes to `RecallDate`, whatever day that is.

**The clock is the Commission's, not ours.** A night this studio does not sit produces no
observation and costs the work nothing: the next observation takes everything dated since. A
**week in which the Commission dated no recall** is the hole — and it is a fact about the subject
(see §11, where I name the hole as the weakest thing in this proposal).

# 5. THE UNIT — what it is made of, from a real one

M3, the recall of 2026-07-30, retrieved in full:

> **Recall Date:** July 30, 2026 · **Recall Number:** 26-651 · **Name of Product:** Nordi Foldable
> Toddler Tower Stools · **Hazard:** the stools can collapse or tip over, and children's torsos can
> fit through openings, creating *"a risk of serious injury and death due to tip over, fall and
> entrapment hazards"* · **Remedy:** Repair · **Units:** 47,166 · **Incidents/Injuries:** three
> reports of collapse or tipping, resulting in two injuries · **Sold At:** Harppababy.com and
> Amazon.com, September 2023–June 2026, ~$130 · **Importer:** HARPPA, Inc., Denver, Colorado ·
> **Manufactured In:** China · **Two product photographs.**
> **The instruction:** *"Consumers should stop using the recalled stools immediately and store them
> away from children."*

Four things in that notice are load-bearing and none of them is a quantity:

1. **A picture of a thing anyone recognises.** A wooden step-stool a toddler stands on to reach the
   counter. This is the whole of what a stranger must be shown, and it takes no reading.
2. **A closed interval:** *September 2023 – June 2026*. Two years and nine months of selling, over
   and unchangeable. This is the ending (§8).
3. **A closed harm:** three incidents, two injuries. No future unit alters them.
4. **The grammar.** The firm *"agreed to"*, the Commission *"announces"* — and the only sentence in
   the imperative is addressed to the person who bought it: **"Consumers should stop using…"**
   That is the accusation already standing in the world's own words, and it is pointed at the
   visitor.

That last point is the exact inverse of the material that killed the last concept, and I want the
gate to see the symmetry: the Prevention-of-Future-Deaths channel died because *"in this material
the state never writes a sentence in which the body that did not answer is the subject of a verb
of failing."* **In this material the state writes, on every single notice, a sentence in which the
person who owns the object is the subject of the verb.** The word is *should*, and the subject is
*you*.

# 6. THE WORK IF IT OPENS — what a visitor meets, concretely

**First encounter (no wall label, no dateline, no index, no "latest").** A pale ground. On it, the
whole stock, all of it, at a size where you can tell a dresser from a bib and cannot read a word.
It is unmistakably a *shop* — the genre a stranger assigns in two seconds is *catalogue*, and that
assignment is the trap, because a catalogue is a thing you are allowed to browse without guilt.

**The turn.** You touch one object. It comes forward at roughly the size it would be if it were in
front of you, and its notice comes with it, in the state's own words: what it does to a child, how
many were sold, how many people it already hurt, where it was sold, who imported it, where it was
made, and the sentence telling **you** to stop using yours. Nothing is added by us. The turn is
that the shop is an inventory of what is already in the visitor's house.

**The floor plan.** The stock stands in **shelf-lengths, one per publication week**, in the
Commission's own order (`RecallDate`, then `RecallNumber`). A week is allotted a fixed length; a
crowded week grows its length rather than shrinking its objects (house precedent: a papered
interval may cost more than one slot, a blank one never costs less — `ARTIST-ANSWER-55.md` Q1). A
**week the Commission dated nothing is that same length, bare.**

**The exit.** Nothing schedules you. No counter, no progress bar, no "updated weekly", no notify,
no come-back. Every state is permanently addressable; every correction stands beside its error and
never replaces it (§7).

# 7. THE UNIT RULE IN DRAFT — committed before unit 1, discretion declared

*Full text becomes `THE-RULE.md` at project-open; this is the draft the gate rules on.*

1. **Observation.** At most once per calendar day, and only on a night the studio sits, fetch
   `saferproducts.gov/RestWebServices/Recall` for `RecallDate` strictly after the last observed
   date, through today.
2. **Admission.** *Every* returned recall enters. No selection, no exclusion, no ranking, no
   editing. Order: `RecallDate` ascending, then `RecallNumber` ascending — the source's own order.
   **Discretion: zero.**
3. **Composition of an object.** First product image from the notice; plus `RecallNumber`,
   `RecallDate`, product name, hazard sentence, units, incidents/injuries, sold-at, importer or
   manufacturer, country of manufacture, the consumer instruction, and the notice URL — all
   verbatim. **A recall with no image enters as an empty place holding its notice**: a thing the
   Commission withdrew and did not show.
4. **No revision, and the source already obeys this law.** CPSC does not rewrite a notice when a
   recall grows; it issues *"Joyin **Expands** Recall of Sloosh Dive Sticks"*, *"Recall of Cpzzkq
   Baby Loungers **Expanded**"* (M2, 2026-07-30). **An expansion therefore enters as a new object
   standing beside the old one, never as a replacement** — the season's append-only rule is the
   channel's own practice, not a discipline we impose on it.
5. **Errors.** Any error of ours is corrected by an appended correction beside the error. Nothing
   is edited. A build whose hash does not match its committed data fails.
6. **Removal — the single declared exception, on the work's face.** If CPSC withdraws a notice, or
   a named party makes a written request, the object is removed and a stated removal notice stands
   in its place. No silent deletions; the removal script refuses to run without a named request
   (inherited from `ARTIST-REPAIR-57.md` §4, the one instrument of that dossier worth keeping).
7. **Auditable by an outsider** against that Thursday's published list, with no access to us.

# 8. THE ENDING, AND THE CARRIED-OUT SENTENCE

The season's ruling: *the ending is the damage*; a stranger carries out a shape they can still be
right about tomorrow. On this material the damage is **printed and closed**: the selling window
(*September 2023 – June 2026*) is over, and the incidents already happened. Neither is a fact about
whether we sat.

**The carried-out sentence — written before the first unit, to be tested at two extents:**

> **"Every one of these was bought by somebody; some of them hurt somebody; and the only
> instruction anyone has is addressed to the person who bought it."**

Each clause has a carrier on the face: *bought* → the Sold At line; *hurt* → the
Incidents/Injuries line; *the only instruction* → the consumer sentence, which is the only
imperative in the document. No clause needs a number we do not have, and no clause needs us to
say anything.

# 9. THE TAKEDOWN, AND THE REFUTATION

**The sentence a serious critic would publish:**

> **"A wall of federal recall photographs is a shopping catalogue with a body count: the studio has
> re-posted a government agency's weekly press batch in a nicer grid and called the agency's
> publication schedule a subject — the third time in one season this house has stood exactly where
> an institution stands and waited for the arrangement to do the accusing."**

**Why the built work refutes it, structurally, and not by protesting:**

1. **The accusation is not asked of the arrangement. It is asked of the visitor's house, and it is
   measured.** Both dead concepts required a page of the state's prose to accuse someone, and both
   died when it would not. This work asks the state's prose to do nothing but *identify*. The
   accusation is delivered by the visitor recognising an object they own — and the pre-registered
   test in §10 **fails the work outright if that does not happen**. A critic can call this a
   catalogue; the instrument, published before any picture, decides it.
2. **The unit's carrier is not the state's sentence — it is the seller's own picture.** The
   photograph in a recall notice is, in the ordinary case, the image made to sell the object. The
   Commission is the courier. Where PFD failed because the state's grammar could not host the
   accusation, here the accusation is in the notice's only imperative and it points at the reader
   (§5).
3. **The body-level finding is not the schedule; it is the repeat.** Three dressers, two drain
   covers, two bed rails, two step stools in three weeks (§2). One notice says *a firm sold a bad
   thing*. Two hundred say *this object fails, whoever makes it* — which is a fact about a market
   and is invisible in any single unit. Pre-registered and thresholded (§10).
4. **On "the third time": the geometry is not the same and this house has the instruments to prove
   it.** Both dead units were text on a measure; both were convicted on left edges, rule lengths
   and sorted length-encodings; the house struck two of its own instruments (T1, T3a) over it.
   **This unit has no measure, no left edge, no rule, no row.** The register charge — the founding
   constraint that has now shaped two kills — has nothing here to bite on.

**The leg I claim: (b) REAL RISK, with (a) supporting.**
*(b)*: the work publishes, under a real person's name and permanently, a standing room in which
named companies' objects are shown beside the state's account of what they did to people — and it
points at a Commission that on **2026-06-29** published *"Statement of Acting Chairman Peter A.
Feldman Regarding the Supreme Court's Decision in Trump v. Slaughter"* and on **2026-07-22**
published *"CPSC Exercises Section 12 Imminent Hazard Authority for First Time in Nearly 40
Years…"* (M5 — titles only; **I did not read the decision or the release and I assert nothing
about their contents**). If that Commission goes quiet, the shelves stay bare and the apparatus
shows it without a session and without a caption. That is power above this house, not failure
below it.
*(a)*: the repeat is a finding produced on the work's own material and re-runnable by a stranger
from the same public endpoint.

# 10. THE ACCUMULATION — what the body does that a unit does not, and the test that would show it

**The claim, in one sentence:** *A unit is a warning about an object; the body is the same object
arriving again and again from different sellers, and a stranger who cannot any longer tell whether
the thing in their own house belongs here.*

**Pre-registered, before any picture exists.** Two cells, fresh severed readers per cell, run by a
hand that did not build the work, coding published verbatim before the stimuli, every response
recorded in full including the ones that embarrass the work.

- **Cell 1** — the stock at extent 1 (one publication week, as built).
- **Cell N** — the stock at extent 20 weeks. *Rehearsal stills only, marked IMAGINED and dated
  before the first unit, per staging condition 3 and the precedent of the last dossier; simulated
  extents are never issued as units and never enter the stock.*

| limb | question put | coded present when | threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| **REPEAT** *(primary)* | "In one sentence, what is this?" | the answer names recurrence or a kind — *"lots of the same thing"*, *"all baby stuff"*, *"the same product from different brands"* | **≥ 4 of 5 at extent 20 AND ≤ 1 of 5 at extent 1** |
| **CANNOT-KNOW** *(secondary)* | after picking at least one object: "Is there anything in your home that belongs here?" | the answer expresses **inability to know**, not yes or no. *Exclusion:* naming an object shown on the shelf that they own scores RECOGNISE, not CANNOT-KNOW | **≥ 3 of 5 at extent 20 AND ≤ 1 of 5 at extent 1** |
| **RECOGNISE** *(recorded, not claimed)* | as above | they name an object of theirs | recorded for the record; no threshold |
| **ROLE** *(absolute bar, inherited)* | volunteered in any answer | **one single reader** names the buyer, the shopper or the consumer as the party **at fault** | **a cell fails outright at any count** |

**The door, per staging condition 4: if extent 1 ≥ extent 20 on REPEAT, the accumulation does
nothing and the work does not open.** The ROLE limb is the standing rule this house bought with
ten readers last session, carried in unchanged in force and re-pointed at this material's real
inversion risk — that a room of cheap online goods reads as *look what fools buy*. If it reads
that way to one person, it is not stageable, whatever the tallies say.

**Machine check, alongside and subordinate to the readers:** the share of objects whose product
type already stands in the stock. Published each observation; it is evidence, never the claim.

# 11. THE EPOCH-1 OBJECT, THE FORCING SENTENCE, AND MY OWN WEAKEST POINT

**The epoch-1 object is a screen.** Here is the sentence, for the gate to put verbatim:

> **The screen is forced because this work's unit is the picture that sold the object, and the
> Commission's own "Sold At" line says where it sold — `Harppababy.com and Amazon.com` — so every
> other form available to this house either drops the picture (sound, printed instruction, spoken
> unit) or reprints it somewhere nobody has ever bought anything (a wall, a sheet), whereas this
> work has to hand the object back on the surface it was bought on.**

**And the whole state is never available at one moment.** The entrance shows the *extent* — how
much stock there is — and at that scale not one notice is legible. The work itself is one object
at hand-size with its notice, and there is no view in which two notices can be read at once. That
is the same distinction the house drew before, and it must be argued again rather than assumed.

**MY OWN WEAKEST POINT, named by me and not left for a critic: the hole.** On this channel silence
is the *normal* state — the Commission speaks one day in seven — so a bare length of shelf does not
obviously mean *the world was quiet*; it can just as easily read as *the office was shut*. The
season made the hole load-bearing and ruled that it must read with no caption, and I do not
believe I have met that bar. What I have is honest and small: a week with nothing is a bare
shelf-length identical in size to a full one, and the reading available to a stranger is *most of
the time, nothing is withdrawn*. Whether that is a finding or a banality is the first thing I
would put to the Dramaturg, and I would rather it kill the concept at the gate than be discovered
at extent 40. **A second, lesser weakness, stated for completeness:** `SITE-API.md`'s size
guideline (~3 MB shipped, images inlined as data URIs because rasters do not travel) may cap the
extent this work can ever reach, and the differential in §10 needs extent 20 to run.

# 12. THE RUNNER-UP, AND WHY IT LOST

**Alarm Phone — the Mediterranean distress-call channel** (`https://alarmphone.org/en/`, retrieved
2026-08-02). A volunteer hotline that receives calls from boats in distress and publishes what it
heard and what the authorities did or did not do. Its own self-description as returned by the
fetch: *"Hotline for boatpeople in distress. No rescue, but Alarm."*

**Why I wanted it.** It is the one channel I found where **the accusation is already in the
world's own words and is made by the people it happened to** — which is precisely what the last
ruling instructed this house to look for. On the material bar it beats the recall channel and it
is not close.

**Why it lost, on evidence I retrieved tonight:**

1. **The clock.** The dated posts on the page I retrieved run **2026-07-18, 2026-05-14, 2026-04-06,
   2026-03-17, 2026-03-06** — irregular, with gaps of weeks to months. That fails this season's
   clock condition on the same ground that disqualified a candidate before, and I will not open a
   third concept on a cadence I have to hope for. (I retrieved only the English news index; a
   faster channel of theirs may exist elsewhere and I did not retrieve it — §14.)
2. **The geometry.** Its unit is a paragraph of an activist network's prose. Re-typesetting
   somebody's document is the exact form that has now failed twice in this house, and a third
   attempt with better material would be the same mistake wearing a better subject.
3. **The neighbours.** Forensic Oceanography's *Liquid Traces* / WatchTheMed and Banu Cennetoğlu's
   *The List* sit so close to this material that the daylight would have to be argued rather than
   shown — and Alarm Phone's own platform *is* WatchTheMed.
4. **Consent.** The unit would carry other people's emergencies, published by a network with its
   own relationship to them. That is a permission question we cannot answer from a desk.

**Two further candidates checked and dropped tonight, so the record shows what I looked at:**
parliamentary and court channels (August is recess almost everywhere — the same October trap that
killed candidate one), and the U.S. military's own daily photographic service (public domain,
enormous, showable — and it is an institution's picture of itself, which is the failure this house
has now made twice; a third time with better pictures is still a third time).

# 13. THE NEIGHBOURS, AND THE DAYLIGHT

### Outside the house

**1. Josh Begley — *Metadata+* / *Drones+*** (`https://theintercept.com/2017/03/28/after-12-rejections-apple-accepts-app-that-tracks-u-s-drone-strikes/`,
retrieved 2026-08-02). An app that *"would send you a push notification every time a U.S. drone
strike was reported in the news"*; each unit *"perhaps a date, the name of a province, maybe a body
count"*. **The nearest neighbour this proposal has**, because it is the canonical one-unit-per-event
work, and I name it first rather than let a gate find it.
**Daylight, structural:** Metadata+ *schedules the viewer* — the push notification is its entire
mechanism, and it is exactly what this season names as the gravest failure mode. It has no body: a
recipient can never meet the extent, only the newest. And its unit is metadata — a date, a place, a
count. This work forbids notification, forbids scheduling, **stages the extent before any unit**,
and its unit is a photograph of a thing, not a count of people.

**2. Christien Meindertsma — *PIG 05049*** (2007;
`https://www.artic.edu/artworks/204509/pig-05049`, retrieved 2026-08-02 via the extraction tool).
*"Tracked all 185 products made from a single pig"*, catalogued as an illustrated book, the objects
shown at true scale. The closest neighbour on *form*: an inventory of ordinary objects as an
argument about a system.
**Daylight, structural:** PIG 05049 is **closed, complete and retroactive** — 185 products, one
animal, three years of research, a book you can hold whole. Its whole state is available at one
moment; that is its achievement. This work cannot close, is never whole, admits nothing
retroactively, and its contents are chosen by a federal commission on a Thursday rather than by an
artist over three years.

**3. The recall genre's own popular form — the "failed products" collection.** Named because a
critic will name it: a wall of withdrawn consumer goods is adjacent to the curated museum of
commercial failure.
**Daylight, structural:** a failure collection is *curated, closed and comic* — someone chose the
funniest objects, and the joke is that the product flopped. Here nobody chooses, the set cannot be
closed, and the criterion is not commercial failure but **a hazard to a body, stated by the state
in the same sentence as the object's name**. *(Marked: this neighbour is named from the genre, not
from a source I retrieved tonight — I did not fetch a page for it, and it is listed as unverified
in §14.)*

### Inside the house — required by season condition 6, argued structurally

**Spielraum / Headroom** (`https://frankbueltge.de/headroom` — plain fetch returned **403**;
retrieved via the extraction tool 2026-08-02, per the house's standing rule that an unreachable
source is a claim about the fetcher until the other route is tried). It is an **instrument**: a

carried as found.
**Daylight:** every number on that page is *ours*, arrived at by arithmetic on somebody's annual
report; its whole state is available at one moment, by design, because it is an instrument and an
instrument must be readable at once; and its clock is the annual report — one tick a year. This
work computes **nothing**, has no dial, no figure of its own authorship, and ticks weekly on a body
that publishes whether or not we are watching.

**Diminishing Returns** (`projects/diminishing-returns/`, house record). Four acts, a closed corpus
of four companies' disclosures, played in sequence, arithmetic as the experience, an ending you
reach.
**Daylight:** acts imply completion, and this work has no act, no sequence, no unlock, no
playthrough and no end state; its corpus cannot be enumerated because the Commission has not
finished announcing. Where *Diminishing Returns* stages a calculation the visitor loses, this work
stages a room the visitor is already living in.

**One Tap** (`works/2026-07-23-one-tap/`, premiered 2026-07-23, **killed 2026-07-25** after three
stagings the house's own gate had passed were rejected by the human eye — *"badly staged, and it is
not art"*).
**Daylight, and the lesson taken rather than the difference asserted:** One Tap was a
single-moment argument built out of five figures on one screen, with the apparatus — the panel, the
self-test — visible as the staging. It died because the instrument *was* the work. This work has no
panel, no self-test on its face, no figure of ours, and nothing at all to read at the entrance: the
apparatus is invisible and the only things on the ground are objects. And I take its harder lesson
literally — that passing this house's gate is not evidence a thing is art — which is why §10's
primary instrument is five strangers' sentences and not a coefficient.

# 14. WHAT WOULD KILL THIS CONCEPT

1. **The permission.** If CPSC (or Frank's own legal reading) says a standing room of two hundred
