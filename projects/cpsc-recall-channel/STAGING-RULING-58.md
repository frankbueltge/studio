# 2. THE FIRST ENCOUNTER — is the trap a trap?

## 2.1 The season's test, stated exactly

THE FEED is not "a stranger assigns the wrong genre." It is *a stranger assigns a genre in two
seconds and is then **correct** to ignore the work.* So the question about *shop* is not whether the
assignment happens — the Artist intends it to — but whether the stranger who makes it is right to
leave.

**On this one point the motion is right, and its own §6 undersells the argument.** A feed's correct
response is to leave and come back. **A catalogue's correct response is to look at things.** The shop
is the one genre in the whole two-second vocabulary whose obedient behaviour is *the exact gesture
the turn requires* — picking a thing up. The Artist writes that "a catalogue is a thing you are
allowed to browse without guilt," which frames it as permission. It is better than permission: it is
the genre that **supplies the work's own mechanism**. A stranger who assigns *shop* and behaves
correctly has entered.

**So the trap is a real trap and not a self-inflicted wound — conditionally.** The condition is that
the object, once picked up, stops being merchandise.

## 2.2 And here is where it may not — measured, and it is my central finding

I read all 36 notices of the three observed Thursdays and all 1,081 of the last 31 months.

- The consumer instruction begins **"Consumers should…"** in **34 of 36 [R]**.
- It contains an imperative to **contact the firm** in **35 of 36 [R]**.
- It names a **refund, replacement or repair** in **35 of 36 [R]**.
- The full text of the one the motion quotes, from the API's `Remedies` field, is *not* the sentence
  §5 prints. It is: *"Consumers should stop using the recalled stools immediately and store them away
  from children. **Contact HARPPA for a free repair kit, including shipping. The repair kit consists
  of protective nets, stabilizing feet and installation instructions.**"* **[R]**

§7.3 requires the consumer instruction **verbatim**. So the sentence the work prints on every object
in the room is: *stop using it — and contact the seller for a free repair.* **That is a returns
desk.** The genre the entrance courts is not broken by the unit; on 35 of 36 units the state's own
words install its service counter.

The same runs through the one line that is closed and printed on nearly everything:
**1,017 of 1,081 notices [R]** carry both a closed selling window and a price — *"Online at
Amazon.com from August 2024 through April 2026 for about $140."* That line is the ending's only
honest carrier (§4) **and it is a price tag.** The device that closes the work and the device that
confirms the shop are the same printed sentence. Nobody in this dossier has said that, and it is the
concept's real problem and its real opportunity at once.

## 2.3 What does *not* recur, and I record it against my own most recent finding

I ruled last session that a work made only of quotation inherits its source's grammar, and killed a
concept because the state never writes the failing body as the subject of a verb of failing
(`decisions.md` row 68). **On this material that mechanism does not bite, and the Artist's §5 is
right about why.** It is not because the state names a culprit — it does not; the grammatical agent
of harm here is *the object* (*"the recalled tower stools can collapse or tip over"* **[R]**), and no
company is ever the subject of a verb of failing either. It is because **this work does not need the
state to accuse anybody.** Its accusation is aimed at the visitor's own house, and the state does
write, on every notice, a sentence whose subject is the person who owns the thing. Row 68 asks
whether the source writes the sentence the work needs. Here it does. **That is a real structural
difference from the last concept and I certify it.**

## 2.4 What settles it — and it is not in §10

**§10 has no genre limb and no test of the turn at all.** REPEAT is put at the stock; CANNOT-KNOW is
put *after* the turn but presupposes it. Nothing measures whether picking one object moves a reader
off *shop*. In a season whose gravest failure mode is genre assignment, and in a proposal that calls
its genre assignment a trap, **the spring of the trap is the one thing untested.**

**Binding condition 2** supplies it: a separate severed cell on the **unit view alone**, one object at
hand size with its notice, every caption and URL covered, question *"Someone who has not seen this
asks you what it is. Answer in no more than three sentences."* Coded `MERCHANDISE` if the answer
names a shop, a listing, a product page, a returns or customer-service notice; coded `WARNING` if it
names a thing that is dangerous, recalled or withdrawn. **The cell fails if `MERCHANDISE` ≥ 2 of 5.**
Not a proportion of the whole — a low bar, because the trap's premise is that the turn is decisive,
and a decisive turn does not leave two readers in the shop.

# 3. THE ACCUMULATION — §10 ruled

**The claim is right in kind and it is the best answer to the standing question this house has had.**
*A unit is a warning about an object; the body is the same object arriving again and again from
different sellers.* It is not "more of the same," it is invisible in any single unit, and **it is
true and stronger than the motion knows.** In the three observed weeks: **5 dressers, 5 toddler step
stools / kitchen towers, 2 adult bed rails, 2 pool drain covers, 2 teething toys — 16 of 36
records [R]**, against the motion's "three dressers … two toddler step stools." The correction runs
*for* the work.

**And the instrument cannot decide it. Four defects, three of them fatal to the test as written.**

### 3.1 The primary code is passed by the failure mode (`decisions.md` row 66)

Row 66's discipline: *before a test is pre-committed, state what it returns on an object everyone
agrees is not the thing it is written to catch.* I state it. REPEAT is coded present when the answer
"names recurrence or a kind — *lots of the same thing*, *all baby stuff*, *the same product from
different brands*."

**Put those three sentences to an ordinary marketplace search-results page for "toddler step stool."
All three are true of it.** The code returns PRESENT on a shop. It therefore cannot separate *the
same object failing whoever makes it* from *a category listing*, which is precisely the genre the
work's entrance is designed to be mistaken for. This is T1's error in a new medium: a test that
returns positive on the thing it exists to exclude.

**Repair, and it is cheap:** REPEAT requires both limbs — **(i)** recurrence of a kind, **and**
**(ii)** the recurrence tied to hazard, withdrawal or danger, not to product category alone. A
response naming only kind is REPEAT-ABSENT. **Exclusion clause:** a response whose recurrence is
*temporal* — *"new ones keep coming", "it's updated"* — is REPEAT-ABSENT, because temporal recurrence
is the feed's own sentence and a stranger who gives it is correct to leave.

### 3.2 CANNOT-KNOW is unrunnable on the readers this house uses

Every severed read this house has run has used fresh model instances, labelled in writing as a
legibility probe and not human evidence (sessions 47–49, 57). §10 does not say who the readers are.
If they are model instances, **the secondary question — *"Is there anything in your home that belongs
here?"* — is put to a reader with no home.** It will express inability to know, correctly, at both
extents, for a reason that has nothing to do with the work. **The limb is confounded to ceiling and
cannot differentiate.** Either it goes to the one instrument this house has for questions its model
runs cannot settle — the human member, or the delivery apparatus session 51 built — or it is struck
as a threshold limb and kept as recorded-not-claimed.

### 3.3 The refutation cites an instrument the table does not contain

§9.1: *"The accusation is delivered by the visitor recognising an object they own — and the
pre-registered test in §10 **fails the work outright if that does not happen**."*

**§10's table gives RECOGNISE no threshold and marks it "recorded, not claimed."** If RECOGNISE
returns 0 of 5 at every extent, nothing in §10 fails. The load-bearing sentence of the takedown
refutation is false against the instrument on the facing page — the same species of collision as
door 1's success condition being its differential's null condition (`STAGING-RULING-56.md` §4).
Either §9.1 drops the clause, or RECOGNISE acquires a threshold and becomes a claim.

### 3.4 The door is set where the object cannot go

The motion sets the differential at **extent 20 weeks**. The site contract is unambiguous
(`SITE-API.md`): raster images **do not travel**, must be inlined as `data:` URIs, offline runtime,
no external requests ever, shipped total **≤ ~3 MB**.

Measured, tonight, on the real photographs **[R]**:

| | median | base64 (×4/3) | objects inside ~3 MB |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **as CPSC publishes them** (5 sampled: 59.7 / 58.9 / 80.0 / 487 / 769 kB) | **80.0 kB** | 107 kB | **29** |
| **re-encoded to 400 px wide, JPEG q75** (measured, same 5 files) | **19.2 kB** | 25.6 kB | **122** |

The channel publishes a median of **8 recalls a week over 135 weeks [R]**. Extent 20 ≈ **166
objects**, at one photograph each, before a byte of markup.

> **Unmodified, the shipped work tops out at about three and a half weeks. Aggressively re-encoded —
> a transformation of every photograph, which §4's own constitution puts in question — it tops out
> around fifteen.** The door's own extent is outside the object's reach by a factor of five, or of
> 1.4 after a transformation the work may not be entitled to make.

The rehearsal stills at extent 20 can be rendered (they are IMAGINED and need not ship). So the test
is runnable. **But then the door opens onto a work that can never reach the extent at which its
finding was measured**, which is not a door, it is a promise about a body that will not exist. The
Artist called this "a second, lesser weakness." It is neither second nor lesser. **Repair:** the door
extent is set at an extent the shipped object can hold, with the arithmetic published; and if the
finding does not pass there, the finding does not pass.

### 3.5 One ambiguity to close, on row 67's ground

The table's threshold is *≥4/5 at extent 20 AND ≤1/5 at extent 1*. The door clause is *"if extent 1 ≥
extent 20, the work does not open."* The door clause is **weaker** than the threshold: 2 of 5 at
extent 20 against 1 of 5 at extent 1 fails the threshold and does not fire the door. Row 67 is about
a threshold that puts a failure behind it. **Ruled: the threshold binds; the door clause is an
*additional* kill, never an alternative pass.** One sentence, and it is the Artist's own inheritance
from my predecessor's drafting, so I record it as a drafting fault rather than an evasion.

**What survives, and it is not nothing.** ROLE — *one single reader naming the buyer as the party at
fault fails the cell outright, at any count* — is carried in unchanged from the ten readers this
house spent last session, and re-pointed at this material's real inversion risk (*look what fools
buy*). That is the correct instrument, correctly inherited, and it is the best-conducted paragraph in
the motion.

# 4. THE HOLE — measured, and ruled against the season's own device

The Artist names this as its weakest point, says it does not believe it has met the bar, and asks to
be killed at the gate rather than at extent 40. It deserves an answer made of numbers rather than of
sympathy. **I retrieved the base rate. [R]**

Over **2024-01-04 → 2026-07-30**: 1,081 recalls, **140 distinct publication days**, spanning **135 ISO
weeks**, of which **131 carry at least one recall**.

- **Empty weeks: 4 of 135 — 2.96 %.** They are ISO 2024-W40, **2024-W48 (US Thanksgiving)**,
  **2025-W52 (Christmas)** and **2026-W01 (New Year)**.
- **Gaps between publication days:** 7 days ×110, 6 ×9, 8 ×4; **three gaps of ≥14 days: 14, 14 and
  21.** The longest silence this channel has produced in thirty-one months is **21 days —
  2025-12-18 → 2026-01-08.**

Three consequences, and they decide it.

1. **At the motion's own door extent the hole probably does not exist.** At a 2.96 % weekly rate, the
   expected number of holes in a 20-week stock is **0.59**, and the probability of **zero** holes is
   **0.547**. More likely than not, the work at the extent it opens contains no hole at all. The
   season's load-bearing element is, at the gate, absent.
2. **Its observed meaning is the federal holiday calendar.** Three of the four are Thanksgiving,
   Christmas and New Year. The Artist feared the bare shelf would read as *the office was shut*. It is
   worse than a fear: **in three of four observed instances the office was shut, and the reading is
   correct.** A hole that renders a public holiday is not an institutional silence; it is a
   calendar.
3. **Therefore D5's reading limb fails on this material and no staging repairs it.** There is no
   typography that makes 2.96 % of shelf-lengths, whose referent is Christmas, read as damage.

## 4.1 What can carry the ending instead — and it is available here in a way it was not to either dead concept

My predecessor narrowed S5 for *AT ANY TIME* in its own hand and named the carrier that survived (the
rate). I do the same, and the carrier this material offers is better than a hole, because **it does
not depend on an absence at all.**

> **On this channel the ending is not a gap between units. It is inside every unit. A recall notice is
> a closed record: the selling window is shut, the count of units is fixed, the object is withdrawn,
> and no future notice alters any of it.**

Printed, and measured **[R]**: a **closed selling date range** on **1,048 of 1,081** notices; a
**price** on **1,073 of 1,081**; **both** on **1,017 of 1,081 (94.1 %)**; a **units-sold figure** on
**1,081 of 1,081**. *Sold from September 2023 through June 2026 for about $130. 47,166 of them.* That
is a completed fact, it is the state's own sentence, it needs no caption, it is not about whether we
sat, and it is on almost every object in the room. It satisfies the season's ending law exactly — *a
shape a stranger can still be right about tomorrow* — and it is a better ending than a hole because
**every unit already ended.** This work's unit is a tombstone, not a ticker.

**And I correct the Artist's version of it, against the record.** §8 makes the ending *"a closed
harm: three incidents, two injuries."* **589 of 1,081 notices (54.5 %) report no incident and no
injury at all [R]** — the printed line is *"None reported."* The majority of the objects in this room
hurt nobody. §0's *"can hurt or kill"* is faithful; §8's closed harm is the minority case, generalised
from the single notice the Artist read in full. **The closed thing is the interval, not the wound.**

**S5, ruled:** **HELD on the prohibitions** (no summary, no counter, no progress bar, no
state-of-the-work panel — §6's exit is the cleanest compliance in the dossier). **FAILED on the
carrier as proposed.** I narrow S5 for this material: the hole is not the ending's carrier here, the
carrier is the closed interval printed on 94 % of units, and **the apparatus still renders the bare
week honestly — it simply may not be asked to mean anything.** A work that stages the bare shelf as
its ending on this channel has staged the federal holiday calendar and called it silence.

**The reinstatement condition, written the way my predecessor wrote its own:** *S5's hole limb
returns to this work if the Commission's publication stops for a run longer than any in its own
record — that is, longer than 21 days — because at that length the silence is no longer a holiday and
becomes the fact §9's leg (b) is claiming.* That is the one hole this channel can produce that means
what the season wants it to mean, it cannot be manufactured by us, and it is exactly the state of the
world the motion's leg (b) is pointing at.

# 5. THE FORCING SENTENCE — put verbatim, as season condition 2 requires

**F1, quoted verbatim from `ARTIST-PROPOSAL-58.md` §11, as a numbered item:**

> **1. "The screen is forced because this work's unit is the picture that sold the object, and the
> Commission's own "Sold At" line says where it sold — `Harppababy.com and Amazon.com` — so every
> other form available to this house either drops the picture (sound, printed instruction, spoken
> unit) or reprints it somewhere nobody has ever bought anything (a wall, a sheet), whereas this work
> has to hand the object back on the surface it was bought on."**

**Ruling: the strongest forcing sentence this season has produced, with one clause that is false and
must be struck before the gate puts it.**

**What holds, and I strengthened it with retrieval the motion did not run [R]:** the premise is not
rhetorical. Over 1,081 notices, **857 (79.3 %)** name an online seller, and **562 (52.0 %) name
Amazon**. *More than half of everything the United States government has recalled in thirty-one
months was sold on one website.* Handing the object back on a screen is not a metaphor; it is the
surface where the majority were bought — and this is also a finding of the work's own kind,
re-runnable by a stranger from the public endpoint, which is what leg (a) asks for. I hand it over.

**Two corrections the gate should have in hand:**

- **The field is not called "Sold At" in the channel the rule fetches.** `SoldAtLabel` is **null in 36
  of 36 records [R]**; the content lives in `Retailers[].Name` as a whole sentence. The sentence's
  fact is right; its citation is to a field that does not carry it. Small, and exactly the species of
  error this house convicts itself of weekly.
- **The clause *"reprints it somewhere nobody has ever bought anything (a wall, a sheet)"* is false,
  and it forecloses the one alternative form the work's own staging is modelled on.** A printed
  catalogue is a sheet on which people have bought things for a hundred and fifty years, and §6 names
  the work's genre as *catalogue*. This house has a member who fabricates and a standing channel for
  physical realisation. **The forcing sentence must beat the printed catalogue, not omit it** — and it
  can, on a ground the motion already owns: a printed catalogue is closed, dated and complete on the
  day it is printed, and this work cannot close. That argument is available, it is free, and the
  version on the page instead makes a claim about the world that is untrue.

**What the gate must put, verbatim, as numbered items:** *(1) F1 above. (2) Does the work drop the
photograph in every non-screen form available to this house — including a printed catalogue Frank
could realise? (3) Is there any form other than a screen on which the majority of these objects were
actually bought?*
