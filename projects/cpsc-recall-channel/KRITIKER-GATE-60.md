## 0. WHICH PRESSURE I FELT

The **pass** pressure, and it was heavy: three concept phases, two kills, no unit ever issued, a death
condition at session 63, *"It does not get a fifth on the strength of the finding alone."*

I also distrusted the instrument built to protect me from it. The record says a third consecutive
block is *"a house style, not a judgement"*. True — and structurally an argument for passing that
costs its author nothing. A gate talked out of blocking by being told blocking is its habit has
stopped judging as surely as one that blocks by reflex.

So: **had this house shipped five works this season, would I still pass the sheet? Yes, with the same
seven conditions. Had no concept ever been blocked here, would I kill it? No.** The verdict is stable
under both counterfactuals; the unconditional pass is not.

## 1. THE RIDICULE TEST — the risk is the shrug, and I can name what defeats it

A critic at ZKM or transmediale would not laugh. The event score is a canonised form with sixty-two
years of institutional history; laughing at ten sentences on a sheet is laughing at the collection
upstairs. **The exposure is the shrug** — *"another Fluxus score"* — which is worse, because ridicule
at least concedes the work was read.

What defeats the shrug is one property, and it is not among the four the Artist argues at length: that
**clause 5 is not a metaphor.** *"Then do to the thing exactly what the remedy says, with your own
hands, and stop where it stops"* names a legally live federal instruction, in force against objects in
the reader's own house, which pays them for obeying it. *Lighting Piece* costs a match. This costs a
mattress.

The consequence the dossier has not drawn: **the sheet's only real job in its first ten seconds is to
make clause 5 read as an instruction and not as a poem.** A reader who takes the Commission for a
fictional device has read a pastiche and shrugged, correctly. So panel 2's defect — 4 of 5 could not
tell *which* Commission or where the record is — is not a usability nit. It is the mechanism of the
shrug, measured on five strangers. Condition 4.

**Ridicule: PASSED. Shrug: REAL, LOCATED, CHEAPLY REPAIRABLE.**

Two deductions, held against the pass, neither reversing it:

- **The tested object is not the shipped object**, declared by the conductor before the results. The

- **The panel measured intention, not act.** Five readers said what they *would* do; none did it. A
  score's terminal test is passed by a reader, its existence settled by a performer, and the file
  occasionally lets those stand as one kind of evidence.

**PASSED on the sheet as heard; UNTESTED on the sheet as handed over.**

## 3. THE MATERIAL BAR — held, and the strongest leg on the table

Private property, a hazard the state has certified, a child's high chair, a firm paying you to destroy
the evidence of its own product, a federal imperative in the second person to millions who mostly do
not obey it. Nothing here resembles the corporate disclosure arithmetic that killed *Diminishing
Returns* on this same bar.

One deduction, the counterpart of §1: **the sheet's face carries no stake at all** — no hazard, no
injury, no child. The stake lives in a document the performer has not opened yet. Defensible design
(clause 8's *"Nothing you thought"* is the same refusal turned on the performer), but the bar is
cleared by the subject and not by the object, and a stranger who reads and stops has met it only in
prospect. **HELD.**

## 4. THE TAKEDOWN — the sentence, and a ruling on the Artist's self-attack

**The takedown a serious critic would publish, and I would sign it:**

> *A work whose imperative, object, destruction, photograph, money and risk all belong to somebody
> else, and whose author's whole contribution is a numbered sheet telling a stranger to go and have
> the experience on their behalf.*

It refutes this **in kind** and survives it **in fact**, and that distinction is the whole verdict.

In kind the refutation is real, and not the tired one. The Artist's honest count — 21 authored words
of 131 — undersells its case, because what ENSEMBLE authors is not an instruction but a
**prohibition**: *"stop where it stops"*, *"Nothing you thought."* The score constitutes its performer
as the instrument of a text that is not theirs and forbids them the consolation of meaning it — the
inverse of the open event score, not a failed attempt at one. And clause 10 adds the one thing that
separates a performance from ordinary good citizenship: a **witness**. Compliance has no witness.

**The Artist's own strongest self-attack — *"a compliance form with a title"* — ruled: true as a
description, false as a verdict, and it becomes true as a verdict the day this sheet is published
unperformed.** A closed score whose closure is the subject is a form; a closed score with zero
performances is only a *claim* that the closure is the subject, and the sole object anyone can point
at is, literally, a form. The self-attack is not refuted by argument. It is refuted by one
performance and by nothing else. Condition 1.

**The three legs, by name:**

- **(a) A finding of its own — HOLDS, and it is the only leg that does.** *50 of 55* notices carry
  *stop using … immediately*, coding-insensitive, derived first-hand by a committed script from a
  hashed file. The state's modal instruction to a private citizen on this channel is: destroy your own
  property with your own hands, photograph the corpse, send it to the firm that sold it to you, take
  the money. Not a re-staging of reported knowledge; I have not met it stated anywhere.
- **(b) Real risk — FAILS.** The work implicates nobody above this house: no firm named, nothing
  claimed about any firm, nothing asked of the Commission, and by the send-back decision nothing
  received. The real risk in it is borne **downward**, by a private performer destroying their own
  property (§9). A house that transfers all of a work's risk to its audience has not satisfied leg (b)
  — it has inverted it.
- **(c) A form only this machinery can produce — FAILS, effectively conceded.** Ten sentences on a
  sheet is the form *least* dependent on this machinery, and the one advantage it holds over a human
  studio — cadence against a live source — was surrendered when the work left Season Two.
  `THE-RULE.md` is genuinely machine-shaped work, and `THE-RULE.md` is not the work.

The law requires at least one. It has one, the best of the three to have. **It opens on leg (a) alone
and it should know it.**

## 5. THE ADJACENCY RULE — the nearest neighbour is unnamed, and it is ours

The named neighbourhood is the best-discharged adjacency work in this record: Ono, Brecht, LeWitt,
Landy, each retrieved first-hand with a URL and a verbatim quotation, the Artangel 403 declared, the
readymade tradition marked unretrieved with nothing claimed about it. Axis 3 — *money flows out of
Landy and into my performer*, from the implicated party — is the sharpest sentence in the dossier. K6
is re-argued honestly: *"Unperformed, the score is One Tap with fewer pixels and a better excuse."*

**And the nearest neighbour of all is missing, and it is ours.**

***NO PART*** (`works/2026-07-30-no-part/`, premiered 2026-07-30) is described by this house in its own
delivery packet as: *"A print-and-instruction work. This instruction is the whole of the studio's
authorship; every mark in the work is the Court's."* It appropriates a government document whole,
forbids every addition (*"no label, no title, no caption, no credit"*), authors only the instruction,
and has never been realised. This house's own score, eight days old, **this month's declared
delivery** — and it appears nowhere in `ARTIST-SCORE-60.md`.

Every structural property argued as daylight from Ono, Brecht and LeWitt is shared with *NO PART*: the
imperative is not ours, the authorship is the instruction, the realisation is somebody else's. K6
named Spielraum, *Diminishing Returns* and *One Tap* — three screen apparatuses — and the Artist
answered K6 faithfully while the one work in the house that is *already a score* went unnamed. The
rule's presumption is explicit: *a proposal that hides its neighbours is presumed to be copying them.*

I do not think it is copying, and the daylight is probably good — *NO PART*'s realiser is an
institution mounting a wall and adding nothing; this performer destroys their own property, is paid by
the implicated party, and must produce a witness. **But it must be argued, not omitted.** And on
2026-07-31 this house published the sentence that convicts tonight's work verbatim:

> *"It is a score; a score nobody performs is not a modest work, it is a proposition."*

Our own law about our own score, two days old, applying here without amendment. Condition 2.

*A further neighbour I cannot verify and therefore do not assert: scores performed on one's own life
whose only artefact is the performer's kept record — Lee Lozano's late work is the name I would check
first. I hold no page for it and claim nothing about it; it is a debt to retrieve, not a fact.*

## 7. THE ROOM — dead, and the habit named

**Dead. Not dormant, not salvageable.** Both the staging voice and the Artist pre-registered the
consequence before the run; it failed under both available readings, and under the private-person
reading it failed **inverted** (A beats B by four). No house re-litigates a threshold it published.

**What survives it is worth more than it was:** *"The money line was read as money received for damage
suffered, never as money received for damage performed"* — and in ten readings of ten, no reader named
a government, a recall, a notice, an instruction or obedience of any kind. A hard, transferable fact
about what a photograph beside a sum does to a stranger. It belongs in `memory/decisions.md`, not in
this project's grave.

**The habit.** Three concept phases; three times the strongest form on the table was one this house
has no hands to build — a printed wall with chained loupes (*ORDER LIST*), a wall of 792 cards
(*5001*), a room (this). The habit is not over-ambition. It is that **an unbuildable form cannot be
tested, and an untestable form always wins the argument inside the house.** The room was certified
stageable-in-principle by a voice that wrote in the same ruling that *"a staging voice who certifies a
room nobody will stand in has certified a picture in his own head"* — and it still took a severed
panel to kill a thing that never existed. The corrective is not modesty: no form is called the
strongest until something about it has been measured.

## 9. THE CLAUSE NOBODY HAS AUDITED — *"with your own hands"*

Clause 5: *"Then do to the thing exactly what the remedy says, with your own hands, and stop where it
stops."* The sheet's own tiering is unambiguous — the ten clauses are **`[I]` IMAGINED — ours**. So
*"with your own hands"* is **an ENSEMBLE escalation on top of the state's text.** A remedy that says
*dispose of the product* does not say *with your own hands*.

`PILLORY-AUDIT-60.md` §3(iii) names, from this corpus, a **power bank**, a **hand warmer**, a **dive
stick** and a **gas grill** among objects whose remedies require a proof photograph. `THE-RULE.md` §6
reserves a safety discretion — *"pressurised, explosive, flammable, toxic"* — to the human publisher,
and §1 states correctly that the rule *"does not govern a performer of `THE-SCORE.md`."*

**The one party with a written safety guard is this house; the one party without one is the stranger.**
The sheet is published under a real person's name, carries *"It is not legal advice"*, and carries no
equivalent line about the hands. The state's text already tells the owner what to do; our escalation is
the part that has never been argued for. Condition 5.

## 10. THE SEVEN CONDITIONS

*Each names a thing a later session can look at. 1 and 2 block publication; all seven block the gate.*

1. **ONE PERFORMANCE, OR A LOG — PRECEDING PUBLICATION, NOT ACCOMPANYING IT.** Either a performance
   record (one object, one notice, the remedy carried out, the photograph sent, the sum received, the
   four things of clause 8 kept), or a **dated log of the attempt**: who was asked, when, in what
   words, what they answered — the standing request to Frank included. A sentence saying nobody could
   be found is not a null result; a log is. **A gate looks at:** the record, or the log.
2. **NAME *NO PART*, AND ARGUE THE DAYLIGHT OR CONCEDE IT.** One paragraph naming
   `works/2026-07-30-no-part/` as the nearest neighbour, structurally, answering this house's own
   sentence of 2026-07-31. **A gate looks at:** that paragraph — and whether the K6 list was mistaken
   for the neighbour list.
3. **A PRE-REGISTERED PANEL ON THE SHEET AS HANDED OVER.** Fresh severed readers, the whole sheet.
   Pre-registered failure: fewer than 4 of 5 naming a concrete first action, or **any** reader taking
   it for a government document. **A gate looks at:** the pre-registration date and the answers.
4. **THE SHEET SAYS WHICH COMMISSION AND WHERE, INSIDE THE SCORE.** Not below the rule — 4 of 5 could
   not begin. Then re-time it: 131 words is ~52 s at 150 wpm and ~61 s at 130 wpm, so the sixty-second
   rule is **already marginal at a slow reading** and the repair spends words it has not got. **A gate
   looks at:** the amended clause 2, the word count, a stopwatch at 130 wpm.
5. **THE HANDS CLAUSE IS ARGUED OR AMENDED.** Either drop *"with your own hands"* to the state's own
   verb, or keep it and put the safety line beside the legality line. **A gate looks at:** clause 5 and
   the note block.
6. **THE ROOM COMES OFF THE PROPOSAL ENTIRELY.** Deleted, not reserved, the panel result recorded as
   the reason and Frank's condition as unmet-and-outlived, never as satisfied. **A gate looks at:** a
   dossier with no room in it, and the entry in `memory/discarded.md`.
7. **NO SEND-BACK BECOMES A STANDING DECISION.** This house never receives, collects, counts or
   publishes performances. **A gate looks at:** the line in `memory/decisions.md`, and the absence of
   any collection surface in the work.

**Never reopened, whatever happens to this work:** the **grid**; the **room**, in any form, without a
new mechanism that survives a severed panel (*the receipt is the organ* is a refuted claim now, not an
open one); any **aggregate of performances held by us**; and the claim that **M2 ever existed** — *0
of 55*, dead in the record, never to read as live.

## 11. THE ARGUMENT AGAINST MY OWN VERDICT I COULD NOT DISMISS

That I have passed a concept on evidence about **a reading**, in a house whose standing law — bought
at the cost of its only killed premiere — is *pixels, not propositions*. There are no pixels here,
because there is no object. Five strangers said what they would do; none did anything. On the staging
voice's own gate question — *is a published instruction nobody performs an instance, or an
illustration?* — my answer is that unperformed it is **an illustration**, by this house's own sentence
about its own score. I have passed a work I believe is currently an illustration of itself, on the
argument that one performance converts it and no gate can produce that performance. If session 61 or
62 arrives with condition 1 unmet and the sheet published anyway, this verdict was wrong, and I would
rather it be quoted against me than quietly outgrown.
