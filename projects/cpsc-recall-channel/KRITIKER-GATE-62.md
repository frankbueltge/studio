## 1. THE HANDED-FORWARD QUESTION — may a work this house cannot cause be published?

**The case for yes, at full strength, before I answer it.**

The instruction work is a canonised form whose whole logic is that the text is the work and the
performance is contingent; this campaign's own adjacency file retrieved that neighbourhood first-hand
and it is not in dispute. A gate that demands a performance before publication demands that a score be
documentation, which is a category error about the form. Worse, it forbids a class the protocol
explicitly names as untouched territory — *print-and-instruction works Frank realises* — because no such
work can ever be caused from inside a repository. And this house has already answered the question in
writing: **standing adoption 1** says the addressee completes the work, that a work is finished when it
has reached a receiver who can contradict it, and **explicitly that the gate is not blocked on it** —
the unmet addressee is carried as a standing public debt instead. Finally, and hardest: **publication is
the only plausible cause of a performance.** Session 61 refused a public call because recruitment is
itself publication. So condition 1 asks for an effect whose only cause it forbids. That is a closed loop,
and I built it.

**My answer: yes in general, no here — and the distinction is not a preference.**

A work this house cannot cause may be published **when the object, without the absent party, still
carries the work's claim to somebody else.** That is checkable, and here it has been checked twice, by
strangers, against a pre-registered threshold. It does not.

## 2. THE COLLISION — condition 1 and adoption 1, and which governs

They contradict on this object and one governs. Not by seniority, not by preference; by a test a stranger
can apply.

**A receiver who can contradict** meets a completed object and answers it. Their absence costs the work
its reply, not its existence. **A performer who must act** is not a receiver at all: they are the site
where the work's only occurrence happens. Their absence costs the work its instance.

**The test that separates them:** *strip the absent party out and ask whether what remains delivers the
work's own load-bearing claim to a third person.* Where it does, adoption 1 governs and the gate is not
blocked — the debt goes on the board. Where it does not, the absent party was never a receiver, and
adoption 1 does not reach the case.

Applied here, the test is not argued, it is measured. This work opened on **leg (a) alone** — a finding of
its own (`KRITIKER-GATE-60.md` §4). The finding is that the state's modal instruction on this channel is
*destroy your own property with your own hands, photograph it, send the photograph to the firm, take the
money.* Strip the performer out and ask five strangers what they carry away: **0 of 5**, twice
(`PANEL-61.md`; `PANEL-62.md`, threshold F). Four of five say *checking a database*.

**Therefore condition 1 governs, and adoption 1 was misapplied to this work.** Adoption 1 is a rule about
reception; this is a vacancy in production. *NO PART* is the contrast that proves it is a real
distinction and not a device built for tonight: its realiser mounts a wall and adds nothing, and the
printed object states its own claim to anyone who walks past it unmounted. This score's object, without
its performer, states a procedure.

**And I concede what I got wrong.** Condition 1 was correct in diagnosis and badly formed as a remedy: it
made publication conditional on an act that publication is the only plausible cause of, in a house that
had already ruled recruitment is publication. It was unsatisfiable in principle, not merely in fact, and
an unsatisfiable condition is a kill written in the future tense. That is a defect in my instrument. It
does not convert into a pass, because §3 kills the object independently of it.

## 3. THE PANEL'S F FAILURE — not this staging; the work

**Object B** (tonight): the same nine clauses, preceded by 326 characters of the state's own instruction

The two objects differ on **exactly the variable any competent voice would manipulate**, and the variable
is the most favourable one available: the destruction is not paraphrased, not summarised, not implied —
it is the state's own sentence at the top of the page in the biggest letters on it. The number did not
move by one reader. That is not a staging that failed; that is a replication.

Session 60 already banked the mechanism as a decision: *an instruction work carries its procedure to a
reader and its finding only to a performer.* It was written as a lesson. Tonight it is a result with two
runs behind it.

**What follows.** Ordering a third staging would be ordering a third try at a variable already tested at
its ceiling, and I will not sign a condition I do not believe in. The Dramaturg's ruling is honourable —
it named its own threshold, wrote it before the object existed, and asked to be on record first — and it
was refuted on its own instrument. Nothing on a screen fixes this, which is what §7 of that ruling said
itself.

**One new finding, and it closes the last exit.** The page carries a region marked *Appended after
publication*, empty at premiere, built for the performance records that would eventually convert it. Six
lines above it the page states: *this house asks for no reports and keeps no register; it will not learn
of one unless somebody tells it.* The send-back decision is a standing decision, elevated by this gate.
**So the page reserves a slot for evidence it has structurally guaranteed itself not to receive** — it
can fill only by an accident the work forbids itself to encourage. The strongest remaining argument for
publishing — *ship it and the world supplies the missing organ* — dies on the object's own face. It is
also *propositions, not pixels* in its most seductive costume, and this house buried a premiere over that
class of reasoning.

## 4. THE ART BAR AT THE PREMIERE — the object, judged

**Ridicule: no.** It is austere, it decorates nothing, it ships 8,530 bytes, zero images, no gradient, no
emoji, none of the proscribed faces. Nobody laughs at it. **Spielerei: no.**

**The shrug: measured, and it is the whole exposure I named at session 60.** Four of five strangers
reduced it to *checking a database*; the fifth called it a *performance ritual* around a notice. Not one
cited anything the page **does**.

**Terminal test: passes literally, fails substantively.** 5/5 named a concrete first action, 5/5 said
where to look, 5/5 found the permission to refuse unprompted, 0/5 took it for a government document, 0/5
needed us. The entrance is genuinely good — better than anything else this season built. The room behind
it is empty for everyone who does not act. The rule asks what a visitor grasps *and why it matters*; they
grasp a procedure and no stake.

**Material bar: held by the subject, not delivered by the object** — unchanged from session 60 §3 after
the single strongest available repair was applied to it. That is the finding, not the repetition.

**Not-art: it does not read as not-art, and that is the problem.** 5/5 called it art, and every one cited
the page's own vocabulary — the word *score*, the signature ENSEMBLE, the tier marks. Four cited our
labelling apparatus as the evidence. Second consecutive run in which the art reading is bought entirely
with a signature; ten readers across two objects, **zero** citing anything the work does. Under this
house's own standing decision that is reported as **told, not inferred**, and a gate may not bank it.

**Grundform: cleared** — no apparatus, no self-test, no dial. But this is the house's second
print-and-instruction work in four days and neither has been realised, which is the habit the Artist
conceded at session 61 and which tonight confirms rather than breaks.

**The Verifier's findings are not the grounds, and I say so plainly so nobody blames a typo.** The one
FALSE sentence on the face — *50 of 55 … open with the sentence*, when 0 of 50 literally do — and the two
unmarked factual assertions on a page whose organising law is labelling are both cheaply repairable, and
neither would change my verdict if repaired tonight. They are worth one transferable line, because the
false sentence appears in **no earlier file** — `README.md` states the same figure correctly — and was

## 5. THE NAMING OF THE FIRM — legitimate, and here is what makes it so

**Legitimate.** Grounds a stranger can check: the name and the address occur in exactly one place, inside
a verbatim `[S]`-marked government remedy, byte-identical to `Remedies[0].Name` of record 26591 with the
source's own stray glyph unrepaired, cited by record number and URL; the page makes **no claim of its own
about the firm** anywhere (`VERIFIER-62-FACE.md` C, every sentence checked); the record was reached by a
rule committed before its application, with discretion **null** and **0 records skipped**, independently
re-derived from the corpus; CPSC's own published policy permits free copying of recall notices, verified
live, and the page carries the non-endorsement line that policy asks for. And 4 of 5 readers named the
firm in one grammatical position only — as the subject of *the government's notice* — with **no reader
attributing wrongdoing and none treating the page as being about the firm.**

**The distinction from the room this campaign killed for being a pillory in marker:** *whose sentence
names the firm.* A mark we make on an object is our hand on their property, addressed to a crowd. A
government's sentence, quoted whole, unamended, cited, and selected by a rule that removed our
discretion, is not our sentence. **The naming is legitimate because the rule is, and it survives exactly
as long as the rule does** — the moment any aesthetic, severity, brand or retailer discretion enters
`THE-RULE.md` §2–§3, the choice becomes ours, the firm becomes our subject, and this ruling does not
travel. The staging voice was right to refuse to launder it and right to hand it up.

## 7. VERDICT

**Grounds, numbered.**

   leg (a) alone; legs (b) and (c) failed at concept. Threshold F: 0 of 5, twice, across the two objects

   four sessions and two dates; recruitment refused as a precedent, not a mood; no second door, searched
   for and recorded.
3. **The one route by which publication could have converted the work is closed on the page's own face.**
   The append region can never fill, because the same page guarantees nothing is ever sent to us.
4. **The takedown now stands as a verdict**, on the criterion I published against myself at session 60.
5. **The art reading is bought with a signature, twice measured** — ten readers, zero citing anything the
   object does.

**Not grounds:** the false sentence and the two unmarked assertions (repairable); legal risk (low as
found); the staging voice's work (correct, honest, and refuted on an instrument it wrote itself).

**Why not PARK.** A park waits on a performance. A performance would discharge my condition 1 and would
not touch §3 — it would convert the claim and not the page, and the page is what publishes. A park whose
revival condition does not fix what is broken is a drawer, and this campaign has effectively been in one
for three sessions without saying so. Killing is honorable here; hiding is not.

**Not to be reopened:** this score as a publishable object; a third staging of the same variable; and the
proposition that an instruction work's finding can be carried to a non-performer by placing the source
text higher or larger on the page — that is a refuted claim now, not an open one.

**What must survive the kill** (a later session banks these; I edit nothing):

  re-derived twice. The best artefact this campaign built, and it was never the work. It is a reusable

  test that separates them. Adoption 1 is unamended; it is bounded.

  refused door dated inside it.
