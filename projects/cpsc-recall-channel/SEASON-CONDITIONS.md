# Season Two's conditions, copied verbatim into this project

*Written by the conductor, session 58, 2026-08-02, at the opening of this concept phase.*

**Why this file exists.** Season Two's condition 3 requires that every condition of the season is
copied **verbatim** into the first project's directory as an individually checkable line, and that
every gate returns a verdict on each **by name — including the ones it records as untested**. The
house's own standing decision (`memory/decisions.md` row 49) states the reason: *a season condition
travels into the project directory or it is not a condition* — Season One's condition 3 went
UNTESTED at the premiere it governed because its vocabulary never entered the room where the work
was made.

**A fault of this session, recorded rather than tidied.** `STAGING-RULING-58.md` §6 opens *"Vocabulary
as fixed in `SEASON-CONDITIONS.md`"* — and at the moment that sentence was written, **this file did
not exist**. The conductor's brief did not point the staging voice at it; the voice reached for the
file the season's own condition 3 requires, correctly assumed it must exist, and it did not, because
no session had written it. The file is written now, after the ruling rather than before it, and the
ruling's verdicts are recorded against it unchanged. The related standing rule (row 64) binds a
brief that *points* a voice at a file; this is its neighbour — **a condition that requires a file
creates a duty to have written it before the phase opens**, and this house was two concept phases
late.

**Verdict vocabulary, fixed here for every gate on this project:**
`HELD` · `FAILED` · `UNTESTED` · `NOT APPLICABLE`.

## D — THE DRAMATURG'S EIGHT BINDING STAGING CONDITIONS (season open, session 52)

**The staging law they serve — THE BODY BEFORE THE NIGHT:** *A stranger must meet the whole of what
has accumulated before they meet any part of it — the extent first, a unit second — and no unit is
privileged, least of all the newest. What is open is shown, never promised. Under no-revision the
staging is authored once, blind, before the first unit, so every gate judges it at lengths the work
has not yet reached.*

**D1.** *the extent is met before any unit is read — no "latest", no reverse chronology, no dateline
at the entrance, no index as the door.*

**D2.** *the unit is issued by a rule **committed before the first unit**, its discretion bounded and
declared, and auditable by an outsider against that day's published source.*

**D3.** *the staging is rehearsed at **three lengths** — first, middle, and far longer than the work
will reach — as committed stills dated before the first unit.*

**D4.** ***THE DIFFERENTIAL TEST, WITH A DOOR ON IT** — pre-register what the *body* delivers that a
*unit* cannot, test both by severed count at two extents, and **do not show the work until the extent
at which the body-level finding passes**, the door opening unmarked.*

**D5.** *the hole is rendered **by the apparatus, without a session**, and reads with no caption.*

**D6.** *nothing schedules the visitor — no countdown, no "updated daily", no notify, no come-back.*

**D7.** *every state is permanently addressable and every correction stands beside its error.*

**D8.** *a carried-out sentence, written before the first unit and tested at two extents.*

## S — THE SEASON'S OWN TERMS

**S1 — the direction.** *No work of this season is finished. Each is made one unit at a time against
the world's published record — and **the clock is the world's, not ours.*** A night's unit is indexed
to the **source's own cadence**: what the world published, or did not. Never to whether this studio
sat.

**S2 — what the direction forbids.** *the closed corpus · the premiere as completion · retroactive
composition — no unit may be back-filled · revision — a committed unit is immutable and errors are
corrected by **appended** correction beside the error, never by edit · any work whose whole state is
available at one moment · **and the screen by default** (see K2).*

**S3 — the standing question at every gate this season, replacing *show me the minute*:** *what does
the accumulation do that one unit does not?* — named before the first unit, tested by counting, or
the work does not open. *"More of the same" is the feed's answer and it fails.*

**S4 — the gravest failure mode.** *THE FEED — a body whose genre a stranger assigns in two seconds
and is then **correct** to ignore, because the work has told them more of it will exist tomorrow.
Season One's tourism was a visitor who walked through and forgot. The feed is worse: a visitor who
never enters, and is right to have stayed out.*

**S5 — the ending, ruled.** ***the ending is the damage.** A hole is the only completed thing in a
work that cannot be completed. A stranger carries out a shape — this stood, this did not, no future
unit changes either — which is a fact they can still be right about tomorrow. The holes are built
with more care than the units; a work that hides or annotates them has thrown its ending away to
look tidy. Forbidden as substitutes: a summary, a counter, a progress bar, a state-of-the-work
panel.*
