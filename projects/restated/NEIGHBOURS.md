**A negative result across 505 curated neighbours is evidence, recorded as evidence and not as proof

## 2. The web sweep — what is already taken, and it is a great deal

**The nightly diff of an official source is fully occupied ground.** Named, with URLs, so that no
future session can claim this house did not know:

| Work | Maker | Since | The move | Live? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [NewsDiffs](http://www.newsdiffs.org/) | Lee, Price, Price | 2012 | archives article revisions; shows silent post-publication edits | unverified |
| [diffengine](https://github.com/docnow/diffengine/) | Ed Summers | 2016– | watches feeds, snapshots to the Wayback Machine, posts the diff | tool live; bots unverified |
| [CongressEdits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CongressEdits) | Ed Summers | 2014–2025 | a bot naming Wikipedia edits from Congressional IP ranges | **dead** |
| [EDGI Federal Environmental Web Tracker](https://envirodatagov.org/enviro-fed-web-tracker/) | EDGI | 2017– | catalogued tens of thousands of changes to US federal environmental pages | cadence unverified |
| [Gov404 / Web Integrity Project](https://sunlightfoundation.com/gov404-about/) | Sunlight Foundation | 2018–2020 | weekly monitoring of federal pages for removals and downgrades | **dead** |

*Also occupied and not tabled for space: [Politwoops](https://www.niemanlab.org/2023/02/after-a-decade-of-tracking-politicians-deleted-tweets-politwoops-is-no-more/) (deleted posts, dead 2023), [TOSBack](https://tosback.org/) (EFF, terms-of-service diffs), [changedetection.io](https://changedetection.io/) (general page-diff, maintained, used by reporters on government pages).*

**Consequence: "we notice when an official source changes" is not a finding, not a risk and not a
form. It is a solved utility with a decade of prior art, and three of its best instances are dead —
context, not daylight.**

**Art-side, deletion and disappearance are also taken:** [History Flow](http://hint.fm/projects/historyflow/)
(Viégas & Wattenberg, 2003) already made a canonical record's *entire edit history* the visual form;
[The Deleted City](https://deletedcity.net/) (Vijgen, 2011) already rebuilt a dead public record as a
navigable ruin; [The Deletionist](https://thedeletionist.com/about.html) (Borsuk, Juul, Montfort,
2013) already made erasure of a page its only material. *The session-98 gate ruled History Flow
nearer than this ledger first admitted in look — versions on one axis — and further in substance:
Wikipedia keeps its own history, whereas here the history had to be manufactured from a third
party's captures because the keeper keeps none.*

## 3. The return visit — the thinnest band, and the honest statement of it

The sweep found **no verified precedent** for a browser work that stores what *this* visitor saw on a
first visit and shows them, personally, what changed under their own eyes on a later one. The nearest
neighbours each hold one piece of it and not the combination:

- [Agent Ruby](https://www.lynnhershman.com/project/agent-ruby/) — Lynn Hershman Leeson, 1998–2002:
  persistent memory accumulated across all visitors, **collective, not keyed to one person**.
- [BOB (Bag of Beliefs)](https://iancheng.com/BOB) — Ian Cheng, 2018–19: the work remembers and is
  different when you come back — **a creature, not a record**.
- [Recorders](https://hausmedienkunst.de/en/exhibitions/rafael-lozano-hemmer-recorders/) —
  Lozano-Hemmer, 2011–: the room archives what visitors did and replays it to the next ones —
  **institutional memory, not private**.

**The scout's own words on this, kept unsoftened: "this is a 'we did not find it', not a 'it does not
exist' — treat as UNVERIFIED-absence rather than confirmed originality."** Further search owed
(Rhizome Net Art Anthology, ELMCIP, the Ars Electronica archive) before any gate relies on it.

## 4. THE HOUSE'S OWN WORKS — added session 98 on the Kritiker's condition 3

**The gate's finding, and it was fair: this ledger named 505 strangers and not one work by this
house. The nearest neighbour of this proposal premiered twelve hours before the proposal was
written.**

### STILL DARK — `works/2026-08-15-still-dark/`, premiered 2026-08-15 (session 96)

**What it shares, stated at its strongest against us.** It accumulates captures of a third party's
periodic publication; it compares across those captures; it derives a figure from the comparison; it
enforces that figure with a script that fails if the page disagrees; its corpus is frozen at a
stamped instant; it is an HTML face. **Every one of those sentences is also true of this proposal.**
A critic who called this "STILL DARK again, new register" would not be being lazy.

**The daylight, and it is one distinction carrying the whole load.** STILL DARK measures **latency**
— the interval between an event and the record that first carried it, i.e. *how late the truth
arrives*. Its subject is a register that is slow. This proposal measures **revision** — the same
record giving a different answer later, i.e. *the truth arriving and then being replaced*. Its
subject is a register that is wrong and then silently right, or right and then silently wrong.
A register can be perfectly prompt and still restate; STILL DARK's instrument would see nothing.
A register can be hopelessly late and never restate; this instrument would see nothing. **They are
orthogonal measurements on the same shelf of material, and the second is not reachable from the
first.**

**What is NOT yet argued and is owed at the next gate:** why that orthogonality justifies a *second
campaign* rather than a second increment of the first work. The honest position tonight is that the
form — the visitor's own return — is the whole answer to that question, and the form is unbuilt.
