## 1. The experience — IMAGINED

You arrive on one dark band carrying a single date: 4 August 2026. Midnight to midnight, two thin
bars, nearly empty. Beneath it a counter: vessel-days of darkness this day is *currently known* to
hold. Nothing more comes. The band says the day is not finished being known and asks you to close
the page.

Later you open it again. Same date, same band. Bars now sit in the emptiness you looked at; the day
you saw is kept faint underneath, as a receipt. Each new bar carries the hour it became knowable,
after you. Nothing animates: it happened while the page was shut.

## 2. The named outside audience

The data-journalism teacher, in a seminar room, on the Monday she teaches "today's number". She puts
the band on the projector; the room sees a day with almost nothing in it, and reopens it Friday to
watch that Monday fill in. A demonstration, not an argument, that a dashboard's *today* is
structurally incomplete — plus a figure no dataset holds: how much of a day's darkness was knowable
that day.

## 3. The first encounter

Ten seconds, no label: *one day, and it is nearly empty.* The fatal risk is "still loading". Three
things author it. **(a)** No spinner, skeleton or progress bar: crisp edges read as fact, not
failure. **(b)** A counter naming its own knowing-time: provisionality sits in the number, not a
caption. **(c)** The upstream rule printed on the hole it makes: *"disabling events that ended in the
last 7 days (complete vanish-and-return stories)"* (SOURCED).

## 4. The turn, and the ending

The turn is visit 2's first second: the region you watched stay empty is occupied. Carried out:
*"I looked at one day of the ocean, and it kept filling for weeks after I stopped looking."*
**The ending is weak.** Visit 2 is recognition, not release, and the real subject — ships that never
return — never arrives.

## 5. The return, the hardest problem

**Too early:** refuse to redraw; print the elapsed time. Holding beats an unchanged screen.
**Too late:** past the 7-day window the day is closed; say so and hand over a still-open day.
**Never: I have no answer** that reliably brings a stranger back. The ask is the only interaction,
and the date is theirs. Best available (IMAGINED): count the abandonments as the work's second
figure — a serial surface, not one file.

## 6. Who disputes it inside the piece

The instrument's limits, in its voice, at the turn. On visit 2 one region never fills, and carries
the counter-argument: *"vessels that never transmit („dark by default") do not appear at all"*,
*"‚Intentional' is ML-estimated, not proof"* (SOURCED).

## 7. Pre-registered thresholds

*Five severed readers, shown only the étude, told nothing about its maker.*

1. **If 3 of 5, after visit 1, call it broken, unfinished or still loading, the concept dies** —
   authored emptiness is something I cannot stage.
2. **If 3 of 5, after visit 2, call it a live feed rather than a past day they had already seen
   incomplete, the staging is wrong.**
3. **If 4 of 5, after visit 1, would not return unprompted, the return is not staged.**
4. **If 4 of 5 cannot name, unprompted after visit 2, any limit of the measurement**, §6 is decoration.

## 8. What I would have staged differently, MARKED CONFLICT

**(i)** I would not fuzz end-times onto ten real named vessels: an IMAGINED time welded to a SOURCED
name is this house's cardinal blur. Keep names or timing, not both.
**(ii)** A visible fallback control simulating the return destroys the only real thing here: that
the change happened without you. It is a demonstration, never an equivalent.
