# DRAMATURG-71 — STILL DARK, increment 1, the sixth form, and the first with no act

*Written before any code exists. §D is the pre-registration tonight's panel is scored against.*

## §A — THE RULING'S CONSEQUENCE

The two-state apparatus dies with the act. It existed only to hold a finding back until a reader
paid for it; with no act to pay with, withholding is a curtain the house pulls for its own
benefit. One state. Nothing withheld.

What replaces the reader's turn is the record's. This work printed a law on its face — *a ceiling
that can only fall* — and tonight it fell: 79 %–100 % to 69 %–100 %, unaided, by the mechanism it
had already named, with two vessels that can be pointed at by name and date. That is a turn with a
before, an after, an agent and a consequence, and it does not need a reader's consent. It is also
the only turn this material has ever actually produced; the five we built were inventions laid on
top of it. The screen's job is now to make the fall legible **as measured**, not to stage it.

## §B — THE FORM

One screen, no scroll, no interaction, no state. Top to bottom:

1. **`4 AUGUST 2026`**, fixed, largest thing on the page. One line under it: *one day of the sea,
   and how much of it was knowable on the day itself.*
2. **Upstream's method quote, verbatim**, small, attributed — placed as the *cause* of everything
   below, not as a caveat beside it. One house sentence follows: *so a day of the sea is nearly
   empty on the day itself, and keeps filling for weeks.* Cause, then consequence, before any
   number. This is the cold visitor's minute.
3. **THE FIELD** — the largest object. Sixteen bars on a 2 Jun–6 Aug field, crossed by a labelled
   rule at 4 AUG, both ends of every band hatched seven days wide. Eleven bars in full ink: the
   edition of 4 August. Five in a second treatment, each stamped at the right with its arrival —
   `+0–1 d` (SOUTHERN SEAS NO.302, RICKY, ALTAR 10) and `+0–2 d` (ALBACORA CUATRO, BONAMI). Row
   text unchanged: SOURCED `name · flag · days dark · waters`, DERIVED the two-ended date line,
   OBSERVED `first seen …`. Three-line tier legend stays, where the eye is.
4. **THE FALL** — one block, the only place a percentage stands above the verbatim:
   `11 of 0–14 · 79 %–100 %` struck, `11 of 0–16 · 69 %–100 %` standing. Three short lines beside
   it: what did not move (11, and why no later night can ever move it); what moved (the
   denominator, by two named ships, two days after the day); and the sentence this work published
   **before** the fall, quoted with the night it was printed. Prediction, then fulfilment, in that
   order on the page.
5. **`verbatim, unedited`** — the `day.py 2026-08-04` output and the command that makes it, so the
   block above is re-runnable by anyone.
6. **OBSERVED — the ledger**, five rows: fetched · status · bytes · body sha256 · content ·
   edition · vessels. Caption cut to one clause: *5 captures, 3 editions, 3 contents, 4 bodies — a
   raw body hash is not an edition's identity; the content column is.*
7. **The floor.** *No number closes this. A method that counts a disappearance only when the ship
   comes back cannot see the ships that never come back. 16 is what this record can place in
   4 August 2026, not what was on the sea that day.* Then the two source and restraint lines.

**CUT, named:** the question, the instruction line, the keypad, the WRITE IT IN button, the YOURS
privacy line, the `YOU WROTE n` line — all with the act. Also cut: both state classes and every
rule that depended on them; the JSON-island disclosure sentence (*"state 1 is a state of this
reading"*) — nothing is withheld now, so its disclosure discloses nothing; the "two captures added
nothing" paragraph and the body-hash-drift paragraph, compressed into the ledger caption; the
render script's typed `11`; `STATE-2.txt`.

## §C — THE ONE RISK AGAINST MY OWN FORM

A cold reader meets both figures **in the same instant, on one surface**. They never saw
79 %–100 % published, never held it, are not surprised by its loss. A struck-through number beside
a standing one is the oldest layout in advertising, and the likeliest reading is that this house
chose a flattering before and a humble after and printed them together — the fall as rhetoric, not
as event. The measurement is real and every piece of its evidence is on the page, but evidence that
arrives *simultaneously with* the claim it supports reads as illustration. If the panel says some
version of *it tells me the number changed*, without reaching the denominator or naming a capture,
this form has died the way the others did: honest, complete, and inert.

## §D — THE PRE-REGISTERED PANEL

Three fresh severed readers, rendered text only, no interaction, no tool. Questions verbatim.

**Q1.** *"In two or three sentences: what does this page show?"*
**3 of 3** must name both (a) ships or vessels that went dark / switched off tracking, and (b) the
single named day 4 August 2026. Naming only one of the two is a miss.

**Q2.** *"This page prints two figures for the same day: 79 %–100 % and 69 %–100 %. Why is there a
second figure, and what does the page say made it change?"*
**2 of 3** must give **both**: (a) that vessels were added to the day by a later edition — the
denominator grew while the eleven stayed eleven (any wording of that asymmetry counts); and (b) at
least one specific printed piece of evidence, from: a named late vessel (ALBACORA CUATRO or
BONAMI), an arrival stamp, a capture timestamp, the ledger, or the verbatim command block. "The
number went down" or "more data came in" alone is a miss.

**Q3.** *"This page marks three kinds of statement: SOURCED, DERIVED, OBSERVED. Say which is
which: (1) `BONAMI · KOR · 22 d dark`; (2) the two-ended date line printed under BONAMI (`dark …
→ back …`); (3) `first seen 2026-08-06T08:16:42Z`."*
**2 of 3** must place all three correctly (1 SOURCED, 2 DERIVED, 3 OBSERVED) **in one answer** — no
partial credit. **Hard clause:** if any reader calls `22 d dark` DERIVED, Q3 fails regardless of
the count.

**Q4.** *"After reading this page, what does it say is not known about 4 August 2026?"*
**2 of 3** must give at least one of: ships that never came back are invisible to the method; the
count is a floor, not a total; the share can fall again on later nights. Answering only that the
end dates are uncertain is a **miss** — that is uncertainty inside the record, not the record's
blindness.

**VOID CLAUSE.** If any reader states that the two figures cannot both be true, or that the page
contradicts or corrects itself, the form is presumed dead on arrival and **no threshold is
claimed** on any question. The fall is the whole object; read as an erratum, it is not a turn. The
70 void does not carry: no question tonight asks what the screen can be totalled into.
