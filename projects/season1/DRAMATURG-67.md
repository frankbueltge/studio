# DRAMATURG-67 — STILL DARK, étude 2, as staged

*Written blind to the Builder, on the strong tier. The four thresholds in §5 were pre-registered
here, before the étude existed, and were not touched after the panel was dispatched.*

*Day staged: **20 July 2026**. All eleven vessels of the 4 Aug edition (D ≥ 16 d, end ∈ [Jul 28, Aug 4]) were certainly dark on it; none could be in its record that day, since end ≤ Jul 20 is impossible. DERIVED.*

## 1. THE STAGING

**t=0, one screen, no scroll.** Top left, large and fixed: `20 JULY 2026`. Beneath it a ruled field sixteen days wide, **genuinely empty** — no slots, no rows, no dashes. Under it a rail, handle at far left, carrying a smaller date: `record as it stood on 20 July 2026`. Two dates, one movable. Otherwise, the source line.

**The visitor drags right.** The handle's date advances; the day does not. **Nothing appears. For eight days of travel, nothing appears.** That dead travel is the mechanism: latency paid by the visitor's hand.

At `28 July` eleven rows appear at once, hollow; at `4 August` they go solid. Hollow = may already be recorded, solid = certainly recorded: that two-state geometry *is* the printed 7-day band. Never a fade.

**The turn** is that instant. Each solidifying row draws its dark interval **backwards** out of its arrival, across the field just crossed. Eleven bars run under 20 July; TUNAMAR's leaves the left edge and never ends. The emptiness was not absence but arrears. Nothing says the day was incomplete: they watch a finished past day acquire eleven ships while its date never changes.

**Ending, one scroll down:** same strip, same rules, dated `5 AUGUST 2026`. Empty, handle at the far left of a rail with no track to its right.

## 2. WHAT I CUT

- **The return visit, entirely.** Nothing deferred to a second sitting: it fired T3, and a demoted mechanism still on screen is a mechanism.
- **Ghost rows, placeholders, "not yet knowable —", skeletons, spinners.** Absence asserted by typography is not absence produced.
- **Every caption that states the conclusion**, and every invented per-vessel time.
- **Autoplay** — a self-filling strip lets the visitor watch waiting instead of doing it.
- **Map, coordinates, the 3,712-vessel-days hero number.**

## 3. THE FIRST ENCOUNTER

*One named day, one empty field, one handle*: a specific past day holding nothing, and what moves is not the day.

## 4. THE ENDING

*"A day already over filled up in my hands — and today looks exactly like the one I started with."*

## 5. THE PRE-REGISTERED THRESHOLDS

*Five severed readers see rendered text of four states in order: handle at 20 Jul, 31 Jul, 4 Aug (bars drawn), then the 5 Aug strip.*

**T1 — if 3 of 5 answer (b) or (c), the concept dies.**
*"After the first screen only: what is this screen doing?"* (a) showing a day that holds nothing (b) still loading (c) broken (d) a form to fill in.

**T2 — [C2 re-run, no caption] — if 3 of 5 answer other than (c), the staging is wrong.**
*"Across these screens, what changed?"* (a) the date shown moved forward (b) new ships went dark while I watched (c) the day stayed the same; what was known about it grew (d) a live feed updated.

**T3 — if 3 of 5 answer (a) or (d), the mechanism fails.**
*"Why did the field hold nothing at first?"* (a) no ships were dark then (b) those ships hadn't come back yet, so nothing could be recorded (c) the maker hadn't collected the data (d) a rendering error.

**T4 — if 3 of 5 answer other than (b), the ending fails.**
*"The last screen is dated today and is empty. What does that tell you about today?"* (a) nothing happened (b) today has no record yet and may fill later like the other day (c) the piece ended (d) the sea is quiet.

## 6. MY OWN WEAKEST JOINT

The two dates. Everything rests on reading the handle as *when I am asking* and the heading as *what I am asking about*. Collapse them and the drag is calendar-scrubbing: a dull timeline with a late spike. Typography and one control label defend it — thin instruments. And the dead travel is deliberate nothing, a stranger's tolerance for which is not mine to control.
