# PANEL-66 — five severed readers, on the built étude

*Dispatched the same night the étude was built and frozen. Each reader was shown the étude's two
screens as plain text, in order, told nothing about who made it, forbidden to read anything else,
and instructed not to revise stage-1 answers after seeing screen two. The reviewer controls were
stripped from what they saw. Not one threshold was touched after dispatch: they are
`DRAMATURG-66.md` §7, written before the étude existed. Tier: this file reports measurements, not
claims about the world.*

**The instrument's own limit, printed before the numbers.** These readers did not leave and come
back. They read screen two minutes after screen one, in one sitting. The étude's subject is a
delay a person lives through; **no reader in this panel lived through it**, and nothing below
measures whether a real return works. This is a limit of the instrument, not of the readers.

**A defect in the run, printed rather than smoothed: reader C returned no answers to Q1–Q3.** Only
the stage-2 answers came back. Thresholds 1 and 3 therefore have **n = 4**, and both are scored
below on what that permits, not on what we would prefer.

## The four thresholds

| # | as pre-registered | returned | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1** | if **3 of 5** call it broken, unfinished or still loading after screen one, **the concept dies** | **0 of 4** — all four said `WORKING AS INTENDED` | **passes**, and would pass on any answer C could have given |
| **T2** | if **3 of 5** call it a live feed rather than a past day they had already seen incomplete, the staging is wrong | **0 of 5** — all five chose *"the same past day, which was incomplete when I first saw it"* | **passes at maximum — and see the finding below** |
| **T3** | if **4 of 5** would not return unprompted after screen one, the return is not staged | **4 of 4**: one `NO`, three `MAYBE`, each conditioned on chance recall | **FIRES** — 4 is the trigger, and C cannot lower it |
| **T4** | if **4 of 5** cannot name any limit of the measurement, the counter-argument is decoration | **5 of 5** named one unprompted | **passes** |

**Scoring ruling, made with only readers A and B visible and recorded before C, D and E were
seen:** a `MAYBE` conditioned on chance recall counts as *would not return unprompted*, because the
threshold asks whether the **work** stages the return, and a return that depends on the visitor
happening to remember is staged by nothing. The reading that favours us was available and refused.

## Two findings the thresholds do not carry

**1. T2 passed on a caption, not on an experience.** Four of the five readers who chose the right
answer quoted the page's own sentence back as their reason — *"the page explicitly says 'This is
not a new day, and this is not a live feed'"* (A), *"the page says so directly"* (C), and B and E
in the same words. The étude told them the answer and then measured whether they had read it. **A
threshold that a caption can pass is a threshold about the caption.** The staging voice's own bar —
what a cold visitor grasps *without* a wall label — is not what T2 measured.

**2. A severed reader named the blur, unprompted, as the work's weakness.** Reader C at Q6, asked
for a limit of the *measurement*, answered instead about us: the `+Nd` stamps are *"the studio's own
invented/seeded construction layered on top of the real … data, which blurs what's actually
measured versus fabricated for effect."* The marking was on the face of the étude, in the reader's
own path, and the reader read it — **and still called it blur.** That is `DRAMATURG-66.md` §8(i),
which was written blind to this panel and to the built file, corroborated from outside the house.

## What the other readers said the measurement cannot see

Four of five, unprompted, arrived at the same structural limit: a method that counts a
disappearance only when the ship comes back **cannot see the ships that never come back**. In
reader D's words, it *"likely undercounts the most concerning cases."* That is the Artist's own
declared weakest joint, found from outside in one sitting by four strangers.
