MEMO — DRAMATURG, still-dark, night of 8 August

**§1 FIRST ENCOUNTER**

The head is not broken in its order. It is broken in its referents. Both arrangements failed because both put the same lede first, and that lede is two sentences containing four things the reader has not been given: *this record*, *can place in*, *the list dated 4 August*, *its figure*. A reader arriving cold is asked to hold a record, a list, a placement operation and a prior publication of a number, none of which exist yet. That is why hoisting the definition changed nothing: you moved a good sentence over a wall the reader still has to climb. And it is exactly why the control reader summarised the page as "a record of ships for August 4, 2026" — that is a faithful reading of the lede with the unresolved terms stripped out. She did not fail. The sentence told her that.

Cuts at the top, none of them a reorder:

- "the ships this record can place in 4 August 2026" — *can place in* is the page's private verb, load-bearing and unearned in line one. Cut it here; it survives fine at line 188 where the reader has the machinery.
- "one of them after this page had printed its figure" — a forward reference to material 1,400 pixels down. It buys a frisson at the cost of the one referent the reader cannot spare. Move it to sit against the SUPERSEDED figure, where it detonates.
- The method-sheet quotation opening the third block. It defines going dark, and the very next sentence defines going dark again, better, in the page's own voice. Two definitions nose to tail, the borrowed one first. Cut the quotation. This is not the tested restaging: nothing moves.
- "counts only disabling its own source classifies as high-confidence and intentional" — a garden path with two elided relatives. Nobody parses that on first pass. Rewrite or cut the clause; the 12h/50nm thresholds carry it alone.
- The title is a date. The sub-line is the only place the subject appears, in abstraction ("knowable"), and it is repeated verbatim at line 140. The first encounter offers no ship, no switch, no dark.
