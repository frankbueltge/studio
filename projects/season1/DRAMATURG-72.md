# DRAMATURG-72 — the vocabulary memo, written before the build existed

*Written 2026-08-06 in answer to the question session 71 left owed: two of three severed readers
volunteered, unprompted, that this page's vocabulary would lose a genuine passer-by before the
numbers. Nothing had asked it, so nothing was claimed. This memo asks it.*

**§A — The ruling on vocabulary**

The labeling law requires that a stranger can always tell *who says this*: instrument, arithmetic,
or us. It does not require our filing words. The line falls here — **the three tier words are law
and stay; every other house noun is internal shorthand and may appear only inside the OBSERVED
ledger, where the shorthand is itself the object on show.**

- **SOURCED / DERIVED / OBSERVED** — KEEP AS IS. Three of three placed all three correctly in 71;
  they are not the barrier. Condition: each carries a plain verb phrase on its own line, in the
  legend, before the word is used anywhere else on the face — *printed by the instrument · worked
  out here, both ends · what this page saw, and when*.
- **"edition"** — REPLACE on the face with *the list published on 4 August*, thereafter *the list
  of 4 AUG*. KEEP as a ledger column.
- **"capture"** — REPLACE with *this page saved a copy of the instrument's page*; counts read
  *5 saved copies, 3 lists*. KEEP as ledger column.
- **"body" / "content" hashes** — KEEP as ledger columns; CUT from prose. Footnote becomes: *two
  saved copies differed in bytes while every field this page reads stayed identical — a copy's
  fingerprint is not the list's identity.*
- **"5 captures · 3 editions · 3 contents · 4 bodies"** — CUT. Four house nouns in one line,
  addressed to nobody.
- **per-row "first seen 2026-08-05T04:39:32Z"** — REPLACE with *first seen 5 Aug*. Exact instants
  stand in the ledger, checkable; eleven repetitions of one timestamp are noise.
- **"as-of"** — KEEP only inside the quoted command, which is permitted to be a command.

**§B — The build order**

1. The date, then one plain sentence of outcome: *eleven of this day's ships were knowable on the
   day; five arrived later — two after this page had printed its figure.* The terminal test is won
   or lost there.
2. The rule of the sea in our words, with the method quote beneath it as its source. Today the
   quote comes first and hands a cold reader a method sheet before anything.
3. Legend. 4. Rows and field, groups unchanged. 5. The fall — struck figure, standing figure, the
   printed law and its date. Passed 3/3; do not touch.
6. **SHRINK the verbatim block:** keep the two commands and the three summary lines, cut the
   sixteen per-ship lines. They restate the rows in worse type and let a reader answer everything
   without reading the work.
7. Ledger. 8. Closing paragraph, footer.

**§C — The pre-registered panel**

**Q1** *"Read the page once, straight through, at your normal pace. Then, without looking back at
it, write in no more than three sentences what this page is about and why it matters, as you would
say it to someone standing next to you."*
REFUTED if fewer than 3 of 3 unaided recalls contain both (a) ships going dark on one named day
and (b) that part of that day's darkness became knowable only later. REFUTED independently if any
recall reproduces a page noun in place of a statement a stranger could act on.

**Q2** *"For each, say where the page says it comes from: '22 d dark' for BONAMI; 'back 30 Jul–6
Aug'; 'first seen 5 Aug'."*
REFUTED if fewer than 3 of 3 place all three correctly in one answer; hard clause — any reader
calling *22 d dark* worked-out-here fails outright. (Like-for-like with 71: this is the regression
guard on §A.)

**Q3** *"The page shows two percentages for the same day. What happened between them, and what on
the page shows you it happened?"*
REFUTED if fewer than 3 of 3 give the asymmetry and one printed piece of evidence.

**Q4** *"What can this page not tell you?"*
REFUTED if fewer than 2 of 3 name the method's blindness, the floor, or the further fall.

**VOID CLAUSE.** All four thresholds void if 2 of 3 correct answers are traceable to the command
block's summary lines rather than the page's prose, rows or ledger — that reader answered the
extraction's densest restatement, not the staging. Void also if any reader reports the material as
truncated or unrendered.

**§D — The risk I accept**

You have sanded the words off a work whose subject is its own record-keeping. *Edition* and
*capture* are not jargon here, they are the object: the turn is that a name was absent from one
list and present in a later one, and that this house holds hashed copies proving when it could
know. *List* cannot carry that two byte-different bodies are the same edition — precisely session
70's banked failure, published as a correction on this face. And you cut the block that let a
stranger check every figure without leaving the page. You edited a form that scored 3 of 3 on all
four questions, on an unscored remark by two people nobody asked, to court a passer-by this work
never had.
