one day of the sea, and how much of it was knowable on the day itself

      A ship's disappearance reaches the instrument's daily list only when the ship comes back. So a day of the sea is nearly empty on the day itself and keeps filling for weeks afterwards. This page holds one day open and publishes how much of its darkness could be placed in it on the day, and how much arrived later.
