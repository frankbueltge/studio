**What that does to the composition, which is the reason this is blocking and not a note.** I drove the run and looked at it. It is the subject, staged, not illustrated. And directly on its lower edge stands a nine-line wall of the brightest text on the page. The emptiness is not open; it is a gap between two blocks, and the lower block is heavier than the upper one.

**And it is live in a way no eye can reach.** Its box is **109,855 px² at 390** — the largest element in the head by a factor of seven. That is the definition of decorative motion: a node the instruments book as moving and a visitor cannot see move.

**Its trajectory, which is the part that makes it mine and not a fact voice's.** 75 px at 390 (session 92) → 150 px (session 93, `DRAMATURG-93` recorded the growth as the price of a landing) → **318 px tonight**. Tripled in three sessions, while three gates cut thirteen words here and a sentence there.

**What must change.** Not which sentences it owes — a fact voice ordered the fourth clause and I do not unbuild another voice's condition, that ruling is `DRAMATURG-93` cut 5 and it is still good. **I rule on its rank.** The caveat comes into the register this page already has for everything that talks about the material — 55 % ink, and not larger than the premise — and its 148 words come down to what only it can say. Its last clause prints `11 of 29` in words; `#sd-arrive-frac-fixed` prints `11 of 29` three hundred pixels above it, in the head, at the beat where it changes. That is the fourth printing of one number and it is the one printing nobody reads.

**Testable:** `getComputedStyle` on `#sd-arrive-hedge` — font-size not greater than `#sd-arrive-gloss`'s, colour at this page's commentary alpha; and its height at 1400×900 not greater than the reserved height of `#sd-arrive-names-since`.
