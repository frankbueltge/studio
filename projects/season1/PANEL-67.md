# PANEL-67 — five severed readers, on étude 2

*Dispatched the night the étude was built. Each reader was shown the page's four rendered states in
order, told nothing about who made it, forbidden to use any tool, and instructed not to revise the
stage-1 answer after seeing the later states. Not one threshold was touched after dispatch: they are
`DRAMATURG-67.md` §5, written before the étude existed. All five returned complete answers.*

## The instrument's own defect, printed before the numbers — and it is ours

The étude is a **picture with one moving control**. The readers were shown **text**, and where the
page has geometry, my dispatch had to describe it: *"[ an empty ruled field, sixteen days wide,
holding nothing ]"* and *"[ rail: handle at the far left, with no track to the right of it ]"*.
**Those words are mine, not the page's.**

Reader E answered Q1 by quoting them back: *"the labels ('empty ruled field, holding nothing') …
read as a designed state, not an error."* That is last night's defect exactly — a threshold passed
on a caption — **relocated from the work into the measuring instrument.** The house voided a passing
threshold for this last night; it does not get to keep one tonight because the caption was its own.

- **T1 — VOID.** Its two decisive words were written by us. Whether an empty field reads as broken
  is not answered by telling a reader it is a field.
- **T4 — passes, qualified.** *"no track to the right of it"* is also my description; the readers'
  reasons rest additionally on the page's own dates and on the mirroring, which are the page's.
- **T2 and T3 — pass unqualified.** Every reason given quotes text the page actually prints: the
  fixed heading `20 JULY 2026`, the row labels `possibly recorded` / `recorded`, the band
  `back 28 Jul – 4 Aug`, and the method sentence.

## The four thresholds

| # | as pre-registered | returned | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1** | if **3 of 5** answer *still loading* or *broken*, the concept dies | **0 of 5** — all five answered *a day that holds nothing* | **VOID** (see above), not passed |

| **T3** | if **3 of 5** answer *no ships were dark then* or *a rendering error*, the mechanism fails | **0 of 5** — all five: *those ships hadn't come back yet, so nothing could be recorded* | **passes** |

**What C2 asked and what came back.** Last night's page printed *"this is not a live feed"* and then
measured whether readers had read it. **Tonight the page prints no such sentence, and five of five
still chose the past day over the live feed.** The option *"a live feed updated"* was on the card and
nobody took it.

## Two findings the thresholds do not carry

**1. Every reader, unprompted, called the first encounter opaque.** Reader C: an empty field with no
hint that data arrives retroactively *"looks exactly like a loading or broken state."* Reader D: *"a
first-time visitor could easily read stage 1 as a broken/empty widget."* Reader A: the mechanism
*"only became clear by comparing all four states side by side."* Reader E proposed the repair the
staging voice explicitly forbids — *"a single line like 'drag to replay how this day's record filled
in over time'"*. **Five readers chose the right answers and five readers said they had to work for
them.** The thresholds measure the arrival; they do not measure the cost of the journey, and the cost
was named five times out of five.

**2. The blindness was found again, from outside, in one sitting.** All five named it at Q6, in their
own words: a method that records a disappearance only when the ship returns cannot see the ships that
never return. Reader B: *"it likely undercounts actual disappearances."* Reader E: *"a lower bound,
not a complete tally."* That is the Artist's declared weakest joint, corroborated by ten strangers
across two nights.
