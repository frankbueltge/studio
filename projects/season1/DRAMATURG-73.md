## §A — The change

**One change tonight: the lede rewritten so the day is inside it.**

Computed slots in braces; nothing in braces may be typed.

> **Of the ships this record can place in {4 August 2026}, {eleven} stood in the list dated
> {4 August} itself. {Five} arrived later — {two} of them after this page had printed its figure.**

- `{4 August 2026}` — `printed_date(DAY)`. `{4 August}` — day and month from `DAY`, no year: add the
  helper, do not type the string.
- `{eleven}` — `word(n_obs)`, **lower case**, not sentence-initial. `{Five}` —
  `word(n_hi - n_obs)`, capitalised. `{two}` — `word(len(gained))`.

The rest is fixed text, carrying no claim the old sentence did not. *Dated* stands; *published on
the day itself* appears nowhere.

**Why this wording.** Not deleting a pronoun but putting the date where unaided recall keeps things —
the opening of the load-bearing sentence, then again in the same breath. No anaphor is left in it.
Rejected: *enlarging the headline* — a headline is what recall drops first, which is the finding,
not a fix; *naming the day in the second paragraph* — a reader who stops after sentence one must
still carry the day; that reader is the terminal test; *swapping "that day" for the date in
place* — it then lands mid-sentence, behind a number, in the weakest position. Moving `{eleven}` off
the front costs nothing: Q1 never scored the count.

**No second change is ordered.** Two staged changes would make tonight's Q1 result unattributable: a
pass could belong to either. The object already carries one non-staged delta, the TUNAMAR waters
repair — accuracy, not staging; **no threshold may be claimed for it**.

## §C — The panel: questions, thresholds, void clause

Three severed readers, the browser's own extraction and nothing else, no interaction, no tool.
**Nothing sent to them — question, framing or covering note — may contain the date 4 August in any
form.**

**Q1** *(the repeat)* — *"Read the page once, straight through, at your normal pace. Then, without
looking back at it, write in no more than three sentences what this page is about and why it
matters, as you would say it to someone standing next to you."*
**REFUTED if fewer than 3 of 3** unaided recalls contain both **(a)** ships dark on one named day,
the day given as a calendar date, and **(b)** that part of that day's darkness became knowable only
later. *One day* or *that day* does not satisfy (a). **REFUTED independently** if any recall states
the page shows the list *published* on the day itself — the date would then have been bought with a
claim this record cannot make.

**Q2** *"For each, say where the page says it comes from: '22 d dark' for BONAMI; 'back 30 Jul–6
Aug'; 'first seen 5 AUG'."*
**REFUTED if fewer than 3 of 3** place all three correctly in one answer. Hard clause: any reader
calling *22 d dark* worked-out-here fails outright.

**Q3** *"The page shows two percentages for the same day. What happened between them, and what on
the page shows you it happened?"*
**REFUTED if fewer than 3 of 3** give the asymmetry — numerator fixed, total grown — **and** one
printed piece of evidence.

**Q4** *"What can this page not tell you?"*
**REFUTED if fewer than 2 of 3** name the method's blindness, the floor, or the further fall.

**VOID CLAUSE.** All four results are thrown out if **any** of: a reader reports the material
truncated, unrendered or missing a section; 2 of 3 correct answers trace to the command block's
summary lines rather than the prose, rows or ledger; the extraction sent out does not carry the §A
lede verbatim in its computed form; or the date appears in the panel's own instructions.

## §D — What a refutation would mean

If Q1 falls again with the day named twice in the first sentence, the diagnosis was wrong, and two
alternatives are named now so tomorrow cannot invent a third. **One:** unaided recall of a text
extraction keeps mechanism and drops particulars wherever they sit — Q1 then measures recall, not
this page, and the threshold is what must go. **Two:** the day competes not with silence but with a
crowd — 5 AUG, 6 AUG, the list dates, the first-seen dates, ten weekly gradations — and one more
mention cannot make it salient; the owed change is then **subtraction of competing dates, not
addition of ours.** We commit now: two is tested first, by cutting. If that fails too, Q1's
threshold is retired as unmeasurable and this house stops claiming a stranger carries the day away.
