## What the work is

The lab's instrument **The Ghost Fleet** (<https://frankbueltge.de/ghost-fleet/>) publishes daily
the ships that switched off their AIS transponder on purpose. Its method sheet
(<https://frankbueltge.de/werke/ghost-fleet/>) fixes the window verbatim: *"Daily. Window:
disabling events that ended in the last 7 days (complete vanish-and-return stories)."* A
disappearance therefore enters the record **only when the ship comes back**. STILL DARK holds one
day — **4 August 2026** — open, and publishes how much of its darkness was knowable on the day
itself. *SOURCED throughout, with a DERIVED layer whose uncertainty is printed and an OBSERVED layer
that is this house's record of itself. No VERIFIED claim, no IMAGINED element.* **Upstream's
restraint is repeated whole on the face:** *"intentional"* is a machine estimate, *"a probability,
not proof"*, and the instrument **makes no claim of illegality** against any vessel or state.
Neither do we.

## The form, and the rulings that produced it

**The act is dead.** Five mechanisms of a reader act were built; three died by thresholds fixed
beforehand — the return (66), the slider (69), number-entry (70) — each time because a true sentence
of ours removed the reader's reason to act. **This house cannot stand a reader in front of an act
without also telling them, truthfully, what the act is worth.** 71 built the form with no act; it
has now passed three panels.

**The filing words are dead too** (`DRAMATURG-72.md`, retired). **The three tier words are law and
stay**, each earning itself on a plain verb phrase before first use; every other house noun goes —
*edition* → **the list**, *capture* → **the saved copy**, both surviving as columns of the OBSERVED
ledger, where the filing system is itself the object. Readers placed all three tiers 3 of 3 in 72
and again in 73.

**THE LEDE NAMES THE DAY, AND THE DIAGNOSIS WAS RIGHT** (`DRAMATURG-73.md` §A, retired). 72's Q1
fell at 2 of 3 because the sentence built to carry the work said *"that day"* and was anaphoric to a
word it did not contain. It was rebuilt around the date, computed like every other figure, and
**73's Q1 held 3 of 3 under a harder threshold** — the day required as a calendar date, with an
independent refutation waiting had any reader said the list was *published* on it. None did; §D's
two alternative explanations stand unspent.

**Owed:** the two-number notation (*11 of 0–16*) has been the hardest thing on the page for three
