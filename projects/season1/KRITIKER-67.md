## On the object

Not Spielerei. The negative affordance is new: a control that does nothing for half its travel, and
the nothing is the argument. Eight days of dead drag, then eleven rows at once, hollow, then solid.
Nothing fades; nothing pretends.

Weaker than staged. `DRAMATURG-67` §1 promised *"TUNAMAR's leaves the left edge and never ends."* In
the build every row carries `sd2-runoff`, `sd2-certain` 2–11, `sd2-band` 11–18: **eleven bars,
identical, 56 days and 16 days drawn the same width.** Inside a sixteen-day field that is honest —
the window truncates them all — but the picture therefore carries no quantity; the only variation
sits in the text beside it. A chart in which nothing varies is a diagram of a definition, not a
measurement.

## The opacity finding

Not a defect, and not free. The dead travel *is* the latency; annotating it away would be last
night's caption again, and Reader E's repair is correctly refused. But the panel did not measure what
five readers named: they held four states stacked and could compare; a cold visitor holds a handle at
0 with no evidence that moving it repays. The thresholds measured arrival, not the journey.
**Opacity earned; abandonment unmeasured.**

**The T1 void was right, and too lenient elsewhere.** T4 rests on a house-written phrase too and was
merely qualified. A page whose argument is geometry cannot be panelled as prose without the house
writing the geometry. T2 and T3 quote the page's own words and stand — but "unqualified" is a word
this instrument has not earned.

## My ridicule: relocated, then mostly discharged

Nine rows of *"not yet knowable —"* are gone; before 28 July the DOM holds no rows at all, and the
5 August strip is empty because the rule just learned makes it empty. Absence is now produced. It
relocated once — eleven identical rows solidifying in one instant — but that simultaneity is the
window's, not the typographer's, so it survives.

**What I ridicule tonight:** *a rail that pays out eleven identical bars, on a work whose whole claim
is a quantity, with one night of captures on disk and the instrument itself answering "not yet
measurable."*

## Against myself

Last night I bet my name on the day-strip. Tonight I again license a repair I have not seen measured:
production opens on an object whose only tested surface is a transcript of itself, and all three of my
conditions fall due after the thing is built. If the premiere ships eleven identical bars and no
OBSERVED number, the failed forecast is mine, twice dated.
