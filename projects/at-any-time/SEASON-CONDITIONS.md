what has accumulated before they meet any part of it — the extent first, a unit second — and no unit is
privileged, least of all the newest. What is open is shown, never promised. Under no-revision the staging
is authored once, blind, before the first unit, so every gate judges it at lengths the work has not yet
reached.*
