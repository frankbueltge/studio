# THE RULE — the canonical text

*The issuing rule of *AT ANY TIME*, in one file, because a rule that lives inside a proposal is a rule
nobody can check. Season Two's staging condition 2 requires the rule to be **committed before the first
unit**, its discretion **bounded and declared**, and **auditable by an outsider against that day's
published source**. This is that commitment. Nothing here is in force: **no unit has been issued and the
concept has not passed its gate.***

**Status: DRAFT AT CONCEPT, session 54 (2026-07-31). Two clauses are marked OWED and the work cannot
open until they are written — see §4.**

---

## §1. The base rule (the Artist, session 53 — unchanged)

> Once each calendar day, read the Court's own orders index for the current Term
> (<https://www.supremecourt.gov/orders/ordersofthecourt/25>, and from the first Monday in October 2026
> the index for the 2026 Term). Take every row on that index whose date is that day **and whose type, as
> printed by the Court, is "Miscellaneous Order."** Order Lists are not taken. Rules documents are not
> taken. For each row taken, fetch the linked PDF, record its URL, byte length and SHA-256, and render
> its pages entire — unaltered, uncropped, unrotated, nothing removed and nothing added — at the work's
> one committed scale. Place them at that day's page-height, in the index's own order. A day with no
> Miscellaneous Order on the index carries nothing.

**Audit, demonstrated rather than promised.** The conductor re-derived the whole channel from that index
tonight with one regular expression: **104 rows — 72 Miscellaneous Orders on 55 distinct dates, 29 Order
Lists, 3 rules documents** — matching the corpus built last session from the downloaded documents, by a
different route. The type label the rule keys on is printed by the Court **in the link text itself**, so
the selection contains no judgement of ours: it is a string comparison against the Court's own word.

## §2. Amendment 1 — THE START CLAUSE (Dramaturg, session 53; adopted)

> **The work's first day is the first day the channel publishes.**

Without it there was an **81.4 %** chance the permanent entrance was blank, unfixable under no-revision.
It is not back-fill and not selection: it indexes the work's beginning to the source as tightly as
everything else. **Demonstrated tonight:** the three entrance stills open on 6 October 2025 carrying a
sheet.

## §3. Amendment 2 — THE LATE-TRANSCRIPTION CLAUSE (conductor, session 54; adopted)

The daily read runs on best-effort infrastructure whose own documentation says queued jobs *"may be
dropped"*. A dropped run on a day the Court published would render that day as a hole — and in a work
whose blanks are load-bearing, that is not degradation, it is **the work stating something false about
the Court**.

> **Every run re-reads the whole of the current Term's index, not only the current day.** A day whose
> slot is empty in the work but carries rows on the Court's index is written at the next run — the
> content taken from the source's own dated row, unchanged — and **that slot is marked, permanently, as
> written late, with the date it was written.** A day's content is always the source's; only the
> transcription can be late, and a late transcription says so on its face.

*(The deliberately ugly option. The tidy alternative — filling the day in silently — produces a work
whose holes cannot be trusted, which is four fifths of the work. **Flagged to the gate:** an idempotent
whole-index re-read has the shape of the diff-and-watch that season condition K1 kills, and the gate is
asked to rule that the internal read is not the forbidden thing rather than to assume it.)*

## §4. TWO CLAUSES OWED — the work cannot open without them

Both were invisible in prose and became visible the moment the thing was built.

**(a) THE MULTI-ORDER DAY.** 11 of 55 unit-days — **20 %** — carry more than one Miscellaneous Order;
2026-05-21 carries five. The base rule says *"place them at that day's page-height"*, and a page-height
holds one sheet. The étude implemented a squeeze — five sheets into 223 px each — which **violates this
rule's own word `unaltered`**. Docket item **A7**. No option is adopted here; the gate rules.

**(b) THE MULTI-PAGE DOCUMENT.** 2 of 72 documents run to two pages (both 25A539, the emergency
SNAP-funding case, 7 and 10 November 2025) — **3.6 % of unit-days**. The rule says *"render its **pages**
entire"*, plural, and the model says one page-height per day. Those two sentences cannot both hold on
those two days. Docket item **8**, measured and closed as a fact; the consequence is open.

## §5. The bounded discretion, declared

Every decision this work contains, made before unit 1 and never again: **(i)** the hour of the daily read;
**(ii)** the render scale; **(iii)** the ground; **(iv)** which Term index is read — dictated by the
Court's own numbering; **(v)** the two clauses of §4, once written. **There is no selection.** Every
miscellaneous order enters — the bar-discipline orders, the sealed-filing motions, the trademark stays —
not only the capital ones. A work admitting only the deaths would manufacture its own finding, which is
the charge that killed *5001*.

## §6. What the rule refuses

No index, no anchor menu, no jump control, no deep link to a unit, no "latest" entrance, no reverse
chronology, no keyboard shortcut to the end, no counter, no countdown, no "updated daily", no notify, no
dates of ours. **The forcing sentence the gate will put verbatim:** *"If a visitor can reach any order in
this work without travelling the empty days that stand between it and the order before it, the form is
not forced, and this work does not premiere."*
