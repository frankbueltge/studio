## 1. The work

A white column, one page wide, that grows downward by exactly one page-height every calendar day.
Nearly all of it is blank. On the days the Supreme Court of the United States issues what it itself
calls a **Miscellaneous Order** — an order "issued in individual cases at any time," off the Monday
list, off the argument calendar — that day's page carries the Court's own sheet, reproduced entire as
an image at one committed scale, never retyped.

Most of those sheets are a single page with about six lines on it. Some of them read, in full:

> *(ORDER LIST: 609 U.S.)* TUESDAY, JULY 14, 2026 · CERTIORARI DENIED · 26-5061 SOCHOR, DENNIS V.
> FLORIDA (26A54) · The application for stay of execution of sentence of death presented to Justice
> Thomas and by him referred to the Court is denied. The petition for a writ of certiorari is denied.

A stranger meets: a very long empty white thing; then, travelling it, a few nearly empty pages of
typewriter print. No dates of ours, no labels, no counter, no index, no controls. The title is the
Court's own phrase for what it does in these documents.

## 2. The unit and the issuing rule

**The unit is a calendar day.** The clock is the world's: the day advances whether or not this studio
sits (`memory/decisions.md` row 53).

**THE RULE — committed in full before unit 1, and this is its whole text:**

> Once each calendar day, read the Court's own orders index for the current Term
> (`https://www.supremecourt.gov/orders/ordersofthecourt/25`, and from the first Monday in October
> 2026 the index for the 2026 Term). Take every row on that index whose date is that day **and whose
> type, as printed by the Court, is "Miscellaneous Order."** Order Lists are not taken. Rules
> documents are not taken. For each row taken, fetch the linked PDF, record its URL, byte length and
> SHA-256, and render its pages entire — unaltered, uncropped, unrotated, nothing removed and nothing
> added — at the work's one committed scale. Place them at that day's page-height, in the index's own
> order. A day with no Miscellaneous Order on the index carries nothing.

**The discretion, bounded and declared — there are four decisions and they are all made before unit
1:** (i) the hour of the daily read; (ii) the render scale; (iii) the ground; (iv) which Term index is
read, which is itself dictated by the Court's own numbering. **There is no selection.** Every
miscellaneous order enters — the bar-discipline orders, the sealed-filing motions, the trademark stays
— not only the capital ones. A work that admitted only the deaths would be manufacturing its own
finding, which is exactly the charge that killed *5001* (`memory/decisions.md` row 26: the argument
ran equally on the defended parties, so the cruelty was chosen for force).

**Auditable by an outsider against that day's published source:** open the index, filter to that date,
count the rows marked Miscellaneous Order, open each PDF, hash it. Every one of those steps was
performed by hand tonight, on five documents, with `curl` and `python3`+`zlib` — no library, no
service, no judgement.

**A unit must never cost a session.** The read is scripted and has no decision in it. A session whose
only work was issuing units is recorded as an unworked night, not as progress.

## 3. What a hole is

**A hole is a calendar day on which the Court's miscellaneous-orders channel published nothing.** The
apparatus renders it as one page-height of the work's ground, unmarked: no date, no tick, no rule, no
dimming, no "no orders today," no caption of any kind. It is produced by the daily read finding zero
rows, and by nothing else.

The holes are the majority of the work and are built with more care than the units, because they are
the only completed things in it. Measured tonight, from the Court's own index for the 2025 Term: the
miscellaneous channel published on **55 of the 296 days** from 6 October 2025 to 28 July 2026 —
**18.6 %**. Median gap between two publishing days: **5 days**. Longest: **20 days**. So roughly four
days in five are holes, and the reader's ordinary experience of this work is emptiness.

**Declared bound, on the record and not on the work's face:** a day carrying only an Order List is a
hole under this rule. That is honest because the source is declared narrowly — not "the Court" but
*the channel the Court labels Miscellaneous Order*, whose silence is a real silence. **A night this
studio holds produces no hole**: the day's slot comes from the calendar and its content from the
Court's own dated index, read whenever the apparatus next runs. Nothing is composed after the fact;
nothing before the work's first day ever enters (no back-fill).

## 4. THE DIFFERENTIAL

**What the accumulation does that one unit does not: it changes the category of the object the reader
thinks they are holding.**

One unit is a decision about a person. Two units are a *form*. The disposing language is pre-existing
and identical across different people, and identity is not a property any single document has. This is
not "more of the same" — it is the reader discovering that "the same" is the finding.

The mechanism is already visible on material opened tonight. 14 July 2026 (SOCHOR), 28 July 2026
(DUCKETT, 26-5156) and 28 July 2026 (OCCHICONE, 26-5158) carry, word for word:

> *"The application for stay of execution of sentence of death presented to Justice Thomas and by him
> referred to the Court is denied. The petition for a writ of certiorari is denied."*

**PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION (primary, gating).** Severed readers, five per cell, questions and coding
rules committed before the first stimulus is captured (`memory/decisions.md` row 23).

- Code **FORM** = the reader's unprompted free text names the disposition as pre-existing, standard,
  identical, template, boilerplate or form language, **or** states that different people received the
  same words. Coded by a reader who does not know the hypothesis.
- **Cell A (one unit):** one order document alone. Predicted FORM **≤ 1 of 5**.
- **Cell B (the body):** the work at extent *E*, entered at its beginning. Predicted FORM **≥ 4 of 5**.

**Secondary, non-gating — code OFF-CALENDAR** = the reader states the acts fell at no fixed interval /
not on a schedule. Predicted ≤ 1 of 5 in A, ≥ 3 of 5 in B.

**The two extents: E1 = 3 orders in the body. E2 = 8 orders.** **The door opens at the first extent at
which Cell B passes and Cell A fails**, unmarked, with no announcement (staging condition 4). Until
then the work is not shown at all — which is also the anti-feed mechanism, see §8.

**This prediction runs against our own most recent evidence and is therefore not wish-shaped.**
`memory/decisions.md` row 36 found that mass makes individuation *perceptible* — with the mass present,
readers picked the rarest formula in the document and read cruelty; without it they read a machine
working normally. That mass was **simultaneous** (789 people, one sentence, one day). This one is
**temporal** and separated by weeks of blank. Whether recurrence-across-time reads as a form or simply
as a series of unrelated events is genuinely open, this house has never tested it, and the Artist has
committed to the direction that our own prior evidence does *not* obviously support. If Cell B comes
back at the same rate as Cell A, there is no second explanation and the concept dies (§10).

## 5. The form, and season condition 2

**The epoch-1 object is an HTML page, and it is argued forced.**

The material is a **published page** and the subject is the **interval between publications**. Only two
forms carry both.

- **Paper** carries the page perfectly — these are 612 × 792 pt sheets, the identical geometry to the
  document *NO PART* is made of — and cannot carry the interval, because a wall shows everything at
  once and nobody can be made to walk twenty blank sheets in a room we do not have. It is also
  unavailable: Frank stated in writing on 2026-07-28, *"ich habe aktuell keine Kapazitäten so etwas
  umzusetzen"* (`REQUESTS.md`), and this house already carries one unbuilt physical promise.
  `memory/decisions.md` row 18's standing corollary is directly on point: *when a studio already
  carries one unbuilt physical promise, a new physical concept opens screen-first.* Proposing a second
  unhangable paper work would be the season's first act.
- **Sound** carries the interval and this material has none. Inventing one is *Ledger of Days*, killed
  in session 01 as wallpaper by definition, and this proposal will not re-propose a corpse.
- **The screen** is the only surface this house can operate that carries the page at its own geometry
  *and* the interval as distance that must be travelled — and, load-bearing, it is the only one that
  **can be added to without a session and without a hand**, which is the season's entire thesis and
  the protocol's takedown leg (c).

And it is **not the Grundform**. The Grundform is *the screen apparatus with a visible self-test* — a
terminal, a console, an instrument. This page has no input, no control, no state the visitor changes,
no computed value, no self-test on its face, no number authored by us. It is a surface of paper. That
is a different object, and the Artist accepts being held to it.

**THE FORCING SENTENCE — verbatim, offered to the gate as a numbered item, in the knowledge that the
last such sentence this house wrote was never put and a season condition died of it:**

> **"If a visitor can reach any order in this work without travelling the empty days that stand
> between it and the order before it, the form is not forced, and this work does not premiere."**

An outsider tests it in pixels: search for an index, an anchor menu, a jump control, a deep link to a
unit, a "latest" entrance, reverse chronology, a keyboard shortcut to the end. Any of them and the
sentence has failed. Note the one thing it does **not** forbid: staging condition 7's permanent
addressability, because an epoch URL returns *the whole work as it stood on that date, entered at its
beginning* — it addresses a state of the body, never a position inside it.

**What this form needs from a human realiser: nothing.** That is a claim made deliberately and it is
checkable.

## 6. Nearest neighbours and daylight

**The three the season requires, whatever the subject (condition 6), argued structurally:**

- **Spielraum / Headroom** — the lab's automated watch on hyperscaler efficiency-vs-consumption
  disclosures, which this studio originated and handed away (`REQUESTS.md`, 2026-07-12; the team's
  response of the same date says they built it "with an automated watch on the annual reports"). Its
  live state is **not confirmed first-hand** — `frankbueltge.de/spielraum` returned HTTP 403 on
  2026-07-31. **Daylight, structurally:** Spielraum's unit is a **diff** — it watches a value and fires
  when the value moves; its payload is *change*, its subject is disclosure arithmetic, and its silence
  means *a number stayed the same*. This work's unit is an **act**, not a change. Nothing is compared
  to a previous state, no series exists, no value is tracked, there is no revision to detect and no
  watchlist we assembled — the category is the source's own, printed on the source's own index. Its
  silence means *an institution did not act*.
- ***Diminishing Returns*** (killed session 05) — died on the terminal test (a zero-context visitor
  needed prose) and the material bar (corporate disclosure arithmetic is evidence inside a work, never
  the subject of one). **Daylight:** there is no arithmetic here, no metric convention, no unit to be
  taught, no ratio. The entire content of a unit is one sentence of ordinary English that a stranger
  with zero background understands completely and immediately, and the stake is a person's execution,
  not a PUE figure. The terminal test is passed by the material itself, not by a caption.
- ***One Tap*** (killed after premiere, three stagings, three rejections by the eye) — died as a
  *body*: an instrument with a dial, then a prompt box, then a redaction, whose genre a cold desk eye
  assigned as an AI product. **Daylight:** there is no instrument. No input, no control, no meter, no
  dial, no state the visitor changes, nothing this studio computes and nothing this studio authored on
  the face. The specific defect that killed it — five incommensurable measurements forced onto one
  axis — has no analogue, because there is no axis and nothing is compared to anything.

made three concepts out of this Court had never once written down the name legal scholarship uses for
exactly this material is itself worth recording.

## 7. The takedown

> **"Ensemble has made the same work twice: another Supreme Court document reproduced whole on a page,
> with a date axis added."**

**How the built work refutes it — by construction, with a kill attached.** The refutation is the
differential instrument in §4 turned on the charge. *NO PART* is one document and one day; run Cell A
on the whole of *NO PART* and the code FORM cannot come back, because there is nothing for a form to
recur across. Run Cell B on this body and FORM must come back at ≥ 4 of 5. **If it does not — if the
two works read the same to severed readers — then the charge is true, they are the same work, and this
one dies on the spot** (§10, death condition 1). This is not a request to be believed; it is the count
that settles it, named before the evidence exists, in the direction that can lose.

The second refutation is material rather than structural: nothing in *NO PART* is off-calendar, and
nothing in this is a mass. The two works cannot be swapped for each other in either direction.

**The takedown law's three-way test — what is claimed:**

- **(c) — a form only this machinery can produce. CLAIMED, and it is the primary leg.** A daily read of
  a live public index, indefinitely, with no human deciding what counts and no judgement anywhere in
  the loop, is production no human studio sustains. This is the protocol's leg (c) verbatim and first
  on its list of forms this house has never touched. Five premieres in, we have never spent the one
  advantage we have.
- **(a) — a finding of our own. CLAIMED, narrowly, and not inflated.** Three, all measured from the
  source tonight: **(i)** the Court's miscellaneous channel published on 55 of 296 days of the 2025
  Term (18.6 %), median gap 5 days, longest 20 — derived from the Court's own index, which publishes no
  such summary; **(ii)** the three orders of 28 July 2026 are **not** all in the same words — two carry
  the singular formula, the third (26-5162, DUCKETT ET AL. V. DESANTIS) carries *"stay**s** of
  execution of sentence**s** of death"*, which **corrects the material handed to this session**;
  **(iii)** a capital stay denial occupies a layout box of 463.78 × 159.71 pt on a 612 × 792 pt sheet —
  15.3 % of it — with 543.7 pt, 68.7 % of the page's height, blank below the last line, set at 10.02
  pt, the identical type size to every text operation in the 39-page order list this house premiered.
  None of these is a scandal and none is presented as one.
- **(b) — real risk upward. CONCEDED, in writing, as `memory/decisions.md` row 31 requires.** This work
  reproduces public-domain federal documents and asserts nothing about them. It carries no exposure.
  *NO PART* had to make this concession and so does this. Safety is not courage, and it is not
  redescribed as any.

## 8. THE FEED

The season's gravest failure mode is a body whose genre a stranger assigns in two seconds and is then
*correct* to ignore, because the work has told them more of it will exist tomorrow. Mechanically, this
is not that:

1. **The work says nothing about tomorrow.** No dateline at the entrance, no "updated," no counter, no
   progress bar, no state-of-the-work panel, no notify, no come-back, no live indicator, no reverse
   chronology, no newest-first. Staging conditions 1 and 6 are enforced by the absence of the elements,
   not by a promise.
2. **The genre assigned in two seconds is *paper*, not *feed*.** The first encounter is a white column
   of the Court's own page geometry with almost nothing on it. A feed's signature is a stack of items;
   this has almost no items and mostly nothing.
3. **The newest unit is at the far end, not the near one.** Entry is at the beginning. Nothing is
   privileged, least of all the newest — the Dramaturg's law, mechanically.
4. **And the decisive one: a feed's promise is that tomorrow's item will be different. This work's
   finding is that tomorrow's item will be identical.** There is nothing to come back for and the work
   demonstrates it, by showing the same two sentences under two names. **A stranger who leaves has the
   finding.** That is the exact opposite of the feed's structure, in which leaving is the mistake.
5. **The door (§4) is itself the anti-feed device.** The work does not exist publicly until it has
   enough units for the finding to be *in* it. It cannot be a promise of future content, because it is
   not shown while it is one.

## 10. The season's death condition (season condition 5)

**Three states, each checkable by someone who was not in the session. Any one of them closes the
direction early.**

1. **The differential fails at extent E2.** If, with **8 orders** in the body, the pre-registered body
   cell has not passed — FORM ≥ 4 of 5 in Cell B against ≤ 1 of 5 in Cell A, codes and questions
   committed before the first stimulus — the direction is declared **failed** and Season Two closes
   early with that count published. No re-runs, no re-coding, no third cell invented afterwards.
2. **The source is too slow to be a clock.** If **30 calendar days** after the first unit the
   miscellaneous channel has produced fewer than **4 unit days** — against a measured base rate of
   18.6 %, which predicts ~5.6 — the work is parked and the season reports, as its result, that the
   direction was untestable on this source within a season's span. That is a finding about THE CLOCK,
   not an excuse.
3. **The form does not carry, and this one can fire in the concept phase, before a single unit
   exists.** If the three rehearsal stills of étude 1 (§11) read to the outside eye as a chart, a
   texture or a broken page, the concept dies for three stills.

**Sessions.** This work may consume **at most 5 of the season's ~10–15**: two concept sessions (this
dossier plus the études), one gate, at most two thereafter. The remainder is protected for concept work.
**Issuing a unit consumes none**, by design (§2) — and if that turns out to be false, that fact alone is
grounds to stop, because a clock that needs a session is a studio with a diary.

**Stated plainly, because the gate should not discover it:** under the door (§4) this work will most
likely **not be shown inside Season Two**. At 18.6 %, eight orders take roughly six weeks. The season's
deliverable is therefore the dossier, the three stills and — this is the point — **a countable result
from étude 3, which can pass or kill the concept in one session, on documents already published**. The
season gets a finding, not a promise.

## 11. The form études

Three. Each is bounded, discardable, buildable by a Builder in one session in this environment, and
each names the result that kills the concept. An étude is a study; none of these is increment 1, and
none of them issues a unit.

**Étude 1 — THE STILLS, at three lengths.** *(Binds first, per `memory/decisions.md` row 24c — the
still-frame test — and discharges staging condition 3 in the same object.)* Fetch the 2025 Term's 72
miscellaneous-order PDFs (~28 KB each; fetched in a session step and committed, since builds have no
network), render their pages, and lay them at their **true calendar positions** across the term's 296
real days. Produce four committed stills, dated before the first unit: the entrance at **day 20**, at
**day 120**, at **day 296**, and one **reading position**. Judged as images, every caption covered,
motion frozen, on one type scale and one spacing scale, no stock UI geometry anywhere. **What it
settles:** whether a field that is four-fifths blank is an image or an empty page — and whether the
entrance still reads as an extent when the extent is long. **What kills the concept:** the day-120 or
day-296 still reading as a chart, a heatmap, a texture, or an unloaded page. **The eye moves earliest
here** (row 24d): this is the étude offered to Frank, where a dissent costs three stills.

*Note on honesty: the rehearsal uses the past term as rehearsal material only. The work back-fills
nothing; the stills are studies and are labelled as such.*

**Étude 2 — THE READING SCALE.** Render the five verified July documents at the one committed scale and
measure at **deviceScaleFactor 1 and 2** (row 25c) at a 390 px viewport and at a laptop width. **What it
settles:** whether the Court's two sentences are actually readable at the scale that also delivers the
interval, and whether the blank page reads as blank rather than as a failure to load. **What kills the
concept:** if reading requires a zoom control, a second scale, or any affordance the visitor must
operate — because that control is the thing the forcing sentence in §5 forbids, and the form would then
not be forced.

**Étude 3 — THE DIFFERENTIAL PILOT.** Severed readers, five per cell, questions and coding rules
committed before the first stimulus is captured, coded by a reader who does not hold the hypothesis.
Cell A: one order document. Cell B: the three verified capital orders in their true calendar positions
with the real blank days between them — extent E1 = 3. **What it settles:** whether the category-change
happens at all, and at what extent. **What kills the concept:** Cell B returning FORM at or below Cell
A. This is the cheapest possible kill for the most expensive possible mistake, and it can be run before
any unit exists.

**The carried-out sentence (staging condition 8), written now, before the first unit, to be tested at
two extents:**

## 12. What the Artist suppressed

Named as honestly as possible, because last season's suppression list turned out to be a list of
assumptions and one of them was hiding the better work.

1. **The paper work — and it may be the better one.** One printed page per day, pinned in sequence, is
   the canonical realisation of this exact form; Kawara and Hsieh did not need a browser. It is not
   proposed **because nobody can hang it**, which is an availability argument and not an artistic one,
   and that is said rather than dressed as a form decision. The one mitigation that is real: the units
   *are* printable by definition — they are the Court's own 612 × 792 pt sheets — so if a wall ever
   comes within reach the work already has a body and needs no redesign.
2. **Sound, entirely — and the Kritiker's own synthesis with it.** *HEARD / NOT HEARD* is beaten here on
   **timing, not merit**: the argument-audio page carries no 2026 Term content, the Court hears argument
   October through April, and a same-day audio clock started now produces its first unit around 5
   October 2026 — after this season has closed. A work whose first unit arrives after the season ends
   cannot be the season's first work. **That is a scheduling verdict and not a judgement on the form**,
   and if the season's span were three months this proposal would rank second. It is the ranked
   alternate.
3. **The mass — deliberately, and it costs something.** Excluding Order Lists buys daylight from *NO
   PART* and throws away the counter-image that would make the recess legible: the Monday ceremony as a
   dense slab beside the near-blank at-any-time sheet. The live alternative design is *both types as
   units*, and it has not been taken. If the stills come back inert, that is the first thing to try.
4. **Editorial selection — so most of the work will be boring.** Bar discipline, sealed motions,
   procedural stays. The deaths are found among them or not at all. That is probably right; it is also
   the likeliest way this work fails to hold anyone's attention, and no mechanism in the design protects
   against it.
5. **Any claim at all.** The work argues nothing, and a critic may reasonably say it therefore *says*
   nothing — that it is a beautifully typeset public-records viewer. The only answer is §4's count, and
   if that count fails there is no other.
6. **The assumption most to be suspected: that the work must be one continuous surface.** If étude 1
   kills the column, the live alternative is one addressable page per day with no continuous field — and
   there is no answer yet for how the extent gets met in that version. It is not a fallback; it is an
   unsolved problem named rather than hidden.
7. **And the one that should worry the gate most: this Court has now been proposed a fourth time.**
   ORDER LIST, 5001, *NO PART*, and now this. The reasons: the material is verified in our own hands,
   the season is roughly a fortnight long, and nothing else on the table carries stakes a stranger
   recognises in one line. But a house that has made four concepts from one institution's PDFs is a
   house with a monoculture, and "there was no time to go looking" is exactly the kind of reason this
   season was declared to break. **If the Kritiker kills this proposal on that ground alone, the ruling
   will be correct and the Artist will not have an answer to it.**
