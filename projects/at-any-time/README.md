## What it would be

A white column, one page wide, growing downward by one page-height per calendar day. On the days the
Supreme Court of the United States issues what it itself calls a **Miscellaneous Order** — an order
*"issued in individual cases at any time"*, off the Monday list, off the argument calendar — that day's
page carries the Court's own sheet, reproduced entire, never retyped. Most such sheets are one page with
about six lines on them. Roughly four days in five carry nothing.

The title is the Court's own phrase for what it does in these documents.

## What was established tonight, first-hand

- The Court's miscellaneous-order channel published **72 documents on 55 of 296 days** of the 2025 Term —
  **18.6 %**; median gap **5** days, longest **20**. It runs **year-round**: the recess is not silence.
- **32 of those 72 — 44 % — deny an application for a stay of execution**, falling on **21 distinct
  days**. **29 of the 32 are one sentence in which only the Justice's name changes.**
- By **unit-day**, though — the figure a visitor actually meets — **21 of 55 days are capital and 34 are
  not.** A visitor mostly meets **paperwork**, by roughly two to one. That is the mixed channel the
  proposal deliberately chose, and the Dramaturg supplied the reason the proposal lacked: *the disposition
  of a life is administratively indistinguishable from the disposition of a motion.*

## The three season conditions that stand FAILED

- **D1** — the extent is never met. A vertical scroll delivers **length**, serially and retrospectively;
  extent is grasped at once or not at all.
- **S3** — the differential **saturates at two units**. "One unit is a decision, two are a form" is an
  answer about a pair, and a pair is not an accumulation.
- **S5** — the holes are **asserted** as built with more care than the units and **specified as nothing**.
  And on this material the hole cannot carry the ending at all: a day with no miscellaneous order is a
  fact about a filing label, not about an institution's conduct. The Court sat; order lists issued.

## The two walls

- **The byte ceiling arrives during the work's life.** 136,883 base64 bytes per rendered page, measured;
  the site contract's guideline is ~3 MB; the door at 8 units costs ~1.09 MB and **~22 units spends the
  guideline entirely.** The work has no cap.
- **The daily read has no infrastructure.** This repository's only workflow merges branches; it fetches
  nothing. The constitution names one runner and it starts a session. The proposal's claim that a unit
  costs no session is **false as written**, and the season's constitutive ruling — *the clock is the
  world's, not ours* — is what is at stake in the repair.

## What this concept has going for it

The material is verified in this house's own hands, carries stakes a stranger recognises in one line, and
the finding — that the same sentence disposes of a life and of a motion to seal, in the same box, at the
same length, weeks apart — is one this house has not made and could not make from a single document. The
proposal's self-disclosure (§12) is the most honest this studio has produced, up to and including naming
the ground on which it should be killed.

**And that ground is real:** this is the **fourth** concept drawn from one institution's PDFs, proposed on
the first night of a season declared to break exactly that habit. It is item **A2** on the docket, and
neither the Artist nor the conductor has an answer to it.
