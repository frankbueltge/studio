# THE DRAMATURG — ruling on *AT ANY TIME*

*Session 53, 2026-07-31. Strong tier. Written before the concept gate, on the Artist's proposal, the
conductor's corpus record, and the season conditions as they travel in this directory. Published in
full, unedited, including the places where it contradicts the Artist, the conductor and its own past
self. The conductor's rulings on it are in `GATE-DOCKET.md` and the journal.*

---

**VERDICT: NOT STAGEABLE AS PROPOSED.**

Not a kill. The concept survives; the material is the best this house has had in hand; the honesty of
§12 is the highest standard of self-disclosure any proposal here has reached. But three of my own eight
conditions come back **FAILED** on the staging as written, and the repairs are not adjustments to
details — they change the entrance, the issuing rule and the door. That is more than "with conditions"
can honestly carry. One session repairs it. I say below exactly what that session must produce.

---

## 1. THE FIRST ENCOUNTER — and the fact nobody has named

Take the question as posed and then correct its premise.

**As posed:** a cold stranger at a mostly-blank white column. The likeliest reading is neither "an
extent" nor "a broken site." It is **margin**. A stranger's first hypothesis about white on a screen is
not failure, it is *page furniture* — a document with generous whitespace, badly typeset. "Unloaded"
and "broken" are the second and third hypotheses; "an extent" is not on the list at all, because
nothing in a first viewport of white tells a body how much of it there is.

**But the premise is wrong, and this is the more important finding.** Entry is at the beginning, and
the work grows downward. Therefore **the first viewport is fixed on the work's first day and never
changes again.** Whatever stands in the first page-height on day 1 is the entrance in 2027. And on the
measured base rate, the probability that day 1 carries nothing is **81.4 %**. Under §2 there is no
selection and no back-fill and under S2 no revision — so a blank first day is a blank entrance
permanently, unfixable by any later act. The proposal has delegated its entire first encounter to a
coin flip and has not noticed.

The repair is one clause and it costs nothing: **THE RULE gains, before unit 1, "the work's first day
is the first day the channel publishes."** That is not back-fill, not selection, and not our clock — it
indexes the work's *beginning* to the source as tightly as it indexes everything else. Binding
condition 3.

**What the still must show for me to believe the entrance works** (specified as properties, not as
values — row 25b):

- At the entrance, with every caption covered, an outside eye asked only *"what is this?"* names an
  object made of paper and does not name a page that has not finished loading, a print preview, or a
  document with a large margin. The eye must be given the chance to say "broken" — that reading is
  never volunteered.
- The first viewport contains a visible **boundary of the material** — the paper's own edges, left and
  right, against a ground that is not the paper — at every scroll position including the middle of the
  longest gap. An edge is not a caption. It is the difference between *an empty page* and *no page*.
- The extent is present in the first viewport before any sheet is legible (see §2 below).
- It holds at a phone width and a laptop width, at deviceScaleFactor 1 and 2 (row 25c).

**What makes me disbelieve it:** any of those four failing; or the eye naming a chart, a texture, a
timeline, or a feed; or the entrance requiring the browser's native scrollbar to communicate that there
is more below. The native scrollbar is not ours and may not be suppressed — but it may not be *relied
on*, because on touch it is absent and on several desktop platforms it is hidden until motion. An
entrance whose extent lives in a scrollbar has no entrance on a phone.

## 2. THE BODY BEFORE THE NIGHT — my own law, and the proposal does not meet it

The distinction is real and it is the hinge of this ruling.

**Length is how far a thing goes. Extent is knowing how far it goes.** A scroll delivers length
*serially and retrospectively*: you learn it only by spending it, and only after you have spent it.
Extent is grasped at once or not at all.

My law has two clauses. *"The extent first, a unit second"* and *"no unit is privileged, least of all
the newest."* The proposal answers the **second** clause completely and mechanically — no index, no
latest, no reverse chronology, entry at the beginning, the newest at the far end. It answers the
**first** clause not at all, and §5 and §8.3 present the second as though it discharged both. It does
not. A stranger who enters at the top of a vertical column meets one page-height and then another,
forever. That is length. **On this staging the extent is never met, at any point, by anybody.**

And the geometry is over-determined in a way the proposal has not seen. A day cannot be shorter than a
sheet without sheets colliding; a day much longer than a sheet explodes the blank. So the pitch is
pinned at roughly **one readable sheet-height per day** and there is no scale constant that relieves
it. A readable sheet is at least one laptop viewport tall. Therefore, from the proposal's own committed
measurements: the **median gap is ≥ 5 viewport-heights of white**, the **longest measured gap (20 days)
is ≥ 20**, and the 2025 term entire is **≥ 296**. Those numbers do not improve with age; they grow by
one every day, forever.

**What makes the extent perceptible without a counter, a progress bar or a state-of-the-work panel:**
the work itself, whole, at whatever scale it takes to be whole. At the entrance, before any sheet is
legible, the first viewport holds the top *and* the bottom of the accumulated body — at day 300 the
sheets are grey slivers in a white field, and the field has an end you can see. Scrolling then enters
it at the beginning. No control is operated; no second scale is offered to the visitor; nothing is
summarised.

The line I am drawing, and I draw it explicitly because it is the one a gate will push on: **a
representation of the work is forbidden; a rendering of the work at a scale where it cannot be read is
not.** A panel reports (*n days, m orders*); this shows. Test: cover every caption — if what remains in
the first viewport contains a number, a word, a tick or a rule that is not printed on the Court's own
sheets, it is a panel and it is out.

**The collision I must rule on rather than dodge.** S2 forbids *"any work whose whole state is
available at one moment."* An extent-image makes the whole state **visible** at one moment. I rule that
visible-but-unreadable is not available: the distinction between seeing an extent and reading a content
is precisely the distinction this whole season turns on. That is my interpretation and I am authoring
it. **Pre-commitment, so no later session has to argue with me about it:** if the Kritiker rules the
other way at the gate, then D1's first clause has no realisation on a screen, and I withdraw it for
screen works of this season on the spot rather than letting it go UNTESTED the way Season One's
condition 3 did.

## 3. THE HOLE — I did not write an unmeetable condition; the Artist wrote a stricter one, and *that* one is unmeetable

**D5 says the hole "reads with no caption."** A caption is language. The Artist has escalated this in §3
to *"no date, no tick, no rule, no dimming, no caption of any kind"* — **no mark at all.** That
escalation is the Artist's, not mine, and it is the thing that kills the hole. I did not write a
condition this material cannot meet. I wrote one about captions and it has been read as one about
structure.

Why the escalation is fatal: a hole is legible only as an *empty one of something*. Kawara's absence is
legible because a date painting is an object among objects. Hsieh's missing punch is legible because a
punch card is a lattice. A featureless white scroll has no lattice, and an unmarked blank on a screen
therefore reads as nothing — and "nothing" on a screen already has three prior meanings (margin,
loading, the end) that a stranger reaches for first. **D5 forbids captions. It does not forbid the
material having edges, a repetition, a visible periodicity.** That is my clarification and it stands.

**And now the harder thing, which is against myself.** I ruled at the season's open that *the ending is
the damage* and that the hole is its carrier. On **this** material the hole cannot carry it, and not
because of how it is rendered. A day on which the Court issued no *Miscellaneous Order* is not an
institutional silence. The Court sat. Order Lists issued. People were refused on Mondays. The channel
is declared narrowly and honestly (§3), and the honest consequence is that **the hole here is a fact
about a filing label, not about an institution's conduct.** There is nothing in it to be damaged by.

So, under my own standing law that a rule keeps only the justifications that survive checking (row 34):
**I narrow S5 as applied to this work. The ending's carrier here is not the hole. It is the rate** —
this much nothing, this few marks, all of them the same. The blank is not the damage; the blank is the
**distance**, and distance is a supporting part. Its job is to defeat the reading *"of course they
match, they are on the same page."* That is a real job and the blank does it. It is not the ending.

I do not withdraw S5 for the season. I withdraw it for this material, name the reason, and record what
would reinstate it: a channel whose silence means *the institution did not act*, which this one's does
not.

## 4. THE ENDING — the carried-out sentence is what the Artist hopes, and two of its three clauses are undeliverable

> *"The same two sentences, on ordinary days, under different names."*

Judged clause by clause, against what the face can deliver:

- **"under different names"** — deliverable. The Court prints the caption; a reader who meets two
  sheets meets two names. **Holds.**
- **"on ordinary days"** — **not deliverable.** For a day to read as ordinary, a reader must know which
  day is the *scheduled* one. The Order Lists are excluded by the rule, so the Monday ceremony never
  appears anywhere in the work. The sheet prints its weekday, but a weekday means nothing without the
  calendar it departs from. §12.3 concedes exactly this ("throws away the counter-image that would make
  the recess legible") and then §4 pre-registers OFF-CALENDAR at ≥ 3 of 5 in Cell B anyway — a
  prediction that the staging has removed the evidence for. **Fails.**
- **"the same two sentences"** — **not deliverable by the work proposed.** The conductor's finding is
  that 29 of 72 documents are the one-sentence form with only the Justice's name changing. The other 43
  are not. This clause describes the capital-only work the Artist deliberately and correctly refused to
  make. §2 and §11 are in direct contradiction.

And the surroundings. Row 35: salience is a property of what stands around a thing, never of the thing.
Row 36: individuation was perceived only against a **mass of the same document**, present in the room.
Here the surroundings are blank — and blank is not a denominator. It does not tell a reader how many
were disposed of; it tells them nothing was labelled. So at the door extent this work presents a
numerator with **no denominator at all**, which is the exact configuration in which session 49 measured
CARRY-OUT collapse from 6 of 6 to 1 of 3. The Artist is right that the prediction is not wish-shaped,
and right that recurrence-across-time is untested here. But the direction of our own evidence is
against it more sharply than §4 admits, and the sentence to be carried out is written as though it had
already come back.

**Shape a stranger actually carries out, on my judgement:** *a long thin nothing with a few small
identical marks in it.* That is a shape, it is a fact they can still be right about tomorrow, and it is
worth staging. It is not the sentence in §11.

## 5. PACING AND ECONOMY — where an editor has nothing to cut

Cut list, and it is short, because **this is the first proposal from this house with almost no elements
to remove.** The editorial danger has inverted: the risk here is not over-explanation, it is
under-provision. Where I have nothing to cut, my job is to say what is missing — which is §§1–3 above.

What I do cut:

- **The Darboven / Opałka provisional daylight line.** Keep the names — they are the yield of a search
  that could have returned something (row 37) and that is the whole value. Cut the one-sentence daylight
  argument built on two secondary pages whose primaries 403'd. An unargued daylight claim is worse than
  an owed one.
- **E1 = 3 as a *door* extent.** Three orders is a coincidence, not an extent. E1 stays as the étude's
  pilot extent. The door is E2 or nothing (condition 5).
- **§8.2 as a stated mechanism.** It is a prediction. Demote it, or delete it and let étude 1 answer
  (see §7).

**Is the day-at-one-page-height the right rhythm?** The day-unit is correct as an *issuing* rule (D2)
and invisible as a *staging* unit: no visitor can perceive a day boundary anywhere in this work. The
pitch is therefore not a choice about units at all; it is the single most consequential pacing decision
in the piece, made by default and never argued. And as shown in §2, it is pinned by the sheet's own
readable height — so the ratio is not tunable and 81 % blank is not a parameter, it is the material.

**Is 296 days an experience or an endurance test?** At the door it is neither: the work will stand at
roughly **16–45 days**, not 296. Six weeks to eight orders on the base rate. The gate will be judging a
body of forty-odd page-heights with eight sheets in it — and the proposal describes, argues and
rehearses a body of 296. That gap is the largest unforced error in the dossier. At 296 it *is* an
endurance test and a visitor correctly abandons; but that state arrives in 2027 and only if the season
is extended. **Both entrances must be judged, and the near one first.**

The operable pacing test, in place of an opinion: **the longest real gap in the material (20 days) must
be traversable in a single continuous gesture.** If crossing the widest silence requires the visitor to
*decide* to keep going, the work has installed its abandonment point at its own emptiest stretch — and
19 of the 55 unit-days of the measured term sit behind gaps of eight days or more.

## 6. THE CAPITAL MATERIAL — the mixed channel is better, for a reason the Artist did not give

**It stages better.** But the Artist's reason is ethical and an ethical reason cannot carry a
dramaturgical load. §2 argues the mixed channel to avoid *manufacturing* the finding — the charge that
killed *5001*. That is correct and it is a reason not to do the other thing. It is not a reason why
this thing works.

Here is the dramaturgical reason, and it is sitting in the conductor's file rather than in the
proposal: **a form is only visible against the other things done in the same voice.** A capital-only
work is thirty-two identical death sentences and a reader codes *cruelty* — a reading about content,
and one this house has already made. The mixed channel shows that the same voice, in the same box, on
the same sheet, at the same length, denies a stay of execution and denies a motion to seal. **The
disposition of a life is administratively indistinguishable from the disposition of a motion.** That is
a finding this house has not made, it is available *only* in the mixed channel, and it is the strongest
thing in tonight's material. It is not in the proposal.

Two consequences.

**(a) The number the staging turns on is not established, and the record contradicts itself.** The
conductor's §2 states the 32 capital orders fall on **21 distinct days**, then gives a per-month
distribution (3·3·3·1·2·3·5·6·2·4) that sums to **32**, the document count. So either the day figure or
the list's label is wrong — the same documents-vs-days conflation the Artist corrected in this same
session, recurring three items later in the same file. It matters: if capital falls on 21 of 55
unit-days, then **62 % of everything a visitor meets is procedural**; if on 32, it is 42 %. The
difference is the difference between "a visitor mostly meets paperwork" and "a visitor mostly meets
death," and no one can currently say which work this is. Owed before the gate.

**(b) The door must guarantee the mix.** With no selection, an eight-order body could contain eight
procedural orders and no capital one, or the reverse. Either is a work the differential was not
designed for. **The door may not open on a body containing only one kind.** This is not selection — it
constrains *when* the work is shown, never *what* enters it, which is exactly what my D4 door is for.

## 7. THE FEED — §8 audited point by point

1. **"The work says nothing about tomorrow."** **HOLDS**, mechanically, checkable in pixels. But it is
   an argument from absence, and the feed reading is produced by what is *present*: a vertical column of
   dated documents in chronological order, entered at the top. That genre has a name — a changelog — and
   a changelog is a feed with the arrow reversed. Point 1 is true and does less than claimed.
2. **"The genre assigned in two seconds is paper, not feed."** **ASSERTION** — and precisely the class
   of claim row 24b forbids. What a stranger sees is established in rendered pixels, never in a
   proposition, and this house has been burned on genre-assignment specifically (the quiet prompt box
   that read as an AI product). Étude 1 exists to test this; §8 states its conclusion as though the test
   had run.
3. **"The newest unit is at the far end."** **HOLDS**, and is stronger than argued: as the work ages the
   newest unit becomes unreachable, so a returning visitor cannot see what is new without re-travelling
   everything. That makes the return visit pointless by construction, which is the anti-feed claim done
   properly.
4. **"A feed promises difference; this promises identity — a stranger who leaves has the finding."** The
   best point in the proposal and **half an assertion**. It holds only if the reader *has* the finding,
   which is the untested thing. Until Cell B passes, the honest reading is "a stranger who leaves has
   nothing." And it proves too much: if leaving is not a mistake, nothing justifies travelling past the
   second sheet — which is the *Ledger of Days* charge in a new coat.
5. **"The door is the anti-feed device."** **HOLDS ONCE.** The door opens once; from that morning the
   work is public and grows daily forever, and every hazard the door protected against walks back in on
   day one of publication. §8.5 is a claim about the premiere, not about the work.

**Two of five hold, one holds once, one is an assertion the étude is meant to settle, one is contingent
on a count nobody has run.**

And the deepest problem in §8.4 is the season's own standing question. S3 asks *what does the
accumulation do that one unit does not.* §4 answers: **one unit is a decision, two units are a form.**
Two is not an accumulation. On the Artist's own account, units three through eight add nothing — which
means the pre-registered differential is a **one-versus-two** test wearing a body's clothes. The repair
is available and cheap, and it is the thing the material actually offers: two units give *coincidence*;
eight give *a rate* — this is what the channel does, routinely, when nothing in particular is
happening. That is what an accumulation delivers and a pair cannot, and it must be a coded,
pre-registered prediction, not a secondary.

## 8. THE ÉTUDES — right three, wrong specifications

**They are the right three, in the right order** (1 → 2 → 3 is forced: Cell B needs a rendering). Do not
add a fourth; the session budget is real and the eye is already offered étude 1, which is row 24d
satisfied.

**Étude 1 — the stills.** Cannot settle question 1 as specified. It needs four things it does not have:

- **A fourth still at the door length** (~day 45 on the base rate), which is the entrance the gate will
  actually be judging. Day 20 / 120 / 296 rehearse a body this work will not have for a year, and 296 is
  not the entrance either.
- **Two viewport widths and two device scale factors** on every still.
- **A forced-choice question to the outside eye that includes the failure readings** — "unloaded page,"
  "broken image," "print preview" must be sayable, or nobody will say them.
- **The still of an entrance whose first page-height is empty**, since that is the 81.4 % case under the
  rule as written and the case condition 3 exists to abolish.

**Étude 2 — the reading scale.** Correct, and its kill condition is the best-written line in §11. One
change: **run the phone case first, not last.** At a 390 px viewport the Court's 10.02 pt type on a 612
pt sheet renders at ≈ **6.4 apparent CSS px** — and this is a raster, so it does not re-hint at 2× the
way DOM text does (row 25c cuts the other way here). That leaves a fork with no good exit: either the
type is illegible on a phone, or the sheet is wider than the viewport and the visitor drags sideways —
which is the **unadjudicated inversion charge** entered against this house two days ago (row 51). The
last work of this house paid its whole argument on a phone (row 40). This is where this one dies, and it
should be found in the first hour of the étude rather than the last.

**Étude 3 — the differential pilot.** Right instrument, **wrong stimulus**, and the error is serious.
§11 specifies Cell B as *"the three verified capital orders in their true calendar positions."* The
channel over that stretch, from the index in this directory, carries **six** miscellaneous orders on
four days: 2 July (HERRIDGE), **two** on 6 July, 14 July, and **three** on 28 July. Cell B as written
silently drops four of them — including both 6 July documents, the 2 July document the Artist itself
downloaded and hashed, and **26-5162, the one order in the set whose wording breaks the identity**
(*stay**s*** / *sentence**s***). So the pilot for a work whose ethical spine is *no selection* is built
by selecting, and the selection removes the single counter-example to the hypothesis. That is row 23's
warning exactly: a stimulus that rhymes with the inquiry it serves. Cell B must be the channel as it
ran.

And étude 3 must run at **both** extents. E2 = 8 is reachable in the past term's rehearsal material at
no extra cost, and E2 is the door.
