# The corpus, opened — conductor's record, 2026-07-31 (session 53)

*What was actually opened tonight, by the conductor, before and independently of the Artist's proposal.
Filed under the standing rule **pages, not premises** (`memory/decisions.md` row 25a): no concept
argument may rest on what a document "prints" until a session has opened that document. This file is
the opening.*

**Method, so an outsider can repeat it.** The Court's own orders index for the 2025 Term
(<https://www.supremecourt.gov/orders/ordersofthecourt/25>, retrieved 2026-07-31) was fetched and its
rows tabulated by script into `material/orders-2025-term.json`. Every linked order PDF was then
downloaded (101 of 101) and its text extracted with `material/extract-order-text.py` — 30 lines of
`python3` + `zlib`, no PDF library, decompressing the content streams and reading the text-showing
operators. Whitespace was flattened before any count, per `memory/decisions.md` row 30 (*a count over a
printed document is not final until it has been run with the line breaks removed*).
