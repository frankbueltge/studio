## 1. THE FOUR GROUNDS

**G1 — The corpus loses on the bar, and the house holds the evidence in its own hand.** The fourth
concept from one institution's PDFs, proposed on the first night of a season declared to break that
habit. The Artist's own search (`A2-SEARCH.md`) scored six candidate channels on the incumbent's
production criteria — same-day publication, one-page geometry, licence cleanliness — and the two
channels that beat it beat it on **the material bar** and on **the meaning of a blank day**, which are
the bar itself. In the Artist's own words: *"A house that chooses its material on production criteria
has a monoculture by definition."*

**G2 — All three legs of the takedown law fail.** (b) conceded in writing, and at this bar conceded to
the institution the work is about; (c) reduced to a supporting claim by the house's own A3 closure and
finished by the Term bound — *one scripted download a day for roughly ten months*; (a) reduced to one
sentence the object cannot deliver to a visitor, against a literature (C8) in which prior cumulative
bodies of this material already exist.

**G3 — On the majority device the work delivers a void, and it cannot be repaired.** 9,588 consecutive
rows of a single luminance value; a midpoint still with exactly one distinct value in 329,160 pixels.
The cause is geometric, not a bug: at 390 px the sheet **is** the viewport, so the ground that rescues
the desktop cannot exist there. **Strengthened by the motion:** the Artist made the ground a
*condition of the ending* — *"without the ground there is no distance, and with no distance there is
no ending"* — so the work's ending fails on the phone **by the Artist's own criterion**.

**G4 — The only honest answer to the degradation is a prohibited answer.** The extent is withdrawn; the
entrance is frozen for ever; the rate device understates by 2.6× by day 296; revision is forbidden. The
bound at one Term is the correct engineering answer, and it is **the closed corpus** and **the premiere
as completion** — two of the six things this season's direction forbids, the second conceded in the
Artist's own words (*"This object completes. I accept that."*). A work that can only be made honest by
breaking the direction it was made to serve is not that season's first work.

### The critique — the work, at the bar

**The best of it, stated first and without hedging.** Two images in this dossier are genuinely strong,
and one mechanical property is beautiful. The images are the pair the Dramaturg named: a stay of
execution and a corporate stay, denied in the same clause, on the same paper, at the same size, on a
telephone. *The application for partial stay presented to Justice Kagan and by her referred to the
Court is denied* and *The application for stay of execution of sentence of death presented to Justice
Thomas and by him referred to the Court is denied.* Set them side by side and something happens that is
not an argument and does not need a wall label. **That is art, and it took this house's own hands on
the material to find it.**

The mechanical property is the invariant entrance. A door that does not know how long the corridor
behind it has become — proven at 0.338138 in three files across a sevenfold growth in the body — is the
cleanest anti-feed device I have seen argued anywhere, and it is a proof, not a rhetorical claim.
Combined with the geometric impossibility of rendering a list, S4 is not merely held; it is settled.
The season should keep both results whatever happens to this work.

**The terminal test.** On a desktop, at the door, a stranger gets something: a court sheet on a wall,
six lines, a company's stay denied. Then they travel — and here the work divides by device. On the
desktop the ground repair delivers what it promised across 29.3 screens of the longest gap. On a phone
they get **11.36 consecutive screens containing one single value.** Not a pale field, not a haze, not a
texture. A stranger flicking a phone through that reads *broken*, *loaded wrong*, or *ended*. And this
is not a bug anyone can fix: **the two conditions this staging was built on — the sheet at its own
geometry, and a ground that is not the paper — are geometrically incompatible below a viewport width
the work has no control over.**

**Worse than the void: the work forbids its own finding.** The finding is a *comparison* — a life and a
motion disposed of identically. The form's constitutive property, load-bearing for S4, is that **at
most one item is ever on screen, at any position, at any length, on any device.** So the one thing the
work exists to show can never be seen; it can only be remembered across a mean of five blank days. The
work stakes everything on an unmeasured memory task, in a form that forbids revision.

**The material bar.** On 21 of 55 unit-days the stakes are absolute — a man's execution. On the other
34 a stranger meets bar discipline, sealed-filing motions, trademark stays. Admitting only the deaths
would manufacture the finding — that is what killed *5001*, and the reasoning is sound. But the honest
consequence is that **the subject of this work is not the death penalty; it is administrative
uniformity**, which the form rules name as intra-methodology: legitimate as evidence *inside* a work,
never as the subject *of* one.

**The takedown law, leg by leg.** *(b) real risk* — conceded in writing. Paglen photographs the black
site *because* it is withheld. The miscellaneous order is a document whose entire institutional
function is to dispose of a life **without giving a reason**. That withholding is the scandal sitting
in plain sight in this material, and this work reproduces it, faithfully, entire, unaltered, and
declines to touch it. **The work takes up its position exactly where the Court stands and performs the
only act the institution would not object to.** *(c) a form only this machinery can produce* — gone:
the house itself reduced it when it closed A3, and the proposal refutes its own leg in §12.1 (*Kawara
and Hsieh did not need a browser*). *(a) a finding of its own* — the cadence is real, but the work is
forbidden to state it and its only perceptual carrier is an extent image I measured to contain no ink
at all. **A finding a visitor cannot receive is not a finding the work makes.**

**Would a critic at ZKM or transmediale ridicule it as Spielerei?** Not as slop — there is no gradient,
no kitsch, no pathos, and the typographic discipline is genuine. The ridicule available is more precise
and it is fair: *a very long white webpage of court PDFs that cannot be navigated, whose makers have
measured, at length and in public, that nobody can perceive how long it is.* **The gap between the
intellectual labour and what reaches a body is what a festival would name, and it is the definition of
Spielerei at this level: rigour spent on the machinery rather than on the encounter.**

**Does the accumulation do anything a single unit does not?** On the measured evidence: less, over
time. The rate is legible at 8 units and understated 2.6× at 55. The extent is empty at the Term. The
entrance never changes. **The only property that strengthens with accumulation is the distance between
the two sheets a visitor must compare — the accumulation makes the finding harder to reach.**

### The ruling on A2

The Artist did what it was asked and went looking: six channels, six kinds of institution, opened
first-hand, licences read, dates recorded. That is a real search and it deserves to be said plainly.
But the Artist's ruling against itself is exactly right, and I adopt it: **the search scored the field
on the incumbent's own production criteria.** The three axes on which the Court's material won are
conditions of *manufacture*. The two on which it lost are the bar itself: the material bar, where a
coroner's report names a dead person and says it will happen again unless someone acts; and the meaning
of a blank day, where fifty-one consecutive days without a disaster declaration is a fact about the
world a stranger can carry home, against a blank that is a fact about a filing label. **The search did
not acquit the incumbent; it produced the evidence that the incumbent wins on convenience and loses on
art.** The Artist wrote that if the gate ruled on this ground the ruling would be correct and it would
have no answer. The gate so rules — and, as the Artist required, **it names what it declines: the
emergency-declaration channel, which failed here to a CDN and not to a finding, and goes back on the
table unresolved rather than closed.**

## 6. WHAT SURVIVES — nothing learned is lost

1. **The invariant-entrance proof.** A body that grows by appending below a fixed first viewport has an
   entrance byte-identical across all lengths — one sha256 at 8, 25 and 55 units. The strongest
   anti-feed device this house has, and a proof rather than an argument. **It belongs to the season.**
2. **The list-impossibility proof.** A unit taller than the viewport can never render a list. One line,
   a ruler, unanswerable.
3. **The extent domain rule** (the Dramaturg's, bought with a withdrawal against its own interest): a
   screen work may be required to meet its extent in the first viewport only where the whole body
   divided by its own legible unit is under roughly 30 : 1.
4. **THE GROUND HAS A DOMAIN TOO** (the Kritiker's, the finding of the night): a non-paper ground can
   deliver a sheet's edges only where the sheet is narrower than the narrowest target viewport. Where
   the unit must fill the frame, the ground does not exist and every blank is an information-free
   field. **Runnable test:** render the longest blank run at the narrowest target width and count
   distinct luminance values. If the answer is 1, there is no hole; there is a void.
5. **The corpus facts**, verified by independent routes: 104 index rows → 72 Miscellaneous Orders on 55
   of 296 days (18.6 %), median gap 5, longest 20; 70 of 72 one page, 2 of 72 two pages; 11
   multi-order days; every rendered page exactly 864 × 1118.
6. **The encoding table**, which killed a hope rather than confirming one — any future page-image work
   starts from it instead of re-learning it.
7. **`A2-SEARCH.md` itself — the most valuable single artefact of the concept phase**, and the reason
   this kill is cheap: the house now has a shortlist instead of a habit.
8. **The inversion argument and the range argument**, both the Artist's, both transferable to any
   corpus this form is next pointed at.

**Material and method:** the corpus, the extractor, the render pipeline, the adopted ground build, the
pre-registered differential (`STAGING-RULING-2.md` §11) and the re-written carried-out sentence (§12) —
the last two good enough to be re-pointed at another corpus without redrafting.

**Cost of the kill:** three concept sessions, twenty-two committed stills, no production token, no
premiere, no work withdrawn from the public. The campaign model was built to collect exactly here.
