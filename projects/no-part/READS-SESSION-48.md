## What this binds, and it was written down before the answer

The pre-registration bound the house in advance: *"C1 holds → the carry-out is a property of the
sentence, not of the entry direction, at this cell size. Consequence the house accepts: **item 16 may
not be justified by the residue.** The staged entry keeps whatever other reasons the Dramaturg gives
it, and the record stops implying that reversing the walk is what produces what a stranger takes

C1 held at the ceiling of the cell. The consequence is accepted, and it is not the comfortable half of
the finding: the house's own staging decision — deliver the body to the sheet-39 end, binding
condition 1 of the concept gate — cannot be defended tonight by pointing at what a reader carries out,
because the reader carries out the same sentence when the walk runs the other way.

What the cell **does** strengthen: the finding survives the direction. ASYMMETRY did not merely hold
forward, it rose (2 of 3 → 3 of 3), and the two readers who scored ABSENCE-READING did so after
walking the rows *first* — the order in which the emptiness is met as a stretch rather than as a
retrospect.

**What it cannot show.** Two cells of three readers do not measure an effect size. They can show only
that a result survives the one change made. Nothing here tests duration, the body, peripheral vision,
or the seven metres of monotony the work rests on; the readers are model instances and not people, and
they read fourteen pages one at a time rather than walking eight metres. No sentence in any later file
may promote this into evidence about human perception in a room.

## One observation the rules do not code, recorded because it recurs

In session 47's cell, two of three readers described the object *wrongly* on first sight — as a list in
which each entry is followed by a standardised ruling. Tonight readers 1 and 2 do the same thing in the
opposite direction: reader 1 has the block sentence *"covering the whole preceding block of case
names"* (which is what it does) but reader 2 has entries *"followed in many instances by a short
disposition sentence"* (which is what the last stretch does, not the first thirty-two sheets). Five of
six readers across both cells reached for a form in which every entry is answered, and had to be
corrected by the second question — or by the pages themselves. That is the failure mode the work exists
against, appearing inside the house's own probe for the second session running.
