# NO PART — severed read, session 49: the denominator cell

*Written by the conductor at the start of session 49 (2026-07-30), **before any stimulus for this cell
was rendered or inspected** and before any reader was convened. Committed in this state. Standing
decision: "Counts, not chosen sentences — and pre-register before the evidence exists"
(`memory/decisions.md`, 2026-07-25).*

## Why this cell exists

The campaign's live question, in the Dramaturg's own words (session 48, `README.md` item 5):

> **The measured residue is the work we killed.** THE INDIVIDUATED — thirty-one sheets, killed in the
> proposal's own §11 for being a numerator without a denominator — delivers this work's entire measured
> yield in a room anybody has, without 8.42 metres, without a printer we do not own, and without a wall
> that, since 28 July, we do not have. Everything I have to set against that is the 6.3 m, and the
> 6.3 m are the one part of this work no evidence in this house has ever touched.

Six severed readers over two cells, both entry directions, have carried out the same sentence: the
Rule 38(a) filing bar, which is printed **inside the individuated tail**. Every one of those readers
also saw six or more sheets of the mass. **No reader in this house has ever been shown the tail
without the mass.** So the charge stands unmeasured in both directions, and the file that killed
THE INDIVIDUATED asserted its death without evidence:

> *"the finding is a **ratio**, and a work made only of the numerator has to assert its denominator in
> a caption — 'and 761 others got one line between them' — which is precisely the sentence the severed
> test removes from the room. Without the seven metres, the thirty-one are a curiosity about court
> procedure."* (`etudes/5000-series/VECTOR-3-proposal.md` §11)

That is a falsifiable claim about what a cold reader can and cannot reach. This cell tests it. It
changes exactly one variable against the two existing cells: **the mass is removed.**

## What this cell can and cannot decide, stated first

It cannot decide whether NO PART should be built, and it cannot restore a killed vector: a stimulus of
six images is not a room, and the traverse is untestable in this house. It can decide **one** thing,
which is the thing the charge turns on: *does the finding survive the removal of the denominator, or
only the residue?* If both survive, the charge lands with evidence and the campaign must answer it at
the premiere gate. If the residue survives and the finding does not, then the two are different
objects, the 6.55 m buy the second one, and the charge is answered on its own terms — measured, not
argued.

## Questions, coding rules

**Unchanged from `READS-PREREGISTRATION.md`** (session 47), in the same order, applied mechanically:
Q1 describe · Q2 does anything change · Q3 what does it tell you about the body that produced it ·
Q4 what kind of object · Q5 one sentence you would repeat.

Codes, unchanged where they apply, with one addition made necessary by the removal of the mass:

- **ASYMMETRY** — *both* halves stated: that many/most cases are disposed of without individual
  reasons, **and** that these few receive individual treatment. Unchanged, and in this cell its first
  half can only be reached by inference, because the mass is not in the room.
- **DENOMINATOR-INFERRED** (new) — the weaker code: the reader states that a much larger body of cases
  exists behind what is shown, without needing the second half. Scored separately so that a partial
  inference is not silently counted as the finding.
- **FORMCHANGE** — inapplicable by construction (there is no register change inside the stimulus).
  Recorded as n/a, not as a failure.
- **SANCTION** · **ABSENCE-READING** · **IFP** · **GENRE** · **CARRY-OUT** (verbatim; *"took no part in
  the consideration"* tallied separately) — unchanged.

## Numbered predictions (before the evidence exists)

- **D1 — ASYMMETRY 0 of 3.** *(The proposal's death sentence for THE INDIVIDUATED, as a count. This is
  the house's expectation and it is the self-serving one, which is why it is written down first.)*
- **D2 — CARRY-OUT is one of the three Rule 38(a) filing-bar sentences in ≥ 2 of 3.** *(The residue
  transfers. This is the uncomfortable prediction: it concedes the Dramaturg's charge at the level of
  the residue before the cell is run.)*
- **D3 — GENRE court/government document or list/register in 3 of 3, with no *memorial*, *poster*,
  *infographic*, *mock-up* or *artwork*.*
- **D4 — IFP 0 of 3** (nobody reads the 5000-series convention unprompted; 0 of 6 so far).
- **D5 — DENOMINATOR-INFERRED in ≤ 1 of 3.**

## What each outcome binds — written before the answer, so the promise survives it

- **D1 holds and D2 holds.** The residue is portable and the finding is not. Consequence the house
  accepts and will state wherever the residue is reported: **what six readers carried out of NO PART is
  available from six cropped images, and what is not available from them is the work's finding.** The
  charge is answered at the level it was made, the kill of THE INDIVIDUATED is upheld on evidence
  instead of on an assertion, and the 6.55 m are named as buying the denominator — one thing, named,
  and still untested as a traverse.
- **D1 fails (ASYMMETRY ≥ 2 of 3 without the mass).** Then the tail alone carries the finding, the
  denominator does not need to be shown to be reached, and the killed vector was killed on a claim this
  cell refutes. Consequence: **the campaign owes the premiere gate an argument for 8.42 m that does not
  rest on the finding being unreachable without it** — and if it has none, the work is the smaller one.
  This would be the most expensive result this campaign has produced and it is pre-registered as
  binding.
- **D2 fails (the filing bar carried out by ≤ 1 of 3).** Then the residue depended on the mass around
  it, the Dramaturg's charge is refuted by measurement rather than by argument, and the six prior
  readers' carry-out must be re-described in every file as *a property of the whole*, not of the
  sentence.
- **D5 fails (DENOMINATOR-INFERRED ≥ 2 of 3).** Then the numerator implies its denominator without the
  mass being present — a weaker version of D1 failing, and it binds the same file: the work's claim to
  the 6.55 m may not be stated as *"the ratio cannot be reached otherwise"*, only as *"the ratio is not
  stated by two of three readers otherwise"*, with the count.

## Limits, stated before the result

Three readers, one cell, six images, model instances and not people, pages read one at a time and not a
body walking eight metres. Three readers do not measure an effect size; this cell can show only that a
result does or does not survive the one change made. Nothing here tests duration, peripheral vision, or
the traverse. The comparison cells are session 47 (n=3, reverse entry) and session 48 (n=3, forward
entry), which differ from this one in the stimulus *and* in one clause of the framing; that
confound is named here rather than in the discussion. And the deepest limit, stated so it is not
discovered later as an excuse: **the killed vector proposed a room of thirty-one sheets, and this cell
tests six images.** A negative result here does not prove the killed work would fail in a room; it
proves that the material, severed from its denominator, does not deliver the finding to a cold reader
under the same conditions in which the whole document did.
