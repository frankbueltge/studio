# NO PART

*A print-and-instruction work. This instruction is the whole of the studio's authorship; every mark in
the work is the Court's. Ensemble, 27 July 2026.*

## The document

1. *(ORDER LIST: 607 U.S.)* — the order list of the Supreme Court of the United States for
   **MONDAY, OCTOBER 6, 2025**.
   <https://www.supremecourt.gov/orders/courtorders/100625zor_5368.pdf>
   SHA-256 `354c9ba8dbc6e5104a6a6b84ee53a91a6f8e5e87b2d900e8c26f4a67ef6ec652`
2. 39 pages, each 612 × 792 pt = **215.9 × 279.4 mm**. **Every text operation in the file is set at
   10.02 pt = 3.53 mm** — one size, thirty-nine pages, no exception. Every visible glyph is Lucida
   Sans Typewriter, in its regular, Bold and Oblique variants. (The file also invokes a fourth font
   once, on page 3, to set a single space; it prints no mark. Recorded because it is true, not
   because it matters.) A file with a different hash is a different document and this instruction
   does not cover it.

## Printing

3. Print all 39 pages, one to a sheet, at **100 %** on one plain uncoated white or near-white stock.
   No scaling, no fit-to-page, no cropping, no trim, no bleed, no mark added to any sheet.
4. Printers scale by default. Measure one printed sheet against 215.9 × 279.4 mm, and the type against
   3.53 mm, before printing the other thirty-eight.
5. Repair nothing and correct nothing. Whatever the file prints, the wall carries.

## Mounting

6. Sheet 1 at the left-hand end; each sheet followed by the sheet with the next printed folio. The
   sequence is never reversed, reordered, doubled, or interrupted by anything but a break.
7. **One sheet tall, everywhere.** No sheet above or below another. The document is not to be
   re-stacked into a block.
8. **0 mm joint.** Within a run, adjacent sheets touch along their vertical edges — no gap, no
   overlap, no spacer, no batten. Between runs no distance is specified.
9. **One height.** The horizontal centre line of every sheet **1600 mm** above the finished floor
   (bottom edge 1460 mm), level, the same in every run.
10. Every sheet flat against its wall and in one plane: not bent round a corner, not tilted, not
    framed, not matted, not glazed.
11. Nothing else on the wall or in the room: no label, title, date, credit, caption, arrow, floor
    mark, rope or vitrine, and no light aimed at one part of the run rather than at all of it.

## The break

The line is **8,420.1 mm** long. Almost no wall takes it. Where it breaks is yours.

12. A break falls **between two sheets, never through one**; runs are whole numbers of sheets, 215.9
    mm each. The runs follow one another in order along the path a body walks.
13. **Decide the break from the wall.** Measure the room and let the sheets fall where they fall. Do
    not read the sheets in order to choose where to cut.
14. Forbidden: breaking at a heading, a section, or any boundary in the text; placing a chosen sheet
    at the start or end of a run, or keeping it away from one; giving any sheet a wall of its own;
    adjusting the joint, the height or the scale so a run comes out even.
15. Whichever break you choose, named people fall at it — round a corner, onto a return, behind the
    swing of a door, into a passage someone has to decide to enter. That is your decision, it is
    public, and it stands until the work comes down. Neither the document nor this instruction tells
    you who they are.

## The approach

16. Site the work so that **the room delivers a body to the sheet-39 end**: the first part of the run
    a person entering comes within reading distance of is the free end of the last run, at sheet 39.
    The sheet-1 end is the closed end — an inside corner, the end of a wall, a dead end — with no
    route that brings anyone there first.
17. Do this with the room and nothing else. Nothing may be added anywhere to steer a person, and
    nobody is told which way to walk.
18. A wall has two ends. This makes the direction likely, not certain. Someone will walk it from
    sheet 1 and see the work in the other order, and we accept that rather than mark a floor.

## If the wall is too small

19. If the longest single run carries fewer than **15 sheets (3,238.5 mm)**, mount it anyway and
    record that it did so. The work stands; what stops is us — no claim about what a body finds in
    that room may be made from that mounting.

## What comes back

20. The date, the room, the number of runs, each run's length in sheets, the sheet numbers either side
    of every break, the height as built, which end of the line the way in reaches first and whether
    the room allowed a choice, and a photograph you made of what is on the wall. That record is the
    only evidence this work will ever have, and it is not ours to compose.
