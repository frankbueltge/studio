## Why this cell exists

Session 47's read ran one cell only — reverse entry, the order a visitor meets the sheets under
`INSTRUCTION.md` item 16 — and its own pre-registration named the consequence in advance: *"this read
can fail the condition loudly and cannot confirm that entry direction matters. No comparative claim
about forward versus reverse entry may be drawn from it."* It then produced the result that most needs
a comparison: **3 of 3 readers carried out the same sentence**, the Rule 38(a) filing bar, against a
prediction of the certiorari sentence or a recusal line (P5, 0 of 3).

Under reverse entry that sentence is met **early** — in the first third of the stimulus sequence.
So the session-47 result has two readings and its own cell cannot separate them:

- **Content.** The filing bar is carried out because it is the harshest and most individuated sentence
  in the document — wherever it falls.
- **Position.** It is carried out because it was met first, and primacy did the work. In that case the
  residue is a property of the staging, not of the document, and the staged entry direction (item 16,
  binding condition 1 from the session-46 gate) is producing the very finding the house then reports
  as the document's.

This cell is the missing half. It changes exactly one variable: **the order**.

## What each outcome binds — written before the answer, so the promise survives it

- **C1 holds.** The carry-out is a property of the sentence, not of the entry direction, at this
  cell size. Consequence the house accepts: **item 16 may not be justified by the residue.** The
  staged entry keeps whatever other reasons the Dramaturg gives it, and the record stops implying
  that reversing the walk is what produces what a stranger takes away.
- **C1 fails and the forward cell carries out a different sentence.** Then position is doing the work
  and recency is real at this cell size. Consequence: the ending as staged **delivers the certiorari
  sentence or the rows, not the filing bar**, and binding condition 1 has to be re-argued as a
  dramaturgical choice with a measured effect — including the possibility that the walk should be
  staged the other way round, which would re-open a discharged gate condition and re-run the gate on
  the changed state.
- **C2 fails (asymmetry below 2 of 3 forward).** Then the finding itself depends on meeting the
  individuated tail first, and the work's central claim is weaker than session 47's cell suggested —
  reported as such, in the same sentence as the count, wherever the claim appears.

## Limits, stated before the result

Three readers, one added cell, fourteen sampled pages, model instances and not people, pages read one
at a time and not a body walking eight metres. Two cells of three do not measure an effect size;
they can only show that a result does or does not survive the one change made. Nothing here tests
duration, peripheral vision, or the seven metres of monotony the work rests on — the traverse remains
untested and is untestable in this house.
