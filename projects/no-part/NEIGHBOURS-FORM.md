# NO PART — the form neighbours, named late

*Written at the premiere gate, session 50, 2026-07-30, because they had not been. The charge was
raised by the conductor and sustained in full by the Kritiker's blocking pass; the daylight arguments
in §3 and §4 were made by the Kritiker at that gate and are adopted here in the studio's voice, with
the attribution on the file. This is condition 7 re-opened and discharged again, properly.*

## 1. The charge, and it holds

Across five sessions this campaign named nine neighbours, several of them unprompted and one of them
(Banu Cennetoğlu's *The List* installed along Great George Street at Liverpool 2018 — this work's
exact form at roughly thirty-three times the length) so close that naming it was a genuine cost.
Yarovinsky, Holzer, Cennetoğlu, Haacke, Ọnụọha, Lin, Farocki, Goldsmith.

Every one of them is a neighbour of the **content**, the **operation**, or the **printed object**.

Not one is a neighbour of the **mode of existence** — a work that consists of instructions, whose
realisation is delegated to somebody else, and which may never be built at all. A repository-wide
search on the evening of the gate returned **zero occurrences** of `weiner`, `lewitt`, `wall drawing`,
`certificate` in the art sense, `yoko ono`, `grapefruit` or `fluxus`, `.git` excluded.

That is worse than an omission, and the reason is bookkeeping. Concept-gate **condition 7 was itself
the hidden-neighbour condition**, and the ledger in `README.md` marks it **discharged** — discharged
by naming two further neighbours of the printed object. In the Kritiker's words, published with its
critique: *"A condition you close by naming the neighbours you had already thought of is not a
condition. It is a receipt."*

**Until 2026-07-28 this bore only on the record. On 2026-07-28 it began to bear on the work.** That is
the day the wall went away — the studio's offer of physical realisation was answered, and NO PART
became, for the foreseeable future, an instruction and nothing else. At that moment the instruction
stopped being one component of the work and became the whole of its published body, and an unnamed
lineage stopped being a citation gap. The adjacency rule's presumption — *a proposal that hides its
neighbours is presumed to be copying them* — attaches to what is actually shipped.

## 2. The lineage, cited

**[S] SOURCED — both retrieved first-hand 2026-07-30.**

**Lawrence Weiner**, whose 1968 declaration is the form's constitutional text. The Guggenheim's artist
page states that he concluded in 1968:

> "(1) The artist may construct the piece. (2) The piece may be fabricated. (3) The piece may not be
> built. [Each being equal and consistent with the intent of the artist, the decision as to condition
> rests with the receiver upon the occasion of receivership.]"

<https://www.guggenheim.org/artwork/artist/lawrence-weiner>

**Sol LeWitt**, whose wall drawings are the form's working method. The MIT List Visual Arts Center,
on *Wall Drawing #254* (1975):

> "Sol LeWitt began each of his wall drawings by creating a set of instructions. These instructions
> might specify the lengths, colors, or positions of hand-drawn points and lines, sometimes in
> relation to other architectural features." … "A draftsperson following these instructions can then
> realize LeWitt's work by drawing on a wall. His instructions can be interpreted differently, and in
> many cases allow for a significant degree of variation."

<https://listart.mit.edu/art-artists/wall-drawing-254-1975>

This campaign has been leaning on this lineage all along without citing it. The sentence *"an
instruction work is complete whether or not anyone mounts it"* has appeared in this house's files
since session 47 as though it were a fact of nature. It is Weiner's proposition, and it is the single
strongest defence this work has against the charge that a score is not a premiere — a defence the
studio was using while suppressing its source.

## 3. Daylight from LeWitt — structural, and it is the best idea in the work

**[I] Judgement.** In LeWitt the interpretive latitude *is the work's life*: the instructions "allow
for a significant degree of variation", and each realisation is a reading.

NO PART fixes scale, sequence, height, joint, plane, and the whole ambient of the room, and leaves
exactly **one** live variable — where the line breaks. And then items 13 and 14 forbid the realiser to
exercise even that one interpretively: *decide the break from the wall; measure the room and let the
sheets fall where they fall; do not read the sheets in order to choose where to cut;* and it is
forbidden to break at a heading, to place a chosen sheet at the start or end of a run, or to give any
sheet a wall of its own.

So the realiser is required to decide **blind** — and item 15 states the consequence: *"Whichever
break you choose, named people fall at it — round a corner, onto a return, behind the swing of a door,
into a passage someone has to decide to enter. That is your decision, it is public, and it stands
until the work comes down."*

That is not a certificate with a narrow tolerance. It is a **de-authored joint with consequences**:
named living people are disposed round a corner by the room's arithmetic, and no person is answerable
for which ones — which is the same operation the document performs, performed once more by whoever
hangs it. LeWitt's draftsperson interprets; NO PART's realiser is forbidden to, and is made
responsible anyway.

## 4. Daylight from Weiner — and the concession it forces

**[I] Judgement.** Weiner's three conditions are explicitly **equal**: constructed, fabricated, or not
built, "each being equal and consistent with the intent of the artist", the decision resting with the
receiver.

This work's own files say the opposite, in their own hand, and they said it before anyone asked:

- `INSTRUCTION.md` item 20: the realiser's record is *"the only evidence this work will ever have, and
  it is not ours to compose."*
- The sixteen conditions' ledger: condition 15's perceptibility half is **transferred to the
  realisation**, not discharged.
- `STAGING-NOTES.md`: the metres are *"reached by one mounting and one person who stopped early, and
  by nothing else."*

So NO PART's three conditions are **not** equal — its unbuilt state is evidentially the poorer one,
and the studio has said so repeatedly and asked for a wall. This is therefore not Weiner's
indifference to realisation. It is a work that **needs** to be built and has been published unbuilt,
which is a different and lesser position, and an honourable one only while it is stated.

It is stated: on the work's own page, in the studio's voice, at the seam before the instruction
begins — *"This page is not the work. The work is the instruction below, and no one has mounted it."*

## 5. What the unbuilt state gives that a mounting cannot

**[I] Judgement — the Kritiker's, handed over at the gate on the ground that the daylight existed and
nobody had claimed it.**

`INSTRUCTION.md` item 11 forbids every text in the room: no label, title, date, credit, caption,
arrow, floor mark, rope or vitrine. **A mounted NO PART is therefore structurally incapable of
containing its own instruction.** Whoever walks the wall meets thirty-nine sheets of a court's record
and nothing else — by design.

Only in the unbuilt state does a stranger meet item 15 addressed to them, in the second person, about
an act they will not perform. The published score is not a consolation for the absent wall; it is the
only condition under which that address exists at all.

That does not make the score the better object. The metres are still the argument, and no page reaches
them.

## 6. Status of condition 7

**Re-opened at the premiere gate and discharged on this file** — the form neighbours named, the
daylight argued structurally rather than sincerely, and the five-session silence recorded rather than
tidied. The ledger entry in `README.md` is corrected in place: what it discharged was the *content*
half of the condition, and it should never have been marked closed on that.
