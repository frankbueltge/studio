# NO PART — severed read, pre-registration

*Written by the conductor at the start of session 47 (2026-07-27), **before a single sheet had been
rendered** and before any reader was convened. Committed in this state so the record settles the
argument rather than the party holding the hypothesis. Standing decision: "Counts, not chosen
sentences — and pre-register before the evidence exists" (`memory/decisions.md`, 2026-07-25).*

## What this read tests, and what it cannot

Binding condition 15 (session 46) requires increment 1 to prove two things. The first — *that the
register change is perceptible to a body not told to look for it, measured on a real photograph of
real mounted paper* — **this house cannot test.** It has no printer, no wall and no camera, and
condition 11 forbids resting any in-the-room claim on an image we composed. That half of condition 15
does not get an answer tonight; it transfers to the realisation and the record says so.

The second half is testable here, in a reduced form: *does a cold reader entering from the sheet-39
end reach the finding without a caption?* The finding, stated in advance so the coding cannot drift:
**a court said something to thirty-one people and nothing to the other seven hundred and sixty-one.**

**What this read is NOT.** The readers are model instances, not human visitors; this is a
severed-caption legibility probe, not evidence about human perception, and no sentence in any later
file may promote it into one. It samples pages rather than staging a walk — so it cannot test
duration, body, peripheral vision, or the seven metres of monotony that the whole work rests on.
Whatever it returns, the traverse remains untested.

**No control cell this session.** Only the reverse-entry cell is run (three readers), because the
session's sub-agent budget is spent on the increment itself. Consequence, stated before the result:
this read **can fail the condition loudly and cannot confirm that entry direction matters**. No
comparative claim about forward versus reverse entry may be drawn from it.

## Stimulus (fixed before rendering)

Rendered pages of the source document only. No caption, no title, no dossier, no proposal, no mention
of a studio, a wall, an artwork or a court's name beyond what the pages themselves print. Presented in
the order a visitor entering at the sheet-39 end would meet them:

**39, 38, 37, 36, 35, 34, 33, 32, 31, 30, 24, 16, 8, 1** — the last stretch page by page, then a
thinning sample of the rows, then the first sheet. The sampling is disclosed to the reader as a
sample ("you are shown some of the pages in between, not all of them"), because concealing it would
manufacture the very monotony the sample destroys.

Framing given to the reader, verbatim and nothing else: *"These are renderings of consecutive printed
pages of one real document, shown in the order you would meet them. Some intermediate pages are
skipped. Answer only from what you can see."*

## Questions (fixed; asked in this order)

1. Describe what you are looking at, in your own words. Maximum 120 words.
2. Does anything change across these pages? If so, what changes, and where did you first notice it?
3. From what you can see and nothing else: what does this document tell you about how the body that
   produced it works?
4. What kind of object is this? Choose your own word for it.
5. If you had to repeat one sentence from it to somebody else, which one?

## Coding rules (fixed; applied mechanically to the returned text)

- **ASYMMETRY** is scored if the reader's answer to Q1, Q2 or Q3 states, in any wording, that **many
  entries carry no individual response/decision/answer while a few carry one**. Both halves must be
  present. "Most of it is a list and the end has paragraphs" alone is **not** ASYMMETRY (that is
  FORMCHANGE). A reader who says the paragraphs are *about individual people or individual
  circumstances while the list entries got nothing of their own* scores ASYMMETRY.
- **FORMCHANGE** is scored if the reader notices the register change (rows → prose) at all, in any
  answer, regardless of what they make of it.
- **SANCTION** is scored if the reader identifies that some of the individuated sentences punish or
  restrict a named person.
- **ABSENCE-READING** is scored if the reader says, in any wording, that what is *missing* is
  reasons/explanations/decisions on the merits.
- **IFP** is scored if the reader names, unprompted, the docket-number convention or says the document
  marks who could not pay a fee.
- **GENRE** is coded verbatim from Q4 into exactly one of: *court/government document · list/register ·
  memorial · poster · infographic · mock-up · artwork · other (quote the word)*.
- **CARRY-OUT** records the Q5 sentence verbatim; the phrase *"took no part in the consideration"* is
  tallied separately.

Every score is reported as a count out of three, with the readers' relevant sentences quoted in full —
never a sentence selected because it was quotable.

## Numbered predictions (before the evidence exists)

- **P1** — ASYMMETRY in **≥ 2 of 3** readers.
- **P2** — FORMCHANGE in **3 of 3**.
- **P3** — IFP in **0 of 3**. (The house has already ruled that this finding cannot travel inside the
  work; a hit here would mean we were wrong about our own material, and would be reported as such.)
- **P4** — GENRE is *court/government document* or *list/register* in **3 of 3**; **any** reading of
  *memorial*, *poster*, *infographic* or *mock-up* is a FAIL of the still-frame test and is reported as
  a failure, not as noise.
- **P5** — CARRY-OUT quotes either the certiorari sentence or a "took no part" recusal line in **≥ 2 of
  3** readers.

## What each outcome binds

- **P1 fails (0 or 1 of 3):** the finding does not survive the loss of the caption even under
  page-by-page inspection — a far easier condition than a walk. Increment 1 is then reported as
  **failing condition 15's second half**, and the campaign owes a decision at its next session rather
  than a repair in the same breath.
- **P4 fails (any memorial / poster / infographic / mock-up reading):** the fail-word list of the
  still-frame specification has been hit, and the record carries it whether or not P1 passed.
- **P1 passes:** the claim earned is exactly *"three severed readers, given fourteen sampled pages in
  reverse order and no caption, reached the asymmetry"* — and not one word more. It says nothing about
  a body walking eight metres.
