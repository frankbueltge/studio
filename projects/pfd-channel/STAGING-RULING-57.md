# 1. THE VERDICT

> # THIS CONCEPT SHOULD BE KILLED.

Not *not stageable as proposed*. Not *stageable with conditions*. The door was built twice, tested
twice against a threshold published before any picture existed, and returned zero twice — and the
manner of the failure is not a shortfall of degree but an inversion of the content, whose cause is in
the material and not in the staging. I will show that below and then say it in a sentence a gate can
quote (§8).

My predecessor wrote *"The Kritiker kills; I rule on staging."* **I do not accept that limit and I
contradict it by name.** A work's door is not a department of the work. A body of material that
cannot be entered has not been badly staged; it has been found not to be enterable, and the voice
that found it is obliged to say so rather than hand the gate a list of repairs it already knows will
not work. The Kritiker's blocking attack is still the Kritiker's. This is my finding, and it is
dispositive on its own facts.

# 2. THE ENTRANCE EVIDENCE, RULED

**Ten readers. Two doors. Zero.** Against a pre-committed **≥ 4 of 5** at 390 px, written by the
staging voice one session earlier, before the first étude existed, and copied verbatim into
`ENTRANCE-TEST-57.md` before the stimuli.

### 2.1 Does the threshold bind, and to what?

**It binds.** It was published before any picture, it was run by hands that did not write it, the
coding was copied verbatim, every response is recorded in full including the ones that embarrass the
work, and the one contested clause was scored **both ways** with an identical result. There is no
route by which this instrument was tuned to the answer it gave, and I looked for one.

**What it binds to, precisely, in three parts:**

1. **It binds the Artist's own clause.** `ARTIST-REPAIR-57.md` §1 pre-committed: *"if the severed
   entrance test … returns `OVERDUE` in fewer than 4 of 5 readers at 390 px, I cut clause 2 from the
   carried-out sentence at that moment, without a session and without an argument."* It returned 0 of
   5. **The cut has fired.** The carried-out sentence of this work no longer contains *"The date has
   passed."* What survives is a sentence that says a report was written, a body was named, a date was
   printed, an answer did not come, and no coroner can act. **It does not say anything is late.** The
   knife was the Artist's own and I am not required to soften what it did.
2. **It binds the entrance.** My predecessor's own words: *"Below that, the entrance does not open the
   work and the specification returns."* It does not open the work. Both specifications return.
3. **It does not, by itself, bind the concept.** A threshold on a door is a threshold on a door. I
   will not pretend that 0 of 5 is arithmetically a kill, because it is not, and the house's habit of
   letting a number do an argument's work is exactly what put a 21-month clock and a corpus maximum of
   13 on this board. **What kills is the manner of the failure, which the threshold does not
   contain** — and which the run sheet found honestly, in prose, in §5 and §8, and which nobody had
   predicted.

### 2.2 This entrance, the specification, or the material?

All three were tested tonight, and only the third survives.

**(a) A defect of *this* entrance, repairable by staging — REFUTED, by the session's own second cell.**
Cell 2 was a different entrance: the state's true second-person sentence, three verbatim state
sentences at the head, the recipient given its own line, the coroner named, the encoding changed. It
is not a variant of cell 1; it is the repair of cell 1, built the same night from the Verifier's
corrections and the Artist's own file. It failed **identically in tally and worse in direction**:
partial inversion by four of five became total inversion by five of five. A defect that survives its
own repair, in the same direction, with fresh readers, is not a defect of the object that was
repaired.

**(b) A defect of the *specification* — TRUE IN PART, AND SPENT.** It is true, and I record it against
my own predecessor, that binding condition 1 specified a sentence that is **not the state's wording**
(`VERIFIER-57.md` §2(a): wrong person, an inserted phrase found on no report page, a dropped clause) —
a sentence the ruling that wrote it called *"the largest single improvement available to this work and
it costs nothing."* It is true that binding condition 2 specified a truncation that turns a
conditional sentence about a bad reply into an unconditional one about no reply. It is true that
binding condition 3 specified adjacency and got apposition. **Three of the thirteen conditions were
wrong, and two were wrong in the direction of putting words on the face that the state does not
say.** But the specification was corrected inside four hours and rebuilt inside six, and the corrected
build failed harder. **A specification defect that has already been repaired, and whose repair
measured worse, cannot be the explanation.**

**(c) A property of the material that no staging fixes — THIS IS THE FINDING, AND I NAME ITS
MECHANISM.**

Read what the readers were given and what they did with it. Cell 2's head carried three sentences,
every one of them verbatim from the state. Their grammatical agents are: **a coroner** (*"Coroners
have a statutory duty … to make a report"*), **coroner's offices** (*"compiled after receiving
confirmation from coroner's offices that these responses have not been received"*), and **a coroner**
again (*"a coroner would exceed their powers if they chased a missing reply"*). Five of five readers
put the failure on the coroners. They were not being careless. **They were parsing.**

Now the general case, which is mine and which nobody in this dossier has stated:

> **In this material the state never writes a sentence in which the body that did not answer is the
> subject of a verb of failing.** The recipient appears in exactly one construction — *"You are under
> a duty to respond…"* — which is an obligation, not a breach. Every sentence in the record that
> touches the failure has a coroner, a coroner's office, or the Regulations as its agent. The Chief
> Coroner's page is a list of non-responses that never says that anyone failed.

The work's constitution is *we print only what the state prints; text we write about our own work is a
caption and captions are forbidden.* Put those two facts together and the result is not a difficulty.
It is a closure. **A work bound to the state's sentences cannot print the accusation, because the
state has not written it.** The only vocabulary in which the recipient is at fault is one this studio
would have to compose itself — which is the one thing the form forbids, and the moment it is composed
the work is a page of our prose about a government table.

That is not a door. That is the work.

### 2.3 Two further things the pixels settled, which the tally did not

**The byline is typographic, and it is not repaired by removing the head.** Five of five in cell 2
called a dead man the *author* or *signer* of the document. I opened
`entrance-e3-390-foot-only.png` — the placement variant that was rendered and, correctly, **not
run** — to see whether striking the head would strike the inversion. It does not. With the head gone,
the first thing on the page under the title is **MATTHEW WICKES** in letterspaced small capitals, and
the first thing under *that* is **"Christopher Wilkinson, coroner, Hampshire, Portsmouth and
Southampton."** A name in heading typography with a second name and a title directly beneath it is
the universal setting of *byline, then attribution*. Foot-only makes the byline reading **cleaner**,
not weaker. **I therefore rule that the foot-only cell must not be run**, and I am ruling against my
own interest in more evidence: it would spend five readers to confirm a reading I can see, and the
Artist's falsifier has already fired on the head placement without it.

**The faithful sentence puts figures back on the face.** In both e3 entrances I read, in every duty
sentence: *"You are under a duty to respond to this report **within 56 days** of the date of this
report…"* The only version of the sentence that is faithful to the state reintroduces a **numeral,
sixty-three times, at a horizontal position governed only by the wrap** — which is precisely what my
predecessor's §8 test 5 was written to catch, and which nobody has run against e3 because there is no
second pass. The work's choice was: print the state's sentence and carry a recurring digit, or print
our own and be caught by the Verifier. Both branches are taken and both fail.

### 2.4 Was the instrument mis-designed?

**Yes, in three places — and every one of them makes the result worse, not better, which is why I am
willing to say it after a negative.** Saying an instrument was wrong once it has convicted you is
cheap and I would not accept it from the Artist. Here is the test I hold myself to: *does the
correction move the number toward the work?* In all three cases it does not.

1. **The exclusion clause double-counted.** The Artist said so before any stimulus existed
   (`ARTIST-REPAIR-57.md` §7 (v)) and the Artist was right. Scored both ways: **identical, 0 of 5.**
   It never fired on any of ten readers. Adopted, and it changes nothing.
2. **The genre card was unnecessary and rightly not run.** Ten readers volunteered a genre unprompted
   and volunteered the same one. A card offered after that measures the card.
3. **The primary code had no limb for the disease.** `OVERDUE` required *a lateness word applied to
   the dates* and *the failure attributed to the recipient*. It had **no code at all for the failure
   that actually happened** — the accusation landing on the wrong party, and the deceased read as the
   document's author. The instrument under-detected. Had it been written properly, cell 2 would not
   have scored 0 of 5; it would have scored **five of five for an affirmative reading in the opposite
   direction**, which is a different and worse result than a null one.

**n = 5 per cell is coarse and I record it.** It cannot resolve 3-of-5 from 4-of-5. It can resolve 0
from 4, twice, on two independently built objects, with the same directional error in ten of ten
responses. No amount of coarseness manufactures that.

# 4. THE DOOR

> **The door cannot honestly be set, and I am not setting it.**

Not because the work has not earned one. Because a door is a promise to show the work to a stranger at
a chosen extent, and **an entrance that returns zero of ten is a promise to show it to people who will
read it backwards.** Publishing a door tonight would be this house doing the thing it convicts others
of: naming a threshold for the second event while the first event has failed, so that the failed one
is behind us by the time anyone looks.

I therefore discharge §12 item 5 and binding condition 6 the other way, and I publish **what must be
true before any door may be set**, in numbers, so that this is a decision and not a deferral.

**Gate A — the entrance, and nothing may be discussed before it.**
- `OVERDUE`, both limbs, **≥ 4 of 5 at 390 px AND ≥ 4 of 5 at 1280 px**, on one built object, both
  cells fresh and severed, run by a hand that did not build the page. My predecessor tested one width;
  that was too little and it is corrected here.
- **A new third limb, and it is an absolute bar, not a tally: ROLE.** A cell fails outright, at any
  count, if **one single reader** names a deceased person as the document's author or signer, or names
  a coroner or a coroner's office as the party that failed. Not a proportion. One. A work that can be
  read as authored by the person it mourns has no door at any threshold, and this house has now
  measured that reading in nine of ten readers across two builds.
- Coding published before the stimulus, verbatim, with the responses recorded in full.

**Gate B — the differential, only if Gate A passes.** My predecessor's stillness code stands as
written and I adopt it unchanged: both limbs required — a denominator applied to the *not-answering*,
and a projection implying continuation — with the exclusion clause that a duration read off the
printed due dates alone is the inheritance speaking and scores ABSENT. **Thresholds: ≥ 4 of 5 at the
door extent, ≤ 1 of 5 at extent 1. If extent 1 ≥ the door extent, the accumulation does nothing the
inheritance has not already done and the work does not open.**

**Gate C — one thing must be resolved before either gate can mean anything, and `THE-RULE.md`
discovered it honestly.** §2 of the rule concedes, in the conductor's own words, that *"the count of
marks is days this register looked and found the duty outstanding, which is not the same quantity as
days outstanding,"* and instructs that *"the face must never let the two be read as one."* **That
instruction is unexecutable.** The two quantities are drawn on one axis, in one unit, at one scale,
head to tail, in the same ink. There is no typography that separates them and no caption is permitted
to try. This is not a clause of an operating rule; it is a finding against the form, and it should
have been written as one. Until the two quantities are on different axes or one of them is gone, no
door extent can be set, because no door extent can be *read*.

**And the standing rule I add, for this season and not only for this work:** *no door is published
before its entrance passes.*
