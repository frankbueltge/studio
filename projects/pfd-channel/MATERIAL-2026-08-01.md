## 1. WHAT THE CHANNEL IS

When a coroner in England & Wales investigates a death and the investigation reveals *"a concern that
circumstances creating a risk of other deaths will occur or continue to exist"*, and in the coroner's
opinion action should be taken, the coroner **must** report the matter to a person or body with power
to act. The duty arises under **paragraph 7 of Schedule 5, Coroners and Justice Act 2009**; the
procedure is **regulations 28 and 29 of the Coroners (Investigations) Regulations 2013**.
Source, quoted verbatim from the Chief Coroner's guidance chapter 16:

> "Coroners have a statutory duty, should the relevant pre-conditions be met, to make a report to
> those who can take action to prevent future deaths."
> — <https://www.judiciary.uk/guidance-and-resources/chapter-16-reports-to-prevent-future-deaths-pfds/>
> (retrieved 2026-08-01)

**The recipient is under a statutory duty to answer, and the deadline is in the report itself.**
Guidance, verbatim: *"The recipient of a PFD report must respond within 56 days unless the coroner
agrees to extend the response period."* (Reg. 29). The response must contain either *"details of any
action that has been taken or which it is proposed will be taken and set out a timetable"* or *"an
explanation as to why no action is proposed."* (Reg. 29(3)).

The deadline is printed on the face of each report as a **date**, not a duration. From the report on
the death of Najib Naagi (ref 2026-0271, Senior Coroner Mary Hassell, Inner North London, sent to
North London NHS Foundation Trust), verbatim:

> "You are under a duty to respond to this report within 56 days of the date of this report, namely by
> 13 July 2026. I, the coroner, may extend the period if an appropriate application is made."
> — <https://www.judiciary.uk/prevention-of-future-death-reports/najib-naagi-prevention-of-future-deaths-report/>
> (retrieved 2026-08-01)

**And there is no enforcement.** Guidance, verbatim: *"There is no power authorising a coroner to take
any steps if they receive an inadequate or vague reply… the coroner has no authority in law to take
any further steps."* What a coroner may do is tell the bereaved that *"the non-responder is now in
breach of Reg 29(3) and Schedule 5 part 7(2)."*

Reports **and responses** are published: *"There is a presumption that PFD reports and responses will
be published by the Chief Coroner to ensure open justice and to promote learning."*
(<https://www.judiciary.uk/courts-and-tribunals/coroners-courts/reports-to-prevent-future-deaths/>,
retrieved 2026-08-01.)

### 2a. A CORRECTION OF THIS HOUSE ABOUT THE WORLD — the 21-month clock does not exist

`WORKBOARD.md` (line 252) and the session-54 record carry the sentence that this channel *"dies on
the clock — a verified **21-month** publication lag"*, and the gate of session 55 declined the channel
partly on it. **The figure came from one report.** `projects/at-any-time/A2-SEARCH.md:429` states its
whole basis: *"**Robin Ward** — 'Date of Report : 18/10/2024', listed under **July 28, 2026**. A
**21-month** lag."* One instance, generalised on the board into a property of the channel.

Measured tonight across **156 reports** (every entry in the 200-report sample carrying a parseable
report date), lag = publication date − date of the coroner's report:

| statistic | days |
|---|---|
| minimum | **0** (same day: *Tania Jarman*, report 2026-03-12, published 2026-03-12) |
| 25th percentile | 5 |
| **median** | **7.5** |
| 75th percentile | 56 |
| 90th percentile | 80 |
| 95th percentile | 148 |
| maximum | 1,529 (*John Moore*, report 2022-02-08, published 2026-04-17) |

- **66.7 %** (104/156) published within **14 days** of the coroner signing.
- **73.1 %** within 30 days · **81.4 %** within 60 · **92.3 %** within 90 · **96.2 %** within 180.
- Robin Ward's 21 months is in the **top 4 %** of the distribution, not its centre.

**The channel the gate declined on its clock has a median lag of seven and a half days.** The house's
own repeat failure — one instance generalised, then quoted back as verified — produced the ground on
which a channel that beats everything in the file on the material bar was set aside. Recorded here,
not silently patched: the board's line stands with this correction appended beside it.

## 3. THE SECOND RECORD — THE LIST OF THOSE WHO DID NOT ANSWER

The Chief Coroner publishes, twice a year, a table of the reports that were **never answered**:
<https://www.judiciary.uk/guidance-and-resources/non-responses-to-prevention-of-future-death-pfd-reports/>
(retrieved 2026-08-01; committed as `data/nonresponse-tables-2026-08-01.json`). Page text, verbatim:
*"These reports have been compiled after receiving confirmation from coroner's offices that these
responses have not been received. The reports are published every 6 months."*

Each row carries: **name of the deceased · date of report · coroner · coroner area · sent to ·
response due date.** As retrieved, four tables, **49 rows total**:

| period responses were due | published | rows |
|---|---|---|
| 13 Dec 2025 – 14 Jun 2026 | 30 June 2026 | **3** |
| 14 Jun 2025 – 12 Dec 2025 | 31 Dec 2025 | **9** |
| 14 Dec 2024 – 13 Jun 2025 | 30 June 2025 | **12** |
| 1 Jan 2024 – 13 Dec 2024 | 31 Dec 2024 | **25** |

Recipients named more than once across the 49 rows (counted; multi-recipient cells split on `|`):
**Ministry of Justice 7 · HMPPS 5 · College of Policing 3 · Royal College of Psychiatrists 3 ·
Bradford Council 2 · HMP Wandsworth 2.** Every other named body appears once.

**The table is alive, and it forgets.** The judiciary's own page states, verbatim:

> "The names of organisations or individuals who do not respond within the statutory timeframe (or any
> approved extension) may be published on this webpage… **Entries are removed once a response is
> received.**"
> — <https://www.judiciary.uk/courts-and-tribunals/coroners-courts/reports-to-prevent-future-deaths/>
> (retrieved 2026-08-01)

**INFERENCE (marked, and not a finding):** because entries leave when answered, the row counts above
are a snapshot of what is *outstanding on 2026-08-01*, not a time series of how often the duty is
missed; and the oldest table carrying the most rows is consistent with — but does **not** prove — a
population of reports that were never going to be answered at all. Establishing either would require
observation this house has not done. **What is certain from the page's own sentence:** when a body
finally answers, the public record of its having been late **disappears**, and nothing on the site
records that it was ever there.

Context from secondary literature, cited as secondary and **not** relied on: the Chief Coroner
published the first such list on 1 January 2025 and it has been reported under the phrase *"badge of
dishonour"* (e.g. Bevan Brittan LLP, <https://www.bevanbrittan.com/insights/articles/2025/non-responses-to-prevention-of-future-deaths-pfd-reports/>).
Judiciary guidance itself states the opposite framing — that a PFD report *is not a criticism or a
badge of dishonour*. Any use of the phrase must be attributed to its source, never to the Chief
Coroner.

## 4. WHAT A SINGLE UNIT LOOKS LIKE

One report page (the Naagi page above) carries: a reference (`2026-0271`), the date of the report, the
deceased's name, the coroner's name and area, the named recipient, the coroner's stated concern, the
duty sentence with **a named calendar date**, and — where one exists — a linked **response document**.
Where none exists, the page simply has no response on it.

So each unit of this channel arrives with **its own clock already set by the state**, its own named
addressee, and a public place where the answer either is or is not.

## 5. THE HONEST OBJECTIONS, STATED BY THE CONDUCTOR AGAINST THE MATERIAL

Handed to every voice convened tonight, unsoftened.

1. **This is a fourth-and-fifth institution's published PDFs.** The gate of session 55 killed a
   concept on *"I did not search the world for material. I searched for another supremecourt.gov."*
   A UK coroners' channel of published PDFs on an irregular cadence answers the letter of *"outside
   this institution"* and may not answer the charge. **Any proposal must argue the difference
   structurally or concede the point.**
2. **These are real dead people with real names, and named institutions accused by name of a
   statutory breach.** Legal hygiene binds hardest here: every claim about a named third party
   traceable to the primary source; opinion stated as opinion; criticism of method and standard, never
   of character. The bereaved are not material.
3. **The list shrinking (25 → 12 → 9 → 3) may mean the mechanism is working.** A work that stages it
   as damage without testing that reading has decided its finding before looking.
4. **The response documents exist.** Most reports are answered. A work built only on the silences is
   working on a minority of the corpus and must say so at the door.
5. **Nothing here is yet a form.** The season's standing question is unanswered by any of it: *what
   does the accumulation do that one unit does not?*
