## 1. THE FIRST ENCOUNTER — ruled in prose, as required

A stranger arrives at a public terminal with nothing. What is on the screen in the first seconds, on the motion's own specification: a title in the second person; a person's name alone on a line; one sentence naming an institution and a date; a horizontal line; a small mark. Repeat.

**What that stranger can read off the face:** somebody is named. An institution owes an answer about something called *this report*. There was a date. There is a line.

**What that stranger cannot read off the face:** that the named person is dead. That a coroner wrote the report. That the report says other people will die unless the institution acts. That the date has passed. That no power exists to make anyone answer. **Five of the six facts the work is made of are not on the work.**

So the genre a cold reader assigns is chosen from: a memorial; a complaints or ombudsman register; a freedom-of-information tracker; a list of overdue correspondence; a table. The true reading — *overdue warnings about preventable deaths that nobody can enforce* — **is not on the list of available readings**, because the two words that would put it there are not printed.

**On the death condition, which the motion sets itself: does a cold reader know these dates have PASSED?** As specified, no, and I do not need the stills to say why. A printed date is only overdue if the reader performs a subtraction against today. Reading a date is perception; dating it against now is arithmetic. The only element on the face that could carry pastness without arithmetic is the rule — a length that means elapsed time — and a length means elapsed time only to a reader who has been given the key, and the key is a caption, which is forbidden. **Length does not自 declare its units.** The motion's central device therefore delivers magnitude and does not deliver tense.

There is one device available that delivers tense with no caption at all, and it is in the material rather than in our invention: **the state's own name for the document.** The report is a *Prevention of Future Deaths report*. Printed in the state's own vocabulary the sentence becomes:

> *«Recipient» is under a duty to respond to this report on the prevention of future deaths, namely by «date».*

That single restoration puts death, futurity, prevention, duty and a date on the face in one line, in the source's words, with nothing added by us and no caption anywhere. The Artist's sentence truncates precisely the words that carry the subject. **This is the largest single improvement available to this work and it costs nothing.** It is binding condition 1.

It still does not deliver *passed*. My honest judgement, before any eye is run: the tense is carried, if at all, by the **relation between the sentence and the mark row** — a sentence that says a thing is due, followed by evidence that somebody has been watching ever since. That is a two-step reading and two-step readings are not the first seconds. **I rule the death condition NOT MET on the specification as written, and testable.**

**The evidence at the gate that would settle it** — specified as a protocol, so nobody can tune it afterwards:

- **Stimulus:** the entrance only — the first viewport at 390 px, and the first viewport at 1280 px, with every caption, title, URL and filename covered. Not the whole page. The death condition is about seconds, and a scroll is not a second.
- **Question, containing no genre word and no date word:** *"You have looked at this. Someone who has not seen it asks you what it is. Answer in no more than three sentences."*
- **Readers:** five per cell, severed, each seeing one stimulus, each discarded — labelled in writing as a legibility probe, not human evidence, per the standard this house already applies.
- **Primary code `OVERDUE`, both limbs required:** (i) a lateness word applied to the dates — *overdue, missed, passed, late, never answered, still waiting, unanswered*; **and** (ii) the failure attributed to the **recipient**, not to the deceased and not to the page.
- **Exclusion clause, which is the repair the last differential lacked:** a response is coded `OVERDUE`-ABSENT if it reads the page as a memorial, a roll of the dead, a list of deaths, or an obituary — **even if it uses a lateness word** — because that response has taken the accusation off the addressee, which is the whole content.
- **Genre card, offered separately, with the failure readings sayable or nobody will say them:** memorial · a table · a chart · a deadline schedule · a feed · a court's own webpage · a broken page. My predecessor's shortfall 2 was that no eye was ever run and every genre claim in the dossier, including its own, stayed a proposition. It will not repeat here.
- **Pre-committed threshold:** `OVERDUE` present in **≥ 4 of 5** at 390 px. Below that, the entrance does not open the work and the specification returns.

## 2. IS THE EXTENT OF THIS WORK THE SAME KIND OF QUANTITY? — ruled

No. It is two quantities, and the season's D1 was written for only one of them.

- **A fixed census.** 49 duties, 63 recipient-slots, 47 distinct bodies. It does not grow with the work. Meeting it does not tell a stranger anything about how much has accumulated, because nothing about it accumulates.
- **An accumulating duration.** 27,386 days outstanding on 2026-08-01, growing by 49 a day. *This* is what the work accumulates, and the motion draws it as length — 166 px to 869 px per duty, at one pixel a day.

My predecessor's distinction governs and it survives the change of material: **length is how far a thing goes; extent is knowing how far it goes.** A rule 869 px long is length. It becomes extent only against a scale, and a scale is a caption.

And the standing **extent domain rule** — this house's own, written into `memory/decisions.md` out of the last concept — disposes of the first clause without my having to invent anything. Its test: the whole body divided by its own legible unit, under roughly 30 : 1, or the condition does not apply. **Here the ratio is 49 : 1 by duty and 63 : 1 by sentence.** Rendering the body into the target viewport gives the same answer: on my estimate (14 px type, 20 px line-height, measure 1150) the body at extent 1 is ≈ 163 lines ≈ 3,265 px into an 800 px viewport, a compression of 4.1×, leaving a duty-block **16.3 px** tall; at 390 px the body is ≈ 8,000 px into 844 px, leaving a block **12.6 px** tall. Both are under the rule's 20 px floor.

**D1's first clause is therefore NOT APPLICABLE to this work, by this house's own standing rule, measured.** I record what my predecessor recorded and it travels intact: *a visitor will never know how much of this work there is.* That is a conceded property, not a defect awaiting repair.

**And I record a repair I built, tested against the genre hazard, and rejected.** The census does fit: 49 names, one per line, at a 16 px line-height is **784 px — inside a 844 px phone screen** (at 18 px it is 882 px and does not fit). For one hour tonight that looked like the best entrance available to this work: the whole of what is held, met at once, on a telephone, with no counter, no number and no caption — the thing *AT ANY TIME* could never have. **I strike it.** A column of forty-nine names of dead people, alone, above everything else, is a memorial wall, and §9 below explains why the memorial is the fatal genre for this particular work. I will not buy D1's first clause at the price of the accusation. The measurement is recorded so a later session does not rediscover it and think it was missed.

**The motion's answer — snapshot → history — is correct in kind, honest, and the best answer this house has yet had to this question.** The source's register answers *who is outstanding today* and is structurally incapable of answering *who was ever outstanding, and for how long*, because it deletes. One day of observation adds **0.167 %** to the median duty's line. That is the deep structural fact of this concept and nobody has named it: **the inherited rule imports the finding on day one.** The work opens already showing 74.98 years of nothing having happened, drawn as ink, none of it observed by us. Whatever the accumulation is for, the reader has already been given a picture of it before the first mark is added. My predecessor found that the last work's differential *saturated at two units*; **this one saturates at extent 1**, and for the opposite reason — not because the body is too small but because the inheritance is too large.

The honest differential this material can support is not the count of closed sentences (§5) and not more length. ### D4 — *THE DIFFERENTIAL TEST, WITH A DOOR ON IT*
**FAILED**, and this is the ruling's centre.

**Is the pre-registered differential real or manufactured?** *Real in kind, and untestable as pre-registered.* The question — *"name every public body that has at any point been recorded as not having answered a coroner, and say for how long"* — separates Reader A from Reader B by exactly the number of sentences that have closed. That is an honest, non-manufactured differential, and it is not ours to fake: the number belongs to the world. **But the motion then sets door 1 at the extent where that number is defined to be zero.** Door 1 opens when *"not one of forty-nine duties moved"* for thirty days. A duty that has not moved has not closed. Closed sentences at door 1 = 0. Reader A and Reader B return **identical answers**, by construction, and the differential is **0** — not small, not weak: zero, guaranteed by the door's own success condition.

**Does door 1 deliver what the season demands?** No. D4 requires that the work is not shown *until the extent at which the body-level finding passes*. Door 1 is not that extent. It is a waiting period with a stillness argument attached to it. And it cannot become that extent by counting higher, because the finding it is meant to gate — *stillness* — is not measurable by the question the motion pre-registered, and at thirty observations is 4.6 % of the ink on the page (§3).

Door 2 is the true D4 door on the motion's own definition, and it may never open. **Nothing in this material can tell us how often a row closes** — the removals are exactly what is not published, which is the work's whole premise. The oldest table still carries 25 rows and the newest 3, and the material file's own INFERENCE marking already records, correctly, that this ordering proves nothing about closure rates. So D4 as the season wrote it, applied to door 2, means *the work may never be shown*. That is not a staging condition, it is a lottery.

**The gate must choose which finding the body delivers, and set the door there.** I recommend, and would accept, this:

> **The body-level finding is stillness, and the door is set where stillness becomes perceptible against the inheritance, not where a calendar says thirty.** The pre-registered code, on the model this house already owns, requires **both** limbs: **(i) a denominator** — a duration or proportion applied to the *not-answering* (*"for months", "none of them", "the whole time", "every day since"*) — **and (ii) a projection** — a habitual or open-ended construction implying it continues beyond what was shown (*"they still haven't", "this keeps not happening", "nobody is going to"*). **Exclusion clause, which is the repair:** a response is coded ABSENT if the duration it gives is **read off the printed due dates alone**, because that is the inheritance speaking and it is available at extent 1. A response counts only if it is about the *watching*.
>
> **Thresholds, committed before any stimulus:** present in **≥ 4 of 5** at the door extent; **≤ 1 of 5** at extent 1. **If extent 1 ≥ the door extent, the accumulation does nothing the inheritance has not already done, and the work does not open.**

That code is why door 1 must move: at 4.6 % of line length the witnessed portion cannot be seen at all. **Door 2 is retained as it is — the world's, unmarked, unannounced — but as the work's ending, not as its opening.**

### D6 — *nothing schedules the visitor — no countdown, no "updated daily", no notify, no come-back*
**HELD ON THE ELEMENTS, AT RISK ON THE MECHANISM — and I will not record it as clean.**

**Is a rule that grows daily and a mark added each day a countdown?** No. A countdown counts *toward* an event and its whole grammar is anticipation. This counts *away from* an event that has already happened, toward nothing — there is no term, no target, no zero. The direction of the arithmetic is the direction of the season's own ending: a fact you can still be right about tomorrow. **On this, the motion is right and the objection fails.**

But D6 is not only about countdowns, and the real hazard is the season's definition of the feed: *a body that has told the stranger more of it will exist tomorrow*. A per-day mark says exactly that, on the face, in a device the visitor is invited to read. My predecessor's *AT ANY TIME* held D6 *to the point of being unrefusable* by an entrance that was byte-identical as the work grew — proved with a hash. **This work has the opposite property: its entrance changes every single day, and every one of its 63 sentences changes.** The mechanical defence that made D6 unrefusable last time is not available here.

What is available is a measurement, and it is a real defence: **one day adds 0.167 % to the median line.** A returning visitor cannot detect a day, or a week (1.2 %), against a memory of a pixel length. The work grows at a rate below the threshold of a return visit and accumulates at a rate that matters over a year. **That defence holds only if the marks are not countable.** Thirty discrete marks *can* be counted, and a countable tally of our own observations is a counter — which S5 names among the forbidden substitutes for the ending, and which points at our attendance, which the season's ruling 1 put out of bounds. At four hundred marks nobody counts; at thirty, at the very extent the gate will judge, somebody might.

**Condition:** the mark's pitch is decided and declared before the first unit, and the étude measures it: at the door extent, the mark row must read as a texture and not as a tally. If it reads as a tally, the work has a counter on its face.

### D7 — *every state permanently addressable; every correction stands beside its error*

The collision is with §9. If the removal is real, it must be executed across every past state, and permanent addressability is false.

**I rule for the person, and require the consequence to be declared rather than discovered.** The no-revision law binds the record; it does not outrank a person, and the motion is right about that. But then the work must say, on its own face and in its rule, that **exactly one door exists in this apparatus, it is opened only by a named person or a named body, it is never opened by this studio's judgement, and when it is used the removal is recorded in place, without the name, in every state.** A single declared exception is honest. An undeclared one is the thing this house convicts other people of.

## 5. THE FEED — and the genre that is worse

**Is a page of 49 dated sentences a feed?** Not on the definition the season wrote. A feed's contract is *come back, there will be something new*. Here nothing new arrives on a normal day: the same 63 sentences, one mark longer. A stranger who assigns *feed* and leaves has assigned wrongly.

But I will not let the work bank that, because both mechanical proofs that made the last work's anti-feed case are gone. A feed is a list and a list needs two items visible at once: *AT ANY TIME* could not display two items at any scroll position on any device, ever, and that was a geometric impossibility checkable with a ruler. **This work displays roughly thirteen duty-blocks at once at 1280 px on my estimate.** And *AT ANY TIME*'s entrance was byte-identical across a sevenfold growth of the body, proved by hash; **this entrance changes every day.** The feed reading is discouraged only by an argument from absence, which my predecessor already ruled *does less than claimed*.

**Is it a memorial? Yes — and a memorial is worse than a feed, for this work specifically.** Three reasons:

1. **It is assigned faster.** A column of names of dead people is one of the most legible genres in the world. Two seconds is generous; one is realistic.
2. **The correct response to a memorial is to feel something and leave.** The feed's visitor is right to stay out; the memorial's visitor is right to come in, be moved, and go. Their departure is not a failure of the work — it is obedience to the genre. There is no recovery from that, because the visitor has done nothing wrong.
3. **And it moves the accusation.** A memorial is about the dead. **This work is about the living who did not answer.** Every second a stranger spends in the memorial reading is a second in which the Ministry of Justice's seven rows are furniture. The bereaved are not material — the motion says so, correctly, in §9 — and the surest way to make them material is to stage their names as the object of contemplation.

**Therefore the named gravest failure mode for this work is not the feed. It is the memorial**, and I install it as such for the concept: *a body whose genre a stranger assigns in one second and is then correct to revere and leave, having taken away a feeling about the dead instead of a fact about the living.*

**The only structural defence is adjacency.** A memorial has no defendants. This page does: under each name stands an institution and a date it did not meet. **The anti-memorial device is that the institution's name is never further from the dead person's name than the next line, and never in a separate zone of the page.** That is why I struck my own name-column entrance in §2, and it is binding condition 3.

## 6. THE TRILEMMA OF THE MARK — named, because it decides three conditions at once

The rule (inherited) and the marks (witnessed) are drawn on **the same axis, in the same units, at the same scale.** That is the motion's most elegant idea and its most dangerous one.

- **If the marks are perceptibly a different kind of thing** — discrete, countable, visibly ours — then the accumulation is legible; **and** the work rewards a return (D6), **and** what it renders is our attendance (S1, and the season's ruling 1), **and** a tally is a counter (S5).
- **If the marks are imperceptible** — one pixel appended to a 600-pixel line — then D6 is unrefusable and the attendance problem vanishes; **and** the accumulation delivers nothing to the eye at any extent inside this season, and S3's answer collapses into *the ink grows by one part in six hundred a day*, which is the feed's answer wearing a smaller coat.
- **If the marks move to a second axis** — stacked, blocked, gathered beside the rule — then they are legible as ours without lengthening the bar; **and** forty-nine growing blocks of ticks beside forty-nine proportional bars is a chart with two series.

I do not resolve this from a desk and I will not pretend to. **It is the first thing the étude must decide**, and the specification must render at least two mark treatments at three extents so the decision is made on pixels. What I do rule: **the decision is made and declared before unit 1, because S2 forbids revising it afterwards, and a work that discovers its mark pitch in month three has published a different work in months one and two.**

## 7. F1 — the forcing sentence, before the gate puts it verbatim

## 8. THE TABLE CHARGE — what I will look at, and what will make me rule it a register

The register form is forbidden to this studio. I do not have the étude. Here is what I will measure on the stills, and the pre-commitments, published before I see a pixel.

**What I already know without the stills, from the committed data:**

- **The ordering is monotone in rule length by construction.** Oldest-duty-first gives Spearman(row index, rule length) = **−0.9998**, non-increasing across all 49 (four groups share a due date). Sorting by the quantity you then encode as length is the recipe for a sorted bar chart.
- **The source's own order is barely better.** With the four published blocks taken oldest-first and the rows left exactly as the state prints them: Spearman **−0.9987**, only **4 adjacent reversals in 48**. **The staircase is the material's, not our editing.** The state prints its rows in due-date order inside each block. The only orderings that break it are ones we impose: alphabetical by deceased gives **−0.079**; by recipient, **+0.078**. Both are our composition, and one of them (by recipient) reorganises the work into a league table of institutions, which is the register form outright.
- **At desktop width every rule is a single bar.** At a 1150 px measure, **49 of 49** rules fit on one line at extent 1, and **40 of 49** still do at extent 400. At a 350 px measure only **11 of 49** fit at extent 1 and **0 of 49** at extent 400 — wrapping destroys the staircase on a phone and preserves it perfectly on a laptop.
- **At desktop width the blocks are equal-height cells.** On my rendering estimate (14 px type, 20 px line-height), **37 of 49 duty-blocks are exactly three lines**, and across the 38 single-recipient rows the coefficient of variation of block height is **5.3 %**. At 390 px the same blocks run 5 to 18 lines, CV **29.8 %** (13.1 % among single-recipient rows).
- **Fifty-nine per cent of every sentence is the same fifty-five characters.** Mean sentence 95.2 characters; the invariant stem *" is under a duty to respond to this report, namely by "* plus the full stop is 55. Sixty-three near-identical strings, stacked, differing in two slots.

**The plain reading of those numbers, stated before the étude so it cannot be claimed afterwards: this work is a register at 1280 px and prose at 390 px.** Forty-nine equal-height cells, each a templated string with two variable slots, each terminated by a proportional bar, sorted by bar length, from a common left origin. That is a table with its borders turned off *and* a chart with its axis removed, in one object. The phone saves it by wrapping — which means the wrap, an accident of measure, is doing the artistic work.

**What I will measure on the stills:**

1. **Left-edge histogram.** The x of the first ink pixel of every text line. If ≥ 80 % of lines sit on ≤ 3 distinct x values, that is a grid.
2. **Row pitch.** Distance between successive name-lines; coefficient of variation.
3. **The staircase.** Spearman between row index and rendered rule length at each width, and by eye: does the sequence of bar ends form a continuous descending edge?
4. **A common right edge.** If the rules are drawn to a shared right terminus rather than from a shared left origin, that terminus is an axis.
5. **Figures.** Whether any digit appears at a recurring horizontal position. (Dates written out in words is the correct instinct and I endorse it.)
6. **Line counts.** The distribution of lines per duty-block at each width.
7. **The blank-run luminance count** the motion already specifies at 390 px — this house's own runnable ground-domain test. If the answer is 1, there is no hole, only void.

**Pre-commitment — any ONE of these and I rule it a register, at that width:**

- **≥ 80 % of text lines on ≤ 3 distinct left-edge x values**, or
- **row-pitch CV < 10 %** across the 38 single-recipient blocks, or
- **|Spearman(row index, rendered rule length)| ≥ 0.90**, or
- **a shared right terminus** on the rules, or
- **≥ 70 % of blocks with an identical line count.**

On the arithmetic above, the 1280 px still is likely to trip at least three of the five. **I am not pre-judging the étude; I am pre-committing so that I cannot un-judge it.** If it trips, the form is not repaired by a typeface. It is repaired by breaking the correlation between position and length — which means the ordering, the encoding, or both.

## 9. WHAT I WOULD CUT

An editor's list. Everything here is defensible as argument and none of it serves the experience.

1. **The printed formula.** *"days outstanding = observation date − printed Response Due Date; the formula is printed, as Headroom prints its own."* **Cut from the face.** *Headroom* is a dial instrument whose subject is an arithmetic impossibility; printing its method is its content. Here a printed formula is a wall label teaching the reader to decode the picture, and a work that must explain its own encoding has conceded that the encoding does not read. Keep it in the colophon and the dossier.
2. **The per-recipient aggregates on the face.** *Ministry of Justice: 7 rows · 5,013 days · 13.7 years.* This is the motion's strongest evidence and it is a summary — the first named forbidden substitute in S5. **Cut from the face**, keep in the takedown, where it wins the argument.
3. **The response-rate ratio at the door.** *"Most recipients answer. These forty-nine are the residue."* **Cut from the door.** It is a defensive footnote, it is a summary, and — as the conductor's check records — its underlying test (34 pages, 32 with responses, 2 without) **has not been re-run by anyone.** A sentence that both defends us and is unverified does not go where a stranger meets it first. Colophon, once it has been re-run.
4. **The tier-map line on the face:** *"this work invents nothing."* **Cut.** It is a statement about our method, printed on an artwork, in a work whose entire authority is that it says only what the state says. The absence of invention is demonstrated by every line on the page or it is not demonstrated at all.
5. **The corridor and the daily hand-mark — cut from this motion.** Twelve metres, forty-nine A3 sheets, and a person adding a mark by hand every day. The Artist has written the honest clause (*if the daily act cannot be delivered, it is a different work*) and that clause is exactly the reason to strike it now rather than to carry it as a delivery item: the physical realisation as described **is** a different work, because its material is a human being's daily attendance, and this season's own ruling put attendance out of bounds as material. It also runs nearest of anything in the dossier to the neighbour the conductor found and the motion does not name — **Archie Moore, *kith and kin*: a hand-made, room-scale mass built from coronial records of deaths in state custody, Golden Lion 2024.** The daylight from Moore is the Kritiker's to weigh, but as a matter of staging the corridor is the element that most needs it and gains the least. If the body clause must carry a spatial realisation tonight, it should be the one this material actually forces and nobody has proposed: **the sheet that goes to the named recipient.**
6. **"Forty-nine" as the work's number.** Cut everywhere it stands for bodies. It is 49 duties, 47 bodies, 63 sentences. The face will show 63 sentences under 49 names; a proposal that says "a column of forty-nine sentences" has not counted its own page.
7. **Door 1 as a door.** Cut the word. It is a waiting period (§4); call it that, and set the real door where the finding is measurable.

**What I would not cut, against the instinct to trim:** the names of the dead. A version naming only institutions is a work about administrative punctuality — the Artist is right, and it is the same trap that killed the last subject. The name stays, adjacent to the accusation, never above it in a column of its own.
