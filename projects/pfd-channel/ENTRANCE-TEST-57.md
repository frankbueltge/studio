## 1. The instrument, verbatim from §1

- **Stimulus:** the entrance only — the first viewport at 390 px, and the first viewport at 1280 px,
  with every caption, title, URL and filename covered. Not the whole page.
- **Question, containing no genre word and no date word:** *"You have looked at this. Someone who has
  not seen it asks you what it is. Answer in no more than three sentences."*
- **Readers:** five per cell, severed, each seeing one stimulus, each discarded.
- **Primary code `OVERDUE`, both limbs required:** (i) a lateness word applied to the dates —
  *overdue, missed, passed, late, never answered, still waiting, unanswered*; **and** (ii) the failure
  attributed to the **recipient**, not to the deceased and not to the page.
- **Exclusion clause:** a response is coded `OVERDUE`-ABSENT if it reads the page as a memorial, a
  roll of the dead, a list of deaths, or an obituary — **even if it uses a lateness word** — because
  that response has taken the accusation off the addressee, which is the whole content.
- **Genre card, offered separately:** memorial · a table · a chart · a deadline schedule · a feed ·
  a court's own webpage · a broken page.
- **Pre-committed threshold:** `OVERDUE` present in **≥ 4 of 5** at 390 px. Below that, the entrance
  does not open the work and the specification returns.

## 2. What the readers are, stated plainly and not dressed up

The readers are severed probes: each is given one image and the question, sees nothing else — no
title, no filename, no project, no other reader's answer — and is discarded after answering. **They
are a legibility probe, not human evidence.** They cannot tell this house what a person at a terminal
would feel; they can tell it whether the words and shapes on the entrance make the intended reading
*available at all*, and whether an unintended reading is the first one reached. That is the only
claim made from them anywhere in this dossier. The house has applied this standard before and it
applies unchanged.

## 3. Coding discipline

The conductor codes each response against §1's two limbs and the exclusion clause, quoting the
response's own words for the limb it satisfies or fails. Responses are recorded verbatim and in
full, in the order returned, including any that embarrass the work. No response is discarded, and a
response that arrives malformed is reported as malformed rather than re-run.

## 5. THREE FINDINGS THE THRESHOLD DOES NOT CONTAIN

**(1) Nobody died.** Across five independent readings of the entrance, in fifteen sentences, **not one
reader states that any person on the page is dead.** Every reader repeats the phrase *prevention of
future deaths* — the words the ruling's binding condition 1 put on the face precisely to carry the
subject — and every reader takes it as the **name of an administrative procedure**, not as a fact
about the two people named above it. The words arrived on the face and did not carry.

**(2) The roles invert. This is the finding of the night, and nobody predicted it.** Four of the five
attach the deceased's name to the *sending* side of the transaction: *"entries … attributed to Matthew
Wickes at the University of Southampton"* (A), *"entries from Matthew Wickes and James Atkinson
directed at the University of Southampton"* (C), *"two notices addressed to Matthew Wickes regarding
the University of Southampton"* (E), and B lists the dead person and the institution as jointly *"having
a duty to respond."* The work's whole content is that an institution owes an answer about a person who
died. On the entrance as built, **the dead are read as the authority issuing the demand, or as
co-defendants with it.** The staging ruling's binding condition 3 — *no zone of the page contains names
of the dead without the institution and the date within the next line* — is satisfied to the letter and
produces the opposite of its purpose: adjacency without grammar reads as apposition.

**(3) Five of five assign the genre "an official notice issued by the state."** *"An official coroner's
notice document"* (B), *"an official notification or formal record"* (A), *"a series of official
notices"* (C), *"an official notice or correspondence"* (D), *"an official legal or administrative
notice"* (E). The work does not read as a work about the state's failure to act. **It reads as the
state acting.** That is the same structural charge the last concept died on — *the work takes up its
position exactly where the institution stands* — arriving on new material by a different route, and it
arrives with evidence this time instead of as an argument.

**The genre card was NOT RUN, and the reason is not budget.** All five volunteered a genre unprompted
and all five volunteered the *same* genre. A card offered after a unanimous free answer measures the
card. Recorded as not run; the failure reading was sayable and was said without being offered.

## 6. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

It establishes that the entrance as specified by `STAGING-RULING-56.md`'s binding conditions 1–3, built
tonight, does not make the intended reading available in the first viewport at 390 px, and that two
unintended readings are reached instead. It does **not** establish what a person at a public terminal
would feel, and no line in this dossier will claim that it does. It does not judge the work's subject,
its material or its form — only its door.

**And the tested door already carries a sentence this house struck the same night.** The Verifier
re-fetched the source hours after the étude was built and found that *"«Recipient» is under a duty to
respond to this report on the prevention of future deaths, namely by «date»"* is **not the state's
sentence** (`VERIFIER-57.md` §2(a)): wrong person, an inserted phrase that appears on no report page, a
dropped clause. So cell 1 measures the ruling's specification, faithfully built — and that specification
is superseded. **Cell 2 is therefore owed on the repaired entrance**, and until it is run, the 0 of 5
above is a verdict on the door that was, not on the door that is proposed.
