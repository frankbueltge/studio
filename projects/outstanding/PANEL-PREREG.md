# OUTSTANDING — severed-reader panel, pre-registered

**Written and frozen 2026-08-30, session 114, before a single frame was cut and before a
single reader was dispatched.** Sealed with `tools/prereg.py`; the seal and this file's hash
are in `PANEL-PREREG.md.frozen`. Evidence, not process record — session 76's standing ruling
(`tools/record-files.txt`), so it does not count against the project's 3,000-word ceiling.
This house's own reading of what comes back is process record and lives in tonight's journal.

## Which limb of the constitution this answers

PROTOCOL §"Legible — ours to run": *before a work ships, severed readers meet it cold and are
asked what they understood and what they take it to be about. Their answers are published
beside the work, unedited, including the ones that miss it entirely. A work whose severed
readers can say nothing back does not ship.*

OUTSTANDING has never faced it. Six sessions have gone into its plumbing and its face; the
hold on it is a hosting decision that is not ours. This limb is ours, it is owed before ship,
and it can kill.

## What the readers get, and what the strip cannot carry

Frames from **one continuous watch of the live room** on the night of 2026-08-30, against the
live national record with the relay cycling beside it, captured by `tools/panel.mjs` at
1920×1080 every 30 seconds. Two strips of **20 consecutive frames** are cut from that one
watch — ten minutes of real room-time each, the length of a short visit.

Each reader is shown their 20 frames in order and told only this: they are photographs of one
screen, taken 30 seconds apart, in order, over ten minutes; the screen was moving between
them. Nothing else. No title, no wall label, no subject, no country, no mention of weather,
forecast, promise or verification. The frames carry no text of their own.

**Three channels of this work do not survive the strip, and that is stated here rather than
discovered later:**

1. **Motion.** Every mark this work makes is an animation — a flare rising over 0.4 s, a sweep
   crossing a node in 1.6 s, a ring blooming over 2.2 s. At 30-second sampling a reader sees
   afterglow (decay constants 26 s for a lock, 34 s for a rupture, 55 s for a broken silence)
   and never the arrival. The loudest thing this work does is invisible to this panel.
2. **Sound.** A tone accompanies every settlement and carries the verdict's distinction. No
   reader hears anything. The concept says the sound is *what lets the piece work at the
   room's real viewing distance with no text*; the panel tests the piece with that removed.
3. **Scale and the visitor's hand.** The medium is a projection seen from metres away; a
   reader sees a screenshot. The held node — the one gesture that belongs to the visitor —
   cannot be reached from a still and is not tested here at all.

A panel that carried all three would be a visitor, and this house cannot put a visitor in a
room it does not have. What is being tested is therefore the still field and its change over
ten minutes, which is the part that can be carried honestly.

## The two cohorts, and the rule that picks them — stated before the cut

Session 113 measured that **a ten-minute visit sees no settlement at all 27.1 % of the time**,
and this house's Artist then decided that the work **owes such a visitor nothing**, because
the room knows exactly as much in a trough as in a flare. That decision was reasoned. It has
never been put to anyone outside this house. So the panel splits:

- **Cohort A — a strip that carries settlements.** The ten-minute window of the watch with the
  **most settlement flares** (`lock`, `rupture`, `silence`), by the room's own event log.
- **Cohort B — a trough.** The **first** ten-minute window of the same watch containing **zero**
  settlements; if any such window also has zero frames showing afterglow, that one instead.

Three readers each. Both strips come from the same night, the same room, the same watch, the
same instrument, and are cut by `tools/panel.mjs --cut` from a window list printed by
`--pick`, so the choice is a consequence of the rule above and not of a conductor's taste.

## The three questions, verbatim, and nothing else is asked

1. *What do you see? Describe it.*
2. *What do you take this to be about?*
3. *Did anything change across the ten minutes? If so, what?*

Plus one instruction: *If you cannot say, say so plainly — "I can't tell" is a real answer
here and will be published as written.*

No follow-ups, no hints, no second pass. Every answer is published unedited in
`PANEL-ANSWERS.md`, including the ones that miss the work entirely.

## What this house predicts, before seeing a word of it

Written to be wrong. Six readers, three per cohort.

1. **All six describe a field of points, and most read it as a map.** The nodes are placed by
   geography and the silhouette is a country's. Predicted: **5 or 6 of 6** say map, country,
   territory or constellation of places.
2. **None of the six reaches the subject.** Predicted: **0 of 6** arrive at anything like a
   promise, a forecast, a claim being checked, a prediction being scored, or accountability.
   Nothing in the pixels says *promise*; that word arrives through the sound, the motion and
   the wall label, and the panel carries none of them. This is the prediction most likely to
   be embarrassing and it is registered anyway.
3. **The commonest wrong reading is infrastructure.** Predicted: a majority read it as network
   monitoring, server status, air traffic, seismic activity or telescope data — a dashboard of
   a system, not a record of statements.
4. **Cohort A reports change; cohort B reports stillness.** Predicted: **2 or 3 of 3** in
   cohort A name something appearing and fading at particular places; **2 or 3 of 3** in
   cohort B say little or nothing changed.
5. **No reader in either cohort says the screen is broken, frozen or dead.** The field holds
   forty thousand standing claims and redraws them; if a reader calls a trough a malfunction,
   that is a finding against the Artist's decision of 2026-08-29 and will be reported as one.

## How the answers will be read, decided in advance

- **The constitutional bar (kills).** If a majority of readers can say **nothing back** — no
  content beyond an inability to describe what is in front of them — the work **does not
  ship**, and this house says so plainly rather than re-running the panel.
- **Missing the subject is not a kill.** The constitution asks that the misses be published,
  not that they be absent. If prediction 2 holds, the finding is that the still field alone
  does not carry the claim, and what this house does about it is a decision for a later
  session, taken in the open.
- **The cohort difference is a finding either way,** and it is the only evidence outside this
  house's own reasoning about the 27.1 % of visits that see nothing settle.
- **Three readers per cohort measures nothing rate-like.** It can catch a gross difference and
  an inability to speak. It cannot say what a fraction of visitors would report, and no
  sentence written from it will claim otherwise.
