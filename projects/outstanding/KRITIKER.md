# KRITIKER — OUTSTANDING — 2026-08-21

**HELD.**

Not a soft death. The concept clears my bar on its own merits and is blocked by one thing only: a
relay on the work's own origin that the house cannot build.

**The claim.** That a public promise about rain is standing unresolved right now, everywhere, and
that the sky is settling it while you stand there. It is a real claim, it has a truth value, and it
is stated in a unit a stranger already owns.

**The machine advantage, and whether it is perceived.** Verification at scale, joined to the
temporal. Roughly 42,900 open periods and 15,100 stated probabilities standing nationally at any
moment, each checked the instant checking becomes possible, for as long as the room stays open. This
is the first concept of the six where the advantage is *visible as breadth*: hundreds of places lit
and independently live at once is a thing no pair of hands produces by refreshing pages one at a
time. The visitor does not have to be told the count. They see it.

**The minute-twenty test, and my ruling on the hour-delayed feed.** The fifth concept did not die
because its facts existed in the world before the room opened — every settled fact does. It died
because **the work possessed the answers at open**: 401 KiB shipped beside the page, playback at a
viewing speed. Possession, not chronology, is what that test forbids. Under an hour-delayed feed
nothing possesses the verdict at open — not the relay, not the machine, not the institution's own
public channel. It becomes obtainable during the visit. **A delayed feed is not a replay, because
nothing is in hand to replay.** Beyond that, one channel here is unknowable in the strongest sense
available: offices re-draft on their own clocks — tonight's eight sampled issuances span 06:22 to
13:47 UTC — and a bulletin written at 14:30 did not exist at 14:00 in any form anywhere. **The test
passes.**

But it passes only above a cadence. At the Artist's stated hard floor of sixty minutes, a
twenty-minute visit can contain no refresh at all: the page holds one file, fixed at the moment the
visitor walked in, and everything that unfolds is timed animation over a settled file. *That is the
fifth death, re-created by a configuration parameter.* The floor is not the Artist's to set at sixty.

**The form floor.** Cleared without argument: generative visual behaviour, sound carrying a
distinction rather than a mood, time-based behaviour, live data running while watched, optional
interaction. Unattended, no caption, nobody explaining.

**The neighbours.** The discriminating act is ours. Jacobs stood at this door with these materials
and chose not to open it; *Cumulus* states nothing that can be wrong; Polli sonifies weather as
immersion. The industry aggregates monthly and privately; the institution audits itself behind a
federal login. Nobody settles the plain-language forecast claim-by-claim, live, in public. I note the
non-art search's warning that this is weak safety — a gap unclaimed for want of reason, not
difficulty. Weak safety is still safety. Priority is priority.

**(a) The flare field as the fourth death in new clothes.** Rejected. The fourth died because a
room's mood was mapped from a dataset. A flare here is a specific period whose window closed and
which the sky did or did not match — a consequence with a truth value, not a temperature. The tell is
in the road not taken: OVERWRITE's fallback when events went sparse was *our own fetch latency*.
OUTSTANDING's fallback is a real inventory of open claims. One is atmosphere; the other is a held
state.

**(b) The caption problem moved, not dissolved.** Sustained in part, and it is the sharpest of the
three. Rings and hue are the work's private notation, and a stranger owning the forecast does not
thereby own the notation. What saves it is that the notation is taught by demonstration, not by
legend: a dim band that flares teaches every other dim band what it is. That mechanism costs nothing
but requires the flares to arrive early — which binds (b) to the cadence. It is not free, so I bind
it below.

**(c) Does it render the gradient or make room for it?** Makes room. A rate of 2.4 % against 13.9 %
is not perceivable inside one visit at any afterglow. It is perceivable across nights at the rim, and
that is the temporal limb earning its place. Kept — but on live settlements only.

**The road not taken.** I do not overrule. I would have killed OVERWRITE for the same second reason,
in stronger terms: a work whose defence against sparseness is the latency of its own fetching has
named itself. Note also that OVERWRITE's one real instrument, the diff, survives inside OUTSTANDING
as the re-issuance sweep — and is the channel that passes my test most cleanly.

## What binds the build

1. **Cadence: five minutes preferred, ten minutes hard. Sixty is refused.** At least two refreshes
   must land inside any twenty-minute visit.
2. **No archive-derived figure, ratio or weighting may be encoded into the live rendering.** The
   dossier states plainly that the finding has not been independently re-derived. Nothing
   un-re-derived goes in front of a stranger. The silence gradient must emerge from live settlements
   or not at all.
3. **The record's own sentence, verbatim, on the held node** — "chance of precipitation 20 percent",
   or the silence where it isn't. Not a caption, not a legend: the source. The one unit the stranger
   owns must be reachable in the room.
4. **Kept:** the visible dim band for silence, the concentric horizon rings, the rupture-versus-lock
   distinction in both light and timbre, the fading afterglow that refuses to become a tally.
5. **Milestone 5 must be evidenced against a genuinely dry national stretch, instrumented, not
   asserted.** *"The tension is the held state"* is a claim about a room nobody has stood in. Stand
   in it.

**What lifts the hold:** the relay exists on the work's own origin, two JSON files refreshed in place
at ten minutes or better. Nothing else. **If the team can offer only sixty, do not return for a
waiver — at sixty this is playback, and I will kill it.**
