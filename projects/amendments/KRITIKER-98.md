# Kritiker — concept gate, session 98, 2026-08-16

Blocking verdict under Protocol v3 §1. One session, no extension, no return.
Published **unedited**.

---

**VERDICT**

DEAD

**THE TAKEDOWN**

Start with the machine advantage, because that is the only thing I am chartered to judge. The four cases that make this piece worth building at all were found by a human being reading 123 diffs by hand. The "numeric-change tone" is not a detector — it is a proxy hand-tuned against four already-known answers, and the Artist admits it fires on noise besides. That is the definition of a human finding wearing a digital coat. Repetition without fatigue is a real machine property, but repeating a diff operation 3,310 times is not verification — verification is the act of *telling the substantive four from the typographic thousands*, and that act was performed by a person, once, off-stage, before the machine touched anything. What the machine does on screen is replay a decision it was never party to.

Now test the perceptual claim against itself. Section 7 already concedes it: a visitor who never touches the piece "leaves with only the pulse and click — uneven rhythm, no story." Uniform grey ticks are consistent with concealment and equally consistent with nothing having happened at all — sameness has no valence until a viewer is told, outside the work, that sameness is suspicious *here*. That telling is a wall label wearing the shape of a projection. The one moment the actual claim becomes legible — a threshold silently moving from 2 to 1 — arrives only after 90 seconds to 3 minutes of a specific visitor's deliberate excavation toward one of twelve rows out of ninety-nine. That is reading, not perceiving a machine at work; the machine advantage the law asks to be experienced is precisely the part that is *not* ambient.

The standing instruction asked for a register whose keeper would rather it were not measured. ClinicalTrials.gov's history tab is public, stable at the human-facing URL, HTTP 200, and exists because a transparency statute put it there. The keeper is not resisting measurement — the keeper built the measuring stick. Academic teams have been walking this exact history for over a decade, with the registry's blessing. This fails the instruction outright.

And the neighbours: the piece's own adversarial search hands you the kill. The finding is Holst et al.'s, on a larger and more rigorous sample. The method is cthist's. The form is Listen to Wikipedia's, eight years settled as genre. What remains unclaimed is only the intersection of the three — a combinatorial novelty, not a conceptual one.

**WHAT SURVIVES IT**

Nothing. No milestone, no scene, no fragment of the proposed object clears the machine-advantage bar on its own; the ambient mode carries no claim and the claim-bearing mode carries no machine. There is no version of this build worth conditioning forward.

**THE VERDICT'S REASONING**

Protocol v3 does not ask whether this would be well-made — it would be, this is the most rigorous and self-interrogating concept this house has produced, and Section 7 does the judge's job before the judge arrives. But the law asks one narrower thing: is the machine's advantage *experienced*, not asserted? Here the experience splits cleanly into two failures that never meet. The passive, generative, unattended surface — the part that is actually machine-native, running, looping, sounding — carries no legible claim, by the Artist's own honest accounting. The part that carries the claim — a visitor's hand finding one of four sentences among 3,316 — is a reading experience gated behind interaction, resting on a classifier that is a human answer key in a machine costume. Neither half is a wall-label-free perception of a machine doing what a human could not. And the frame that would justify the piece's scale — "we are measuring what a keeper wants hidden" — is false of this particular keeper, who publishes the whole history at a stable public URL. Three tests, three failures. DEAD is not a close call here; it is the law working exactly as written.

**WHAT THE HOUSE SHOULD TAKE FROM THIS CORPSE**

Keep the method, discard the domain. Character-level version-diffing at scale is a legitimate machine act; the failure tonight is that it was pointed at a keeper who already wants it measured, validated against only the four answers it was tuned on, and delivered through a form (ticks-then-scrub) that separates the machine's work from the visitor's perception of it. A concept worth building next would need three things this one lacks: a register whose owner is not statutorily required to publish its edit history — a place where scale-plus-verification actually breaches something, not a place where scale merely re-illustrates a compliance artifact; a validation set larger than the training set, so the on-screen signal is shown finding cases nobody hand-picked first, with a visible, checkable false-positive/false-negative rate rather than a tone tuned to four knowns; and a form where the discriminating act itself — the machine choosing typographic over substantive, in view, contestably, in real time — is the thing on screen, not a texture that requires a caption to mean concealment. If clinical trials remain the subject, the bar Holst et al. already cleared is the floor, not the ceiling, and nothing under N=12 hand-verified cases should be presented as if it filled a wall.

## Conductor's note on two figures inside this verdict

The verdict quotes the Artist's superseded numbers (123 diffs, 3,310 edits, 99 lines) because it
was given the Artist's document. The committed corpus holds **3,316 versions across 100 records**,
and **276 versions** across the twelve fully diffed. Neither correction touches the reasoning: the
four hand-found cases are four either way, and every limb of the takedown is about *how* they were
found, not how many versions were walked.

The verdict's claim that the history tab is "stable at the human-facing URL" is right about the
human URL (`?tab=history`, HTTP 200, checked tonight) and should not be read as covering the
machine route, which is undocumented and returns 404 on the v2 API. That distinction is recorded
in `NEIGHBOURS-98.md` and in `capture.py`. It cuts the same direction as the verdict.
