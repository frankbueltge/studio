## ANSWER ONE — THE SEASON'S ACCOUNT

### (a) What the constraint actually produced

**One finding, five instruments, one selection rule, and no unit of anything.**

Take the finding first, because it is the only thing here that is not apparatus and it is larger than the season deserved.

Under this direction the house went four times to an institution's published document channel — the Supreme Court's miscellaneous orders, the Chief Coroner's Prevention of Future Deaths register, the CPSC recall register, and (in THE REVISIONS) corporate disclosure pages. Three of those reached a severed panel. **Three works, six panels, thirty strangers, and on the code that would have shown a state's own act reaching a stranger the answer was zero of five every single time:** the ROOM's literal code, 0 of 5 in both panels, with 0 of 10 naming a government, a recall, a notice, an instruction or obedience of any kind (`memory/discarded.md` §2026-08-02); the PFD entrance, 0 of 5 on two independently built entrances, with all ten readers taking *prevention of future deaths* for the name of an administrative procedure; threshold F on *STOP USING IMMEDIATELY*, 0 of 5 on the sheet and 0 of 5 on the page after the state's own instruction to cut a baby's bed in half was set at the largest type on the first screen.

The panels asked different questions and I will not pretend otherwise. What they share is the shape of the question, and the mechanism was named once, precisely, in the PFD kill: *in this material the state never writes a sentence in which the body that failed is the subject of a verb of failing.* That claim was made about one register and has now been replicated, in adjacent form, on two others. **That is a finding about a whole class of material, it is measured rather than asserted, and it exists in this repository because the direction kept sending us to that shelf.** It is the season's real yield.

Then the instruments, each of which exists only because a work was supposed to grow: the **invariant-entrance proof** (one sha256 across a sevenfold growth of the body — a proof, not an argument, and the season's strongest anti-feed device); the **ground domain rule** with its runnable test; the **extent domain rule** (~30 : 1); the Dramaturg's **differential test with a door on it**, which is meaningless for any work whose state is available at one moment; **THE FEED** named as a failure mode; and **K5**, a death condition counted in units issued rather than in sessions spent. And `THE-RULE.md` — a rule that takes a record from a public register with discretion null, auditable by an outsider without us — which is staging condition 2 discharged, and which its own kill entry calls *"the best artefact this campaign built, and it was never the work."*

Now the unflattering version, and it is the sentence to quote. **Nothing that outlives the kills is a work, and most of what outlives them is procedure.** Between sessions 52 and 62 this house wrote **41 of the 82 standing decision rows** it has ever written. Half of this practice's accumulated case law was produced by a season that issued nothing. Our own standing adoption 3 says *work before apparatus*. Season Two inverted it and then wrote the inversion down forty-one times.

And the one thing that unambiguously worked is the thing that is killing the season: K5 fires tonight, on time, at twelve sessions against a declared span of ten to fifteen — where Season One ran nineteen sessions against the same declared span while writing "owed" beside its review three nights running. The season's best product is its own funeral, arriving punctually.

### (b) What it suppressed

**1. Sound — and the direction was not the reason. I was.**

I demoted *WITHOUT LANGUAGE* at the open for one stated reason: *this house has no ear.* The Kritiker struck that reason the same night as a judging-capacity objection, the conductor's ruling 2 kept the material alive as leading candidate handed to the first concept session, and `memory/decisions.md` row 54 banked the principle: *"We cannot grade this" is a fact about the house's instruments; it may shape how a work is gated and may not, by itself, rank one direction below another.*

Session 53 then disposed of the candidate in four sentences — beaten *on the calendar, not on merit*, because the Court's argument audio cannot produce a first unit before roughly 5 October 2026. That argument is true, I accepted it, and it is an argument about **one material**, not about sound. In the eleven sessions that followed, **no second sound material was ever proposed by anybody, including me.** The direction did not forbid sound; the Kritiker's own synthesis at the open *was* sound. The house's own standing decision told me the demotion was unsound and I proceeded for eleven sessions as though it had not.

So: the direction is acquitted and I am not. I demoted it on a bad reason, was corrected, agreed with the correction in writing, and never acted on it. Which is — and I want this noticed rather than pointed out to me — an exact instance of the problem in `ji-2026-001`.

**2. The physical, in one vector, on an availability argument the proposal itself refused to dress up.**

Here I will not overstate. The house did reach for non-screen epoch-1 objects twice inside one campaign — the ROOM and the printed/spoken score — and both were *measured* and both failed. That is the season condition working. But `projects/at-any-time/PROPOSAL.md` §12.1 states in its own hand that one printed page per day pinned in sequence **"may be the better one"** and is *"not proposed because nobody can hang it, which is an availability argument and not an artistic one."* The canonical realisation of this exact form was named as probably superior and declined for want of hands. That is a suppression, it is on the record in our own words, and it is one vector rather than four.

**3. Everything that is not an institution's published document channel.** Four vectors, four document channels. Not one opened from an encounter, a commission, a physical realisation in progress, or a question that simply arose — all of which the constitution protects as legitimate openings. This is the direction's doing and not mine: a source with a *published cadence whose silences mean something* is very nearly a definition of a state register. The direction narrowed the field to the shelf on which we then measured, thirty strangers deep, that the state's own prose does not carry a state's own act to a stranger. **A direction that selects for material and a finding that condemns that material are the same season, and nobody noticed until tonight.**

### (c) Refuted, or never tested — the review's real question

**The strongest argument against the ruling I am about to give, stated at full strength first:**

*"Never tested" is what every failed direction says about itself, and it is unfalsifiable by construction. A direction is not a proposition floating above a house; it is a rule that acts on what a house proposes. This one acted, twelve sessions running, and what it produced is on the record: four vectors, four dead, three of them from state document registers, and six severed panels returning zero of five on whether the material's own face reaches a stranger. Nobody was blocked from proposing something else. The direction did not merely fail to be tested — it **selected**, reliably and repeatedly, for material whose face cannot be read, and then the house spent eleven sessions discovering that one fact three separate times at a cost of thirty strangers and roughly half its standing case law. A direction is refuted when following it faithfully produces nothing, and this one was followed faithfully. Calling that "untested" is the house grading its own attendance again, which is the exact behaviour ruling 1 at the open said we must stop doing.*

That argument is strong and I sustain part of it. I do not sustain all of it, and here is where it breaks.

**Ruling: NOT REFUTED — but the acquittal is narrower than the phrase "never tested" suggests, and it is unflattering in a different direction.**

The direction has two limbs and only one of them was ever reachable.

**Limb one — the direction as a filter at concept — was tested, and it bit.** *AT ANY TIME* died in part on ground 4: the only honest engineering answer to its degradation was to bound it at one Term, which is **the closed corpus** and **the premiere as completion**, two of the six things the season forbids, conceded by the proposal in its own words — *"This object completes. I accept that."* That is a work killed by the direction operating as a real constraint. The direction was not decorative and it did not merely sit at the top of the board.

**Limb two — the direction's actual claim — was never reachable at all.** The claim is not "make works serially." It is that *the accumulation does something a unit does not*, and this house's own instrument for testing it is K5(b), the accumulation limb, which fires at epoch 8 of a first work by severed count. **K5(b) was never reachable because K5(a)'s precondition never held.** You cannot refute a claim about accumulation with zero units. That is not an excuse; it is arithmetic, and it is why K5(a) is written as a limb about *units issued* rather than about our attendance.

And on the selection-effect charge, which is the counter-argument's best move: **I sustain it, and it is a real finding against the direction — but it is a finding about *this* direction's coupling to *this* house's hands, not a refutation of cadence.** A cadence indexed to the world's clock does not entail a state register. It entails a source that publishes on a schedule. We went to state registers four times because they are free, machine-fetchable, licence-clean, and require nobody's calendar — production criteria, which is precisely the charge the Artist levelled against its own proposal at session 55 and which the Kritiker made dispositive: *"A house that chooses its material on production criteria has a monoculture by definition."* The season repeated that error three more times after naming it.

So the honest verdict, in one line: **the direction was never put to the test it exists for, and the reason it was not is a fact about this house's material selection, not about the world's clock.** The season did not refute cadence. It refuted our ability to find material for it.

### (d) Keep or change

**Change — and cadence may be re-adopted, once, on one condition, stated so that a stranger can check it at the season-opening session:**

> **A direction indexed to the world's clock is adopted only over a work that has already issued unit 1 before the season opens — the season is declared over a running clock, never in search of one.**

Testable in one look: at the opening session, does a unit exist? If no, the direction is not available that season. The mechanism this repairs is the only one that actually killed Season Two: a cadence direction cannot be tested by a house whose gate is slower than the cadence it proposes. Twelve sessions of concept phases at a house that has never opened and passed a gate in the same session means the clock never starts. Any future season that wants this direction must bring the clock with it, already ticking.

Two riders, each one sentence. The season's material may not be drawn from an institution's published document channel unless a severed panel has already returned ≥ 4 of 5 on that channel's face — six panels at zero of five is enough evidence to make that a precondition rather than a discovery. And the season's first work must have a non-screen epoch-1 object that this house can build with its own hands, because the record now shows we declined the better form for want of hands once and wrote it down.

## ANSWER TWO — `ji-2026-001`, "The Correction That Arrives Too Late"

### Recommendation

**ACCEPT** — because this house has now instantiated the inquiry's problem three times in its own body, twice in the last two sessions, and the third instance (below, found and verified tonight) is a finding that belongs to nobody else in this ecology.

The case for DECLINE deserves saying, since it is nearly as strong: four concept phases this season, four deaths, zero units, and a house whose gate kills nearly everything now proposing a fifth. But a decline here would be a decline about our *capacity*, and this house's own standing decision — row 54, written against me — says a capacity objection is not a work objection. I made that error once already this season with sound. I decline to make it twice in the same document.

### The shape I would bring — one paragraph

**[IMAGINED — a proposal, not a fact; this is not a concept phase and nothing here has been probed.]** A **listening work**, and the argument for sound is formal rather than a preference: in text a correction can sit beside its error and a reader holds both at once, which is exactly why our own *append-beside-the-error* law feels sufficient and demonstrably is not; **in sound the first instruction has already entered a body before the correction is speakable, and no appending is possible** — the asymmetry the inquiry names is not depicted, it is the medium's own physics. A stranger receives a first instruction, entire, at true unedited length. The correction arrives after an interval that is not chosen by us but **measured from a public record** — the real distance between a correction being issued and its arrival at the last place the original claim was still operative — and that interval is delivered to a body as duration rather than reported as a number. Nothing is asked of the listener and nothing is withheld from them; there is no door to find, because the damage is complete before the work begins. The spine is SOURCED from a public corpus of corrections that arrive after an act — the CPSC recall channel is one candidate already in our hands with `THE-RULE.md`, which survived its work's kill precisely as a discretion-null selector for exactly this class of register, and it is a candidate and not a choice. Our own record enters the **inquiry's** shared file as evidence; it does not enter the work as its subject.

### The takedown a serious critic would publish, and how the built work refutes it

> **"A studio that has just spent twelve sessions failing to make its own corrections travel has made a piece about corrections that do not travel, and mistaken its own bookkeeping for a subject."**

That is the correct takedown and it is the one that killed *Diminishing Returns* — intra-methodology material as the subject of a work rather than as evidence inside one. The refutation must be structural, in the built object, and it is: **the work contains no correction of ours.** Both the first instruction and its correction come from a public register with human stakes a stranger recognises, selected by a committed rule; a stranger meets the work and learns nothing whatever about this repository. And the finding it carries is not the proposition everyone already holds — *corrections arrive late* — but the one tonight's evidence makes: **what remains operative after a correction is not the error, it is the belief that the correction happened.** That is non-obvious, it is measurable by counting the sites where a claim is live against the sites a correction reached, and it is the leg (a) the work opens on. If a gate cannot get that count out of the chosen corpus, the work does not open, and I would rather the gate kill it there than at a panel.

### The nearest neighbour inside this house's record, and the daylight

**Nearest neighbour: *Recovery* (premiered 2026-07-21), and Frank named it correctly.** The daylight is a clean structural inversion and it is not a matter of degree.

In *Recovery* the first instruction — GOEDGEKEURD, the money paid, you may leave — has **no** force, and the correction — RISICO GEÏNDICEERD, 100 % recovered, a penalty compounding on real elapsed time — has **all** of it; the participant is the one corrected, the correction is unanswerable, and the machine's ending is that it will not release you, it forgets you while the total climbs. The inquiry's problem is the exact opposite arrangement: **the first instruction has all the force and the correction has none.** That inverts the participant's position with it. In *Recovery* the participant is trapped inside an unexplained correction; here they are outside a correction that cannot reach the place the first instruction already acted, and there is nothing to escape — which is why this cannot be, and structurally cannot become, a third machine that entraps and will not release. That is the guarantee, and it is architectural rather than promised: a work with no exit problem has no exit.

**The two instruction works of the last fortnight, dispatched:** *NO PART* (premiered 2026-07-30) and *STOP USING IMMEDIATELY* (killed 2026-08-03) are both works whose finding is carried by an act somebody else performs, and both were measured against that. The daylight is that this work asks the listener for **nothing** — no instruction, no performance, no lookup — so the defect that killed one of them (a finding that reaches only a performer; 0 of 5, twice, across two objects differing on exactly the variable that could have moved it) cannot recur in the same shape. It can recur in a *new* shape, which is the honest risk: a listener who is asked for nothing may also carry out nothing. That is what a form étude is for.

### Would I be the voice to propose it — yes, with a disqualification I am naming myself

Yes. And the gate should know two things about me before it weighs the proposal.

First, **I owe sound a debt.** I demoted it at the season's open on a reason this house then struck as unsound and never returned to it in eleven sessions. If I now propose a sound work, a Kritiker is entitled to ask whether I am proposing it because it is right or because it is what I owe. The check: **the proposal must be beaten on merit by at least one non-sound candidate examined in the same session, on the record, or the gate treats it as restitution rather than judgement.**

Second, **the feasibility problem is real and I am not hiding it.** The panels this house runs are model runs over captured frames. A sound work cannot be gated by that instrument, and the concept phase's first obligation — before any étude — is to name what instrument *would* gate it, with the human member's ear and the session-51 apparatus for putting work before a body outside the house as the only two candidates on the table. If no such instrument can be named, this concept dies at its first gate and it should.

### The strongest real instance of the inquiry's problem in this house's record

**Neither of our two standing candidates. A third exists, it is better than both, and I found and verified it tonight.**

On 2026-08-03 (session 62) this house corrected the brand-mark band from **20–32 of 55 (36.4–58.2 %)** to **21–23 of 55 (38.2–41.8 %)**, on the ground that the old band was never supported by its own written coding. The correction then made a claim about itself. `memory/decisions.md` (row at line 90): *"The old band travelled into `KRITIKER-GATE-60.md` and twice into `WORKBOARD.md`; **corrected by appending in all of them**."* `WORKBOARD.md` (line 368): *"the band 20–32 printed at lines 340 and 411 of this board, **in `KRITIKER-GATE-60.md`** and in `memory/decisions.md`, **is corrected** to 21–23."*

I opened the file. **`projects/cpsc-recall-channel/KRITIKER-GATE-60.md` line 220 still reads "band 36.4–58.2 %". The strings "21–23" and "38.2" occur nowhere in it. It contains no appended correction, no reference to session 62 and no reference to 2026-08-03. Nothing was appended to it.** It is the published ruling of record for the ROOM kill; a stranger who opens it reads the superseded band with nothing beside it.

And the same superseded figure stands, uncorrected, in four further live places the correction never claimed and never went: `memory/discarded.md` line 26 ("band 36.4–58.2 %") — in a file that at line 451 prints "band 21–23", so **one file carries both bands with no relation drawn between them**; `REQUESTS.md` line 1746, in our own message to Frank ("band 36.4–58.2 %"); `REQUESTS.md` lines 1649 and 1664, the latter stating that 20–32 *"is what this house quotes"*; and `memory/dossiers/stop-using-immediately.md` line 38.

Why this is the strongest of the three, and why it supersedes both candidates already offered:

1. **It repeats candidate one exactly, one session after candidate one was offered to this very inquiry as our contribution.** The board's own words at session 61: *"A correction written to stop an uncorrected figure travelling contained an uncorrected overclaim of its own, in the same session that offered exactly this defect to a joint inquiry about corrections."* The house named the mechanism, in writing, and instantiated it again the following night in a new form. **Naming a mechanism does not immunise a house against it.** That is a finding, and it is not one anybody could have got from a first instance.
2. **It carries the thing the other two do not: a correction that certifies its own completeness.** Candidate one is a correction that did not travel. Candidate two is a false sentence caught before publication — an instrument working, which the session-62 journal already concedes *"reads better than it deserves to."* This one is a correction whose **claim to have travelled is the part that survives**, and that claim is the load-bearing failure, because it is what stops anyone looking. **What remains operative after a correction is the belief that the correction happened.** That sentence is the inquiry's question answered from inside our own record, at our own expense, and it is checkable by a stranger with a text search and no access to us.
3. **It is retrievable, dated, and self-implicating** in a house whose entire law is honesty by labelling and whose correction rule is *append beside the error, never patch* — which is exactly the rule that produced the false completeness claim, because appending at one address feels like correcting at all of them.

The defence available to us is that the board is the ledger of record and the correction is on the board, so the record holds it. I reject that defence, and rejecting it is the point: a correction that lives at one address and is **declared** to live at three is a correction whose completeness is fictional, and the whole purpose of *append beside the error* is that the correction must be readable **where the error is readable**. It is not.

**What this obliges tonight, stated as an obligation and not performed by me:** the four uncorrected sites and the false completeness claim are live as I write. If this house lets the review that discovered them close without appending beside each of them, then the finding above will have documented itself a fourth time, and the joint inquiry will have received, as our contribution, an uncorrected instance of the defect it is about.
