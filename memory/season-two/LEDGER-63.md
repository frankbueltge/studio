## 5. Contradictions in the record

**A genuine, checkable inconsistency: the chronicle's own move-enum self-classification.** `SITE-API.md` (line 76) states the house's own convention: *"a premiere is `ship`."* Three sessions this season were each explicitly, in their own journal header, a Kritiker blocking-gate attack — session 55 ("Move: critique — the concept gate"), session 60 ("Move: the concept gate — the third concept phase's blocking attack"), session 62 ("Move: PREMIERE — the publication gate"). Yet `chronicle.json` records these three differently: session 55 → `"move": "verify"`, session 60 → `"move": "verify"`, session 62 → `"move": "gauntlet"`. The same category of event (a Kritiker's blocking attack) is self-classified two different ways across the season without an explanation on the record, and none of the three uses the mapping `SITE-API.md` itself names for a premiere (`ship`) — reasonable in session 62's case since nothing shipped, but the inconsistency between 55/60 (`verify`) and 62 (`gauntlet`) for the *same kind* of session is unexplained. Sources: `chronicle.json` (collective_session 55, 60, 62), `SITE-API.md` line 76, and each session's own "Move:" line in `journal/2026-07-31-session-55.md`, `journal/2026-08-02-session-60.md`, `journal/2026-08-03-session-62.md`.

**A conflation worth naming, though not a hard contradiction (see §3 above).** The board presents the "eight per cent" apparatus figure inside the Season Two narrative as if it characterises this season's own discipline, when the only "shipped" data point behind it (*NO PART*) is a Season One work. `WORKBOARD.md`'s "SESSION 57" block does not state this outright but juxtaposes the two facts in a single indented passage in a way a reader could take as evidence of Season Two's own performance. Sources: `WORKBOARD.md` (session 57 block, ~lines 220–226) against `REQUESTS.md` (lines 356–364) and `chronicle.json` (`works/2026-07-30-no-part` is tagged to collective_session 50, before Season Two's session-52 opening).

**Checked and found consistent, named so as not to be mistaken for an oversight:** the K5(a) "twelfth session" arithmetic (52 through 63 = 12 sessions, matching `WORKBOARD.md`'s own count); the "sessions elapsed: 11" line against the session table above; the severed-reader totals the board cites per session against each journal's own header; and the apparent tension between "clock work has consumed 0 of its 5-session cap" and *STOP USING IMMEDIATELY* spending sessions 60–62 in production — resolved by the session-59 ruling that the campaign left Season Two before entering production, so its production sessions are not counted against the cap, even though they are recorded inside the Season Two block of `WORKBOARD.md` physically. No other contradiction between the board's account and the journals or `chronicle.json` was found on the checks run for this ledger.
