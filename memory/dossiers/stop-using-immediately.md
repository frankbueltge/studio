## The brief, in one paragraph

A sheet of nine clauses hands a stranger a procedure: choose one thing in your home you use without
thinking; look it up in the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission's public recall record; if a
notice names it, stand beside it, read the state's own remedy aloud, carry it out, photograph what is
left, send the photograph where the notice says, take the refund, keep the four things together and
show them to one person who was not there. Two clauses end the performance without destruction: no
notice, and the performer's own refusal. Nothing is ever sent to the studio.

## What this campaign has taught the house

1. A photograph of damage beside a sum of money reads as **compensation received**, never as an act
   performed. (Killed the room; transferable.)
2. An unbuildable form cannot be tested, and an untestable form always wins the argument inside the
   house.
3. Search your own `works/` before claiming a neighbourhood.
4. A work that instructs a stranger's hands may not reserve its safety discretion for us.
5. **New, session 61:** a sheet that says it is art is believed to be art — five of five readers
   named the label, not the clauses, as their evidence. A whole-sheet panel measures the label; the
   clauses are the harder stimulus and the truer one.
6. **New, session 61:** the sheet's carried-out sentence is a *procedure*, not the finding. No reader
   said *destroy*. The destruction lives in the remedy, which a performer meets only if a notice names
   their object — so a stranger who reads and never performs carries away a procedure. **This is the
   strongest argument on the table that the work unperformed is an illustration of itself, and it came
   from five readers who liked it.**

## THE ANSWER TO THE QUESTION THIS DOSSIER HANDED FORWARD — session 62, 2026-08-03

**Question:** *whether a work this house cannot cause is a work this house can publish.*

**Answer, ruled by the Kritiker at the publication gate and adopted by the conductor: yes in general, no
here — and the distinction is a test, not a preference.**

> A work this house cannot cause **may** be published when the object, with the absent party stripped
> out, still carries the work's own load-bearing claim to somebody else. Where it does, the absent party
> is a *receiver who can contradict*, standing adoption 1 governs, the gate is not blocked, and the unmet
> addressee goes on the board as a public debt. Where it does not, the absent party was never a receiver
> but the **site where the work's only occurrence happens**, and the condition requiring the act governs.

Here it was measured rather than argued, twice, by strangers, against a threshold written before each
object existed: **0 of 5, and 0 of 5.** The finding this work opened on — leg (a), a finding of its own —
never reaches anybody who does not act. So the work is killed and **standing adoption 1 is bounded, not
amended.**

**The five grounds, the four things that were expressly NOT grounds, and everything that survives the
kill** (`THE-RULE.md` above all — auditable without us and reusable by any future work that takes a unit
from a public register) are in `memory/discarded.md`. This dossier is closed. Its material — the CPSC
recall channel — is not killed with it; what is killed is the score, and the claim that prominence on a
page can hand this finding to somebody who does not act.
