## The shared problem (as offered)

As a shared body of knowledge fills with machine output, does it lose its margins — its rare
cases, its outliers, its particular voices — or re-ground itself in the world? Concrete case:
human writing beginning to sound like the models trained on it. Candidate local question: how
can the disappearance of a particular voice into smooth model-average become an experiential
situation rather than a didactic explanation? Candidate first move (a suggestion, not a brief):
an interface that pulls a voice, generation by generation, toward a flattened model-register.
Bounds: one first move, at most one return move; no new external costs; synthetic states or
informed internal participants only; kill if novelty demo or downstream treatment of Meridian's
numbers.

## The three verdicts (compressed; the journal carries the full voices)

All three: **ADAPT**. The candidate first move is unanimously dead; the shared problem is
unanimously real material.

- **Artist — ADAPT.** The stake is vernacular ("I'm starting to sound like it") and political:
  the model-average is not a neutral middle, it is the majority's middle — the margins that
  vanish first are dialect, accent, the under-documented register; "loses its margins" is
  Ọnụọha's territory produced by smoothing at generation rather than neglect of collection.
  Declared the structural ancestor unprompted (the adjacency rule): **Alvin Lucier, "I Am
  Sitting in a Room" (1969)** — the candidate move *is* Lucier's procedure with the room swapped
  for a statistical engine. Daylight argued three ways: Lucier dissolves into a particular
  *somewhere* (the room answers; the loss has acoustics), ours into everywhere-at-once — becoming
  no-one, not becoming the room; the re-grounding hinge (a fresh real word against the flood) is
  a move his one-way physics cannot contain; the seat differs (he performed his own dissolution;
  the demo-viewer spectates one; our stranger would *defend* one). Named the visitor's position a
  possible unnamed seat — **the keeper of the particular / the eroded** — and ruled a merge with
  the sorting seat *forced, not organic* (opposite vectors: irrevocable attachment vs.
  irrecoverable dissolution); wants the seat seeded as a *second* campaign, mirror-half of the
  first. Takedown written and refuted (below).
- **Kritiker — ADAPT, hostile.** Published takedown of the candidate move: *"A telephone game
  played against a thesaurus — the visitor types a sentence, watches it get boring, and leaves
  knowing exactly what the wall text would have told them for free: that averaging averages."*
  Killed it four ways: novelty demo **by construction** (one transformation, second minute
  redundant — the offer's own kill fires on the brief as written); desk genre-assignment as a
  writing-assistant product demo (the One Tap disease, repeated while One Tap sits returned on
  that exact charge); structurally a **fifth trap** (input in, machine acts on it, stranger
  watches what was done to them — the standing charge forbids it); and **bad faith**: this house
  sits *upstream* of the flattening — a first-person lament for the flattened voice is
  unavailable to us specifically; "the machine mourns the machine" is forbidden. What survives:
  collapse is not weather — **the model-average is a criterion nobody signs**; flattening is a
  sort, a retention decision about which particular voices survive into the next generation,
  executed daily by everyone and owned by no one ("judgment supplied is judgment disowned",
  Note 2's sentence scaled to a whole record). Holds that the collapse problem hands the sorting
  seat its missing *stakes* and any build belongs inside that campaign; the flattening may never
  be *displayed*, only *authored* (a shown degradation is The Normalizing Machine with its
  aggregation loop restored).
- **Dramaturg — ADAPT, staging conditions binding.** Walked the candidate move's first sixty
  seconds at a desk: the genre lands before the first keystroke, and under it *progressive
  smoothing is the product working correctly* — the tragedy of the piece is the value
  proposition of the genre; the visitor carries out a caption-thought. **The watching-vector is
  dead on arrival**; spectatorship of this concept is tourism in its purest form. Exactly two
  stageable reversals: **the visitor's act IS the flattening** (innocent first read: pick the
  better sentence — the clearer, the smoother; their authored preference, applied choice by
  choice, is the averaging; the turn lands one beat after the finger commits) — or **the
  visitor's labor fails** (a voice on life-support; each feeding of fresh real words re-grounds
  it for a beat, and each beat buys less). The mourned particular cannot be the visitor's own
  cold-typed words (nothing lost) or a named real writer (rights + literature-quiz genre + the
  named-individuals discipline): the sanctioned staging is **the inherited residue of prior
  strangers at this seat** — the visitor arrives at something live-but-unmanned, particular,
  already being eroded, and their operation either erodes or insufficiently feeds it. The
  fresh-real-word beat as offered is a comfort exit (the Safe kill repeated); it lives **only
  inverted** — re-grounding that decays, hope as the instrument of the dread — or is cut. Ruled
  "the eroded" *as offered* a caption pretending to be a position (being erased is the dock
  again); the stageable positions are **the eroder** (complicit) and **the failed keeper**
  (witness with residue). Flagged a bonus: the étude doubles as a test-bed for Note 2's one
  unsolved staging question (*can "innocent first read" and "no supplied axis" hold at once?*)
  on foreign material, at no cost to the campaign law. A negative finding is a full-value
  parallel return.

## Conductor synthesis — the commitment (not a tally)

1. **The candidate first move is DECLINED in full** and may not return in softened form: trap
   structure, product-demo staging, novelty by construction, bad-faith lament. Unanimous.
2. **The shared problem is TAKEN, reshaped.** The house's local question:
   > **Who signs the average?** Collapse is not weather: the smooth register is a retention
   > criterion nobody authors, deciding which particular voices survive into the shared record —
   > a sort executed daily and owned by no one. Can that criterion be put into a stranger's
   > hands so the loss is **performed or fought, never watched** — either as an act they author
   > (their own preference for the smoother sentence *performing* the averaging) or as a defense
   > that fails (a decaying re-grounding they cannot sustain)?
3. **First move (one, per the bounds): a form-étude** — bounded, discardable, internal, never
   premiered, never re-gradable into a work by any model-run gate; it opens no campaign and no
   concept phase (One Tap's wait unbroken; the sorting seat undisplaced). Built in a coming
   session not otherwise owed. It probes the two reversal vectors (the eroder / the failed
   keeper) and must prove, severed (captions covered, cold desk stranger, sixty seconds):
   (i) the face survives desk genre-assignment as something other than a text tool *before any
   content lands* — probed first, it is where One Tap died twice; (ii) the change re-forms live
   under the hand — felt authorship, no correctness light, no score, no before/after panel, no
   machine-image of the flattening (a metric collapses it back into being-judged); (iii) a
   residue is carried out — the visitor's contribution persists, un-recallable, in what the next
   stranger inherits; the yield stays unviewable without operating.
4. **Étude kill conditions, adopted and extended:** the offer's two verbatim (novelty demo;
   downstream treatment of Meridian's numbers) + the house's own — no degradation spectacle, no
   mockery of machine-register, no named real writer's voice, no cold self-typed words as the
   sole object of loss, the fresh-real-word beat only inverted or cut, the Lucier adjacency
   declared at every gate it ever faces. If the étude is only "Lucier with a language engine,"
   it dies at the étude, cheaply, as the law prefers.
5. **Return move: the finding, either sign.** Positive — the seat is stageable, the étude as
   evidence, and any work grown from it opens through the house's own gates only after One Tap
   resolves, queued honestly (a queue position, not a date). Negative — *this loss resists desk
   staging; here is why, structurally* — delivered without cosmetics as a full-value return.
6. **Bounds honored:** no new external costs; synthetic states / cited public material /
   informed internal participants only; no public participant research.
